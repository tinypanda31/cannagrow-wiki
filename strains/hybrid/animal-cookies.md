# Animal Cookies

![Animal Cookies](/images/strains/animal-cookies.jpg)

## Overview

Animal Cookies is a potent hybrid cultivar created by crossing Animal Mints with Girl Scout Cookies. This strain combines the heavy, resinous genetics of its Cookie family lineage with the robust, fuel-forward character of Animal Mints. The result is a high-THC cultivar with a sweet-diesel-earthy aroma profile and effects that begin with strong cerebral euphoria before settling into deep physical relaxation. Animal Cookies has gained a reputation among experienced consumers for its intensity and complex flavor, making it a standout in the modern Cookie-family strain lineage.

## Genetics & Lineage

Animal Cookies is a two-way cross:

- **Animal Mints** -- A potent hybrid known for its heavy resin production, fuel-forward aroma, and dense bud structure. Animal Mints itself descends from the broader Animal/ Cookies family of genetics.
- **Girl Scout Cookies** -- The legendary hybrid (OG Kush x Durban Poison) that has become one of the most influential genetics in modern cannabis breeding, contributing sweetness, potency, and the distinctive Cookie-family terpene profile.

**Parent lineage:** Animal Mints x Girl Scout Cookies

Animal Cookies emerged from the modern U.S. cannabis breeding scene, continuing the trend of crossing potent Cookie-family genetics with other high-resin cultivars. The strain carries forward the genetic legacy of both OG Kush and Durban Poison through its GSC parent, while the Animal Mints side contributes additional fuel character and resin density. Like many modern hybrids, Animal Cookies has been pheno-hunted by various breeders, resulting in selections that may lean slightly more toward the euphoric or relaxing end of the spectrum.

## Effects & Experience

Animal Cookies is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent euphoria** -- A strong, immediate mental euphoria that is cheerful and engaging
- **Cerebral uplift** -- An initial head-focused lift that promotes mood elevation and mental engagement
- **Progressive body relaxation** -- As the initial euphoria settles, a deep physical relaxation spreads through the body
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension
- **Sedation at higher doses** -- Heavy drowsiness and physical heaviness at elevated doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong to very strong. The high THC content and potent effect progression make Animal Cookies best suited to experienced users. Beginners should start with very small doses.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the high THC content, inexperienced users may experience overwhelming effects at standard doses.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, diesel, and earthy with subtle fuel undertones. The aroma is bold and complex, with the sweet-cookie character inherited from GSC blending with the fuel-diesel intensity from the Animal Mints side. The overall profile is pungent and noticeable.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with diesel and fuel notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, with a cookie-like sweetness that transitions into a heavier, fuel-forward finish.

Animal Cookies is moderately challenging to grow. The Cookie-family genetics bring some sensitivity to overfeeding and humidity fluctuations, but the strain is generally resilient when given appropriate care. The moderate flowering time is a practical advantage. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The strain rewards attentive growing with dense, resinous, and highly aromatic buds.
