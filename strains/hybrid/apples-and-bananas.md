# Apples and Bananas

![Apples And Bananas](/images/strains/apples-and-bananas.jpeg)

## Overview

Apples and Bananas is a balanced hybrid created by crossing Critical Orange Punch with Banana OG. This strain takes its name from the distinctive fruity aroma that blends apple-like crispness with ripe banana sweetness, a flavor profile inherited from its carefully selected parent genetics. The Critical Orange Punch parent contributes vibrant citrus and fruity characteristics along with reliable flowering performance, while the Banana OG lineage brings dense tropical fruit flavors and a relaxing body component. The result is a cultivar that delivers balanced euphoric and physical effects, making it suitable for both daytime and evening use depending on dose. Its approachable fruity-sweet aroma and tropical character make it one of the more dessert-forward strains available, while its moderate growing profile rewards experienced cultivators with dense, resinous flowers.

## Genetics & Lineage

Apples and Bananas is a two-way cross:

- **Critical Orange Punch** -- A hybrid strain known for its bright citrus and fruity character, derived from Critical Mass genetics crossed with an orange-scented selection. Contributes reliable flowering, moderate yields, and vibrant fruity terpene expression.
- **Banana OG** -- An indica-leaning cultivar (Banana Kush x OG Kush) known for dense tropical banana fruit flavor and heavy body relaxation. Contributes the ripe banana sweetness and physical calming genetics.

**Parent lineage:** Critical Orange Punch x Banana OG

Apples and Bananas was bred to combine the bright fruity citrus character of Critical Orange Punch with the dense tropical sweetness and body effects of Banana OG. The resulting cultivar inherits the best of both parents: a vibrant, approachable fruit-forward aroma paired with balanced effects that satisfy both the head and the body. As with many modern hybrids, phenotype selection plays a significant role in the final expression, with some cuts leaning more toward the citrus-orange character and others emphasizing the banana-tropical profile.

## Effects & Experience

Apples and Bananas is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging without being overwhelming
- **Body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm from the Banana OG parentage that accompanies the cerebral effects
- **Mood elevation** -- A happy, positive mood lift driven by the fruity terpene profile and moderate THC content
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental euphoria and body calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Reported to increase appetite, consistent with its dessert-like character

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 17-22% THC range delivers effects that are noticeable and satisfying without being overwhelming for most users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fruity, sweet, and tropical with apple and banana notes layered over an earthy-herbal base. The citrus brightness from the Critical Orange Punch parent adds a fresh top note to the deeper tropical banana character from Banana OG.
- **Taste:** Sweet tropical fruit on the inhale, with apple crispness and ripe banana on the mid-palate. The exhale carries earthy and slightly spicy undertones from the caryophyllene content, rounding out the otherwise sweet profile.

Apples and Bananas is a moderately manageable strain that rewards experienced growers with dense, resinous flowers and a vibrant terpene profile. The balanced hybrid structure produces moderate stretch during flowering, requiring some training but remaining manageable. The fruity terpene expression is best developed under stable, warm conditions with careful attention to humidity during the flowering phase. Proper curing is essential to preserve the distinctive tropical fruit aroma.
