# Banana Kush

![Banana Kush](/images/strains/banana-kush.png)

## Overview

Banana Kush is a hybrid cultivar created through a three-way cross involving Ghost OG, Skunk Haze, and Banana genetics. This strain is best known for its distinctive tropical banana aroma and its effect profile that begins with cerebral euphoria before transitioning into full-body relaxation. The combination of OG Kush-derived potency (through the Ghost OG parent) with tropical fruit character and Skunk Haze vigor has made Banana Kush a popular choice among consumers seeking a flavorful, balanced hybrid with clear Kush lineage.

## Genetics & Lineage

Banana Kush is a three-way cross:

- **Ghost OG** -- A phenotype selection of OG Kush (Chemdawg x Hindu Kush x Lemon Thai) known for its slightly sweeter, fruit-forward expression of the classic OG profile while maintaining the Kush potency.
- **Skunk Haze** -- A hybrid combining Skunk #1 (Afghani x Mexican x Colombian genetics) with Haze (Colombian x Mexican x Thai x South Indian), contributing vigor, yield, and a complex terpene background.
- **Banana** -- A fruit-forward cultivar selected for its distinct banana-like aroma, contributing the tropical sweetness that defines the strain.

**Parent lineage:** Ghost OG x Skunk Haze x Banana

Banana Kush emerged from the U.S. hybrid breeding scene, combining the proven potency of OG-family genetics with tropical fruit character. The three-way cross brings together diverse genetic backgrounds: the Kush lineage provides potency and structure, the Skunk Haze contributes vigor and yield potential, and the Banana parent delivers the defining tropical aroma. This genetic diversity contributes to the strain's balanced effect profile and relatively manageable growing characteristics.

## Effects & Experience

Banana Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant cerebral euphoria that lifts mood and promotes a positive mental state
- **Creative engagement** -- Moderate mental clarity and creative thinking from the Haze-influenced genetics
- **Progressive body relaxation** -- As the cerebral effects settle, a comfortable body relaxation develops
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension
- **Sedation at higher doses** -- Drowsiness and deep relaxation at elevated doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The 18-23% THC range delivers meaningful potency while remaining accessible to a broad range of consumers.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Tropical banana, sweet, and earthy with subtle citrus undertones. The banana aroma is the defining characteristic, supported by an earthy Kush base and a sweet fruit top note. The overall profile is approachable and distinctly tropical.
- **Taste:** Sweet banana on the inhale, with earthy Kush, citrus, and subtle spice notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and fruit-forward, making it one of the most tropical-tasting strains available.
