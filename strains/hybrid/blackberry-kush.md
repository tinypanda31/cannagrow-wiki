# Blackberry Kush

![Blackberry Kush](/images/strains/blackberry-kush.png)

## Overview

Blackberry Kush is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing the legendary Afghani landrace indica with Blackberry genetics. This strain brings together the deep, heavy relaxation of classic Afghani genetics with the sweet, fruity berry character of its Blackberry parent. The result is a strain with a rich berry-earthy-sweet aroma and powerfully relaxing body effects that lean heavily toward the indica side of the spectrum. Blackberry Kush is valued for its evening-appropriate calming effects, its straightforward indica growing profile, and its pleasant berry-forward flavor that appeals to users seeking a dessert-like indica experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

Blackberry Kush is a two-way cross:

- **Afghani** -- The Afghan landrace indica, providing deep body relaxation, resinous buds, compact structure, and earthy-spicy foundational genetics
- **Blackberry** -- A fruity hybrid strain (often traced to Afghani x an unknown berry-flavored selection), contributing the characteristic sweet berry aroma and flavor

**Parent lineage:** Afghani x Blackberry

The combination of two Afghani-influenced parents creates an indica-dominant strain with exceptional body relaxation. The Blackberry parent contributes the sweet, fruity berry aroma that distinguishes Blackberry Kush from more traditional earthy-spic indica strains. This pairing creates a strain that delivers the classic indica experience users expect while adding a pleasant fruity dimension that broadens its appeal.

## Effects & Experience

Blackberry Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep body relaxation** -- The defining characteristic; a heavy, comfortable physical calm that spreads from the core outward
- **Sedation at higher doses** -- The indica influence can lead to noticeable sedation, particularly with elevated consumption
- **Mood calmness** -- A quieting of mental activity that accompanies the body relaxation
- **Stress relief** -- The relaxing effect is frequently cited for unwinding after a long day
- **Sleep support** -- Commonly used as an evening strain to support sleep onset

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The indica-dominant effects are primarily body-focused rather than cerebral, with a gentle to moderate intensity.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The sedating body effects may impair coordination and are not suitable for activities requiring alertness.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Berry-forward with earthy and sweet undertones. The Blackberry inheritance gives it a recognizable sweet berry aroma, while the Afghani parent adds an earthy, slightly spicy depth that prevents the sweetness from becoming one-dimensional.
- **Taste:** Sweet berry on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The berry flavor is prominent and authentic, making it one of the more convincing berry-flavored strains available.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy -- straightforward indica growing profile, forgiving for beginners |
| **Flowering Time** | 50-60 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |

Blackberry Kush is one of the easier indica-dominant strains to grow. Its compact structure, manageable height, and good resistance to common cultivation problems make it suitable for beginners. The relatively short flowering time is an advantage for indoor growers looking to cycle harvests quickly.
