# Blue Dream

![Blue Dream](/images/strains/blue-dream.png)

## Overview

Blue Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by crossing Blueberry with Haze. Originating on the U.S. West Coast (most accounts point to California), Blue Dream has become one of the most widely cultivated, sold, and recognized cannabis strains in the world. Its balanced effects -- combining full-body relaxation from its Blueberry parent with cerebral head effects from Haze -- make it one of the most versatile and universally appealing strains available. The berry-sweet aroma inherited from Blueberry, combined with an easy growing profile and high yields, has made Blue Dream a staple in both commercial cultivation and home growing operations.

## Genetics & Lineage

Blue Dream is a two-way cross:

- **Blueberry** -- The legendary indica by DJ Short (Purple Thai x Thai x Afghani), contributing berry sweetness, relaxation, and fast flowering
- **Haze** -- The classic sativa (Colombian x Mexican x Thai x South Indian genetics), contributing cerebral, uplifting effects and sativa structure

**Parent lineage:** Blueberry x Haze

Blue Dream emerged in the California cannabis scene in the 2000s and quickly gained overwhelming popularity. The combination of Blueberry's approachable flavor and Haze's uplifting effects created a strain that appealed to an exceptionally broad audience. Blue Dream has been widely cloned and pheno-hunted, with selections varying from more indica-leaning (heavier, more relaxing) to more sativa-leaning (more cerebral, more energetic). The original Blue Dream genetics are sometimes referred to as "Blue Dream Original" or "DJ Short Blue Dream" to distinguish from later selections.

## Effects & Experience

Blue Dream is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced full-body and head effects** -- The defining characteristic of Blue Dream is its ability to deliver both physical relaxation and cerebral uplift simultaneously
- **Gentle euphoria** -- A pleasant, approachable mood lift that is not overwhelming
- **Creativity and focus** -- Enhanced mental clarity from the Haze parentage
- **Physical relaxation** -- Comfortable body calm from the Blueberry parentage without heavy sedation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. Blue Dream's approachable effects make it one of the most universally accessible strains, suitable for beginners and experienced users alike.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Berry, sweet, and herbal with earthy undertones. The Blueberry inheritance gives it a recognizable sweet berry aroma, while the Haze parent adds an herbal, slightly earthy complexity.
- **Taste:** Sweet berry on the inhale, with herbal and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it one of the most broadly appealing strains by taste.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Pain management** -- The balanced body relaxation is commonly cited for mild to moderate pain relief
- **Stress and anxiety** -- The gentle euphoria is frequently reported for stress reduction
- **Depression and low mood** -- The uplifting effect is used for mood elevation
- **Nausea** -- Commonly used to address nausea while maintaining daytime functionality
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Moderate appetite increase

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Mild dizziness at higher doses
- Drowsiness at higher doses
- Anxiety possible at high doses, though less commonly reported due to the moderate THC range
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day (less sedating than indica strains)

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy -- one of the easiest and most forgiving strains to grow |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 500-600 g/m² indoor; 600+ g/plant outdoor -- among the highest yielding strains |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- moderate sativa stretch; training recommended but not essential |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; performs well in both. Prefers warm, sunny conditions. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. Vigorous and healthy grower. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, topping, and LST. One of the most resilient and high-yielding strains available. |

Blue Dream is one of the easiest strains to grow and is frequently recommended for beginners. Its vigorous growth, high yields, and good resistance to common problems make it a commercial cultivator's favorite. The moderate sativa stretch requires some training but is manageable. The strain's popularity in commercial growing is driven by its combination of easy cultivation, high yield, and consistent market demand. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.

## Awards & Recognition

- Blue Dream has not won major Cannabis Cup awards as a standalone entry, though its overwhelming commercial success speaks to its recognition
- Consistently among the top-selling strains in dispensaries across the United States
- Frequently listed among the most popular and influential strains of the 2000s and 2010s
- Its genetics have been incorporated into numerous award-winning derivative crosses
