# Breath Mint

![Breath Mint](/images/strains/breath-mint.png)

## Overview

Breath Mint is a hybrid strain created by crossing Mints with Gelato Breath, combining two of the most influential mint-forward genetic families in modern cannabis breeding. This cross captures the sharp, minty fuel character of the Mints lineage and the sweet, dessert-like quality of Gelato Breath, producing a strain with a distinctive mint, sweet, and fuel aroma profile. The effects are balanced between cerebral euphoria and physical body relaxation, making it versatile for both daytime and evening use. Breath Mint inherits the high-potency genetics and complex terpene expression that have made both parent families highly sought after in the modern cannabis market.

## Genetics & Lineage

Breath Mint is a direct cross between two influential modern genetics:

- **Mints** -- A hybrid strain from the Seed Junky genetics program (Marker x Animal Mints lineage), known for its sharp mint and fuel aroma with balanced effects. Mints carries the OG Kush-derived fuel character combined with a distinctive minty terpene expression that has become highly influential in modern breeding.
- **Gelato Breath** -- A hybrid cross involving the Gelato family (Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint GSC) and Breath genetics, contributing sweet, dessert-like character with mint undertones. Gelato Breath inherits the Cookie Family terpene complexity and potency.

**Parent lineage:** Mints x Gelato Breath

Breath Mint was developed to combine the best characteristics of both parent families: the sharp, minty fuel punch of Mints and the sweet, indulgent quality of Gelato Breath. The resulting strain expresses a unique mint-sweet-fuel aromatic profile that bridges the gap between the fuel-forward Mints genetics and the dessert-forward Gelato lineage. This cross represents the ongoing trend of crossing elite modern genetics to create novel cultivars with complex, multi-layered profiles.

## Effects & Experience

Breath Mint is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging without being overwhelming, suitable for social or solo use
- **Body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm that accompanies the cerebral euphoria, creating a well-rounded hybrid experience
- **Mood elevation** -- A bright, positive mood lift that promotes sociability and optimism
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental euphoria and body calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Functional relaxation** -- Effects that are relaxing enough for comfort while remaining functional enough for light activities

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The THC range delivers a meaningful experience that is balanced and approachable, making it suitable for a wide range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Mint and sweet with fuel undertones. The mint character is sharp and cooling, immediately recognizable and distinctive. The sweet Gelato Breath contribution adds a dessert-like quality, while the fuel character from both parent families provides depth and pungency. The overall aroma is layered and complex.
- **Taste:** Mint and sweet on the inhale, with fuel, slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The mint flavor is clean and refreshing, complemented by the sweet dessert character and a fuel finish that appeals to users who enjoy complex, multi-layered profiles.
