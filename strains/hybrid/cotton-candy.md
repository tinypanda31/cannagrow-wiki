# Cotton Candy

![Cotton Candy photo](/uploads/images/strains/cotton-candy.jpg)

## Overview

Cotton Candy is a hybrid strain created by crossing Power Plant with Purple Skunk. The name is a direct reflection of its sweet, candy-like aroma and flavor profile, which evokes the spun-sugar sweetness of fairground cotton candy. This balanced hybrid delivers a combination of euphoric cerebral effects and gentle body relaxation, making it suitable for a wide range of users and consumption occasions. The Power Plant parent contributes vigor and energy to the growing profile, while the Purple Skunk inheritance brings sweet, fruity flavor genetics and moderate indica structure. Cotton Candy is valued for its universally pleasant flavor, approachable effects, and straightforward cultivation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Cotton Candy is a two-way cross:

- **Power Plant** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid (South African sativa genetics) known for vigorous growth, high yields, and energetic effects
- **Purple Skunk** -- A hybrid combining purple genetics with Skunk, contributing sweet, fruity flavor, purple coloration potential, and moderate indica structure

**Parent lineage:** Power Plant x Purple Skunk

The combination of Power Plant's vigor and Purple Skunk's sweet flavor genetics creates a balanced hybrid that performs well in cultivation while delivering an enjoyable candy-like experience. The Power Plant parent brings energy and yield potential, while the Purple Skunk contributes the distinctive sweet, fruity character that defines the Cotton Candy profile.

Cotton Candy is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A pleasant, cheerful mental lift that is noticeable without being overwhelming

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, candy-like, and fruity with earthy and citrus undertones. The aroma is reminiscent of spun sugar and fruit, with the Purple Skunk inheritance giving it a pronounced sweetness that is rounded out by the myrcene earthy base from the Power Plant parent.
- **Taste:** Sweet candy and fruity on the inhale, with earthy and slightly citrus notes on the exhale. The candy-like flavor is prominent and authentic, making it one of the more convincing sweet-flavored strains.
