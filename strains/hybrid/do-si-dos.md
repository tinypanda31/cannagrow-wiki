# Do-Si-Dos

![Do Si Dos](/images/strains/do-si-dos.jpg)

## Overview

Do-Si-Dos is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Girl Scout Cookies with Face Off OG. This strain combines the legendary sweetness and potency of the Cookie family with the heavy, fuel-forward intensity of OG genetics, resulting in a deeply relaxing cultivar with a sweet-earthy-flowery aroma. Do-Si-Dos has gained a reputation for its heavy body effects and potent euphoria, making it a favorite among consumers seeking strong relaxation and stress relief. The strain is part of the broader Cookies and OG family tree that dominates modern cannabis breeding, and its genetics have been incorporated into numerous subsequent hybrid crosses.

## Genetics & Lineage

Do-Si-Dos is a two-way cross:

- **Girl Scout Cookies** -- The legendary hybrid (OG Kush x Durban Poison) that has become one of the most influential genetics in modern cannabis breeding. Contributes sweetness, potency, and the distinctive Cookie-family terpene profile.
- **Face Off OG** -- A specific phenotype of OG Kush (Chemdawg x Hindu Kush x Lemon Thai) selected for its intense fuel aroma, heavy effects, and dense bud structure. Face Off OG is known as one of the more potent and physically relaxing OG phenotype selections.

**Parent lineage:** Girl Scout Cookies x Face Off OG

Do-Si-Dos emerged from the U.S. hybrid breeding scene, continuing the trend of crossing Cookie-family genetics with potent OG selections. The name references the cookie connection through its GSC parent. The combination brings together two of the most influential genetic lines in modern cannabis: the Cookie family (itself derived from OG Kush) and a heavy, potent OG phenotype. This means Do-Si-Dos carries OG Kush genetics on both sides of its lineage, concentrating the Kush character while the GSC side adds sweetness and the Durban Poison sativa influence provides a slight cerebral lift to the otherwise heavy indica profile.

## Effects & Experience

Do-Si-Dos is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A deep, full-body physical relaxation that is the defining characteristic of the strain
- **Potent euphoria** -- A strong mental euphoria that accompanies the body effects
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported significant reduction in mental and physical tension
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that leads toward sleep, particularly at moderate to high doses
- **Pain relief** -- Physical calm and muscle relaxation commonly reported for discomfort

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong to very strong. The high THC content (up to 28%) and heavy indica-leaning effects make Do-Si-Dos best suited to experienced users. Beginners should start with very small doses.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the high THC content and heavy body effects, inexperienced users may experience overwhelming sedation at standard doses.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, earthy, and flowery with fuel undertones. The aroma is rich and complex, with the Cookie-family sweetness blending with the heavy OG fuel character and a distinct flowery top note. The overall profile is deep and pungent.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with flowery, fuel, and subtle citrus notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, with a cookie-like sweetness that transitions into a heavier, fuel-forward finish with flowery complexity.

Do-Si-Dos is moderately challenging to difficult to grow. The Cookie-family and OG genetics bring sensitivity to overfeeding and humidity fluctuations. The dense, resinous bud structure requires attention to airflow and humidity during late flowering. The compact structure is an advantage for indoor growing, and the moderate flowering time is practical. The strain rewards experienced cultivation with exceptionally dense, resinous, and aromatic buds.
