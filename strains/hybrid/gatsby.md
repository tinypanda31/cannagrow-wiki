# Gatsby

![Gatsby photo](/uploads/images/strains/gatsby.jpg)

## Overview

Gatsby is a hybrid strain carrying the influential G13 genetics in its lineage. While the complete parentage remains undocumented, Gatsby combines G13's legendary potency with an unknown complementary genetics to produce a balanced hybrid with sweet-earthy-diesel aroma and well-rounded effects. The strain is named after the literary character Jay Gatsby, and like its namesake, it presents a polished, sophisticated experience. Gatsby delivers a balanced combination of cerebral clarity and physical relaxation, making it suitable for a range of consumption occasions. The diesel undertones in its aroma hint at the potent genetics running through its family tree, while the sweet and earthy primary notes make it broadly palatable.

## Genetics & Lineage

Gatsby carries G13 in its lineage:

- **G13** -- The legendary indica strain (reportedly sourced from U.S. government research programs at Oak Ridge National Laboratory), contributing high potency, deep body relaxation, and resinous production
- **Unknown Genetics** -- The complementary parent has not been publicly documented, but the balanced effects suggest a sativa-influenced genetics

**Parent lineage:** G13 x Unknown

The G13 lineage is one of the most storied in cannabis history. Reportedly collected from government research facilities and distributed to the breeding community, G13 has been incorporated into numerous influential hybrids. Gatsby carries this genetics forward, combining the legendary potency of G13 with complementary genetics to create a balanced hybrid. The unknown parent genetics contribute to the strain's balanced profile, tempering the heavy indica character of G13 with more uplifting elements.

## Effects & Experience

Gatsby is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced cerebral and body effects** -- The defining characteristic; a comfortable combination of mental clarity and physical relaxation
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, approachable mood lift that is noticeable without being overwhelming
- **Relaxation** -- Physical calm that is comfortable and not sedating at moderate doses
- **Stress relief** -- The balanced effect provides stress reduction while maintaining functionality
- **Versatility** -- Suitable for both daytime and evening use depending on dose

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The G13 lineage contributes potency that is noticeable but well-managed by the balanced hybrid genetics.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with diesel undertones. The aroma is layered, with the sweet primary notes sitting over an earthy foundation from the myrcene and caryophyllene. The diesel character is noticeable but not overpowering, adding a pungent complexity that hints at the strain's potent genetics.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with diesel and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and layered, with the diesel character becoming more pronounced on the exhale.
