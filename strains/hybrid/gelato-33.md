# Gelato #33 (Larry Bird)

![Gelato 33](/images/strains/gelato-33.png)

## Overview

Gelato #33, also known as "Larry Bird," is a legendary hybrid strain created by the Sherbinski breeding program, crossing Sunset Sherbet with Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies. As one of the most famous phenotype selections from the original Gelato cross, Gelato #33 has become the defining expression of the Gelato family and one of the most commercially valuable genetics in modern cannabis history. The strain is celebrated for its sweet, fruity, and lavender aroma, balanced euphoric and body effects, and its role as a parent to countless derivative crosses. The Sherbinski creation established Gelato #33 as a cornerstone of modern West Coast cannabis breeding, and its influence continues to shape the direction of contemporary genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Gelato #33 is a specific phenotype selection from the Gelato family, created by the Sherbinski breeding program:

- **Sunset Sherbet** -- A hybrid strain (Pink Panties x unknown Sherbet parent) known for sweet, dessert-like character with fruity and creamy notes. Contributes the dessert-forward terpene profile and balanced hybrid effects.
- **Thin Mint GSC** -- A specific phenotype of Girl Scout Cookies (OG Kush x Durban Poison) selected for its minty, high-THC character. Contributes potency, compact structure, and the GSC genetic influence.

**Parent lineage:** Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies (Gelato #33 "Larry Bird" phenotype selection)

The Gelato cross was developed by the Sherbinski breeding program in the San Francisco Bay Area. When the original Gelato seeds were germinated, multiple numbered phenotype selections emerged, each expressing different characteristics. Gelato #33 -- named "Larry Bird" after the Boston Celtics legend -- was identified as the standout selection, expressing the most balanced and desirable combination of potency, aroma, and effects. The Larry Bird selection became the flagship Gelato phenotype and the basis for the Sherbinski brand. Gelato #33 has since been used as a parent in numerous high-profile crosses and remains one of the most sought-after genetics in the modern cannabis market.

The linalool contribution is particularly notable in the #33 phenotype, giving it a recognizable lavender floral character that distinguishes it from other Gelato selections.

## Effects & Experience

Gelato #33 is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A strong, pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging without being one-directional
- **Body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm that accompanies the cerebral euphoria, creating a well-rounded experience
- **Mood elevation** -- A bright, happy mood lift that promotes positivity and sociability
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental euphoria and body calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Creative engagement** -- Some users report enhanced creative thinking and engagement at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC content delivers potent effects best suited to experienced users, though the balanced character makes the intensity more manageable than one-directional high-THC strains.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, fruity, and lavender with dessert-like and subtle earthy undertones. The aroma is rich and layered, with a noticeable sweetness that resembles dessert or confectionery. The lavender floral character is more pronounced in the #33 phenotype than in other Gelato selections, giving it a distinctive aromatic identity.
- **Taste:** Sweet and fruity on the inhale, with lavender, dessert-like, and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and indulgent, with the lavender-floral character adding a distinctive element to the otherwise sweet-fruity profile.

| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The GSC heritage carries sensitivity to nutrient imbalances and environmental stress. Gelato #33 shares the growing characteristics of the broader Gelato lineage. The compact indica-influenced structure is an advantage for indoor cultivation, but the sensitivity to nutrient imbalances and humidity requires experienced attention. The moderate yields are compensated by the high quality and market value of the flower. The lavender-floral terpene expression is best developed under stable, warm conditions with careful curing to preserve the dessert-forward aroma.
