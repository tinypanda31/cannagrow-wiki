# Gelato #41

![Gelato 41](/images/strains/gelato-41.png)

## Overview

Gelato #41 is a hybrid phenotype selection within the broader Gelato family, created by crossing Sunset Sherbet with Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies. While the original Gelato cross produced numerous numbered phenotype selections (including the famous Gelato #33 "Larry Bird"), Gelato #41 represents a specific cut that emphasizes a particularly balanced expression of euphoric and body effects alongside a sweet-fruity-lavender dessert-like aroma. As part of the Gelato lineage, Gelato #41 inherits the high-THC genetics and influential breeding stock that have made the Cookie Family brand one of the most commercially valuable in modern cannabis. Its balanced effect profile, distinctive dessert-forward terpene expression, and moderate growing profile make it appealing to both consumers and experienced cultivators.

## Genetics & Lineage

Gelato #41 is a phenotype selection within the Gelato family:

- **Sunset Sherbet** -- A hybrid strain (Pink Panties x unknown Sherbet parent) known for sweet, dessert-like character with fruity and creamy notes. Contributes the dessert-forward terpene profile and balanced effects.
- **Thin Mint GSC** -- A specific phenotype of Girl Scout Cookies (OG Kush x Durban Poison) selected for its minty, high-THC character. Contributes potency, compact structure, and the GSC genetic influence.

**Parent lineage:** Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies (Gelato #41 phenotype selection)

Gelato was developed in the San Francisco Bay Area, closely associated with the Cookie Family brand. The original Gelato cross produced multiple numbered phenotype selections, each expressing different characteristics. Gelato #41 is one of these selections, distinguished by its particularly balanced expression of the GSC-derived potency and the Sunset Sherbet-derived dessert terpene profile. Like other Gelato phenotypes, Gelato #41 has become a valuable genetics source for further breeding, and its balanced character makes it one of the more universally appealing selections within the Gelato family.

## Effects & Experience

Gelato #41 is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A strong, pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging without being one-directional
- **Body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm that accompanies the cerebral euphoria, creating a well-rounded experience
- **Mood elevation** -- A bright, happy mood lift that promotes positivity and sociability
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental euphoria and body calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Creative engagement** -- Some users report enhanced creative thinking and engagement at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC content delivers potent effects best suited to experienced users, though the balanced character makes the intensity more manageable than one-directional high-THC strains.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, fruity, and lavender with dessert-like and subtle earthy undertones. The aroma is rich and layered, with a noticeable sweetness that resembles dessert or confectionery. The lavender floral character is more pronounced in the #41 phenotype than in some other Gelato selections.
- **Taste:** Sweet and fruity on the inhale, with lavender, dessert-like, and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and indulgent, with the lavender-floral character adding a distinctive element to the otherwise sweet-fruity profile.

Gelato #41 shares the growing characteristics of the broader Gelato lineage. The compact indica-influenced structure is an advantage for indoor cultivation, but the sensitivity to nutrient imbalances and humidity requires experienced attention. The moderate yields are compensated by the high quality and market value of the flower. The lavender-floral terpene expression is best developed under stable, warm conditions with careful curing to preserve the dessert-forward aroma.
