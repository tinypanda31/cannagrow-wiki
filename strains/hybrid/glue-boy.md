# Glue Boy

![Glue Boy photo](/uploads/images/strains/glue-boy.jpg)

## Overview

Glue Boy is an indica-dominant hybrid representing a specific phenotype selection from the legendary Gorilla Glue #4 (GG4) lineage. This strain captures the heavy, resinous, fuel-forward character that made GG4 one of the most recognizable and commercially successful strains of the 2010s cannabis era. The "Glue Boy" designation refers to a keeper phenotype that emphasizes the deepest body relaxation and the most pungent fuel-earthy-pine expression within the GG4 family. Known for its exceptional resin production (the original GG4 reportedly got its name from the resin that "glued" trimmers' scissors together), Glue Boy inherits the dense, sticky flower structure, powerful body effects, and very high THC content that define the GG4 lineage. Its fuel-forward aroma and heavy physical relaxation make it a favorite among users seeking deep evening relief and cultivators who appreciate heavy resin production.

## Genetics & Lineage

Glue Boy is a phenotype selection from the GG4 lineage:

- **GG4 (Gorilla Glue #4)** -- A legendary hybrid created by crossing Chem's Sister x Sour Dubb x Chocolate Diesel. The cross was developed by the Joesy Whales team (a collaboration between Lonnie and Justin Watson) in the United States. Chem's Sister contributes the pungent fuel character and high potency; Sour Dubb adds earthy-sour depth; Chocolate Diesel brings fuel and chocolate undertones. Together, these three genetics sources produced one of the most resinous and potent strains of the modern era. GG4 won multiple Cannabis Cup awards and became a foundational genetics source for modern breeding.

**Parent lineage:** GG4 (Gorilla Glue #4) phenotype selection

Glue Boy represents a carefully selected phenotype within the GG4 family that emphasizes the indica-dominant, heavy-body relaxation character. The original GG4 cross (Chem's Sister x Sour Dubb x Chocolate Diesel) produced a complex genetic foundation with multiple influential genetics sources. The Glue Boy selection was chosen for its particularly heavy physical effects and the most pronounced fuel-earthy-pine expression within the GG4 lineup.

## Effects & Experience

Glue Boy is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- The defining characteristic; deep physical calm that progresses through the body, ideal for evening and nighttime use
- **Euphoria** -- A noticeable mental uplift that precedes the body relaxation, providing initial cheerfulness
- **Sedation** -- At moderate to higher doses, pronounced drowsiness and sleepiness set in
- **Stress relief** -- The heavy body relaxation is frequently cited for deep stress release
- **Pain relief** -- The intense physical calming is commonly reported for significant pain management

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Very strong. The very high THC content delivers potent, intense effects that are best suited to experienced users. The heavy body relaxation builds progressively and can become overwhelming at higher doses.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the very high THC content and deeply sedating nature of this strain, it is not recommended for beginners or for daytime use.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fuel, earthy, and pine with pungent depth. The caryophyllene-dominant fuel character is the most prominent element, supported by earthy myrcene and fresh pine notes from pinene. The aroma is heavy, pungent, and unmistakably diesel-like.
- **Taste:** Fuel and earthy on the inhale, with pine and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and pungent, carrying the diesel-fuel character that is one of the strain's most defining features.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Heavy drowsiness and sedation, particularly at moderate to higher doses
- Dizziness upon standing
- Couch-lock that may be undesirable for some users
- Anxiety or paranoia at high doses, more likely due to the very high THC content
- Impaired coordination during the experience
- Nausea at very high doses in some users

Glue Boy is a moderately manageable strain that benefits from the robust GG4 genetics. The moderate flowering time of 55-60 days and moderate-to-high yields are attractive characteristics. The heavy resin production is a notable feature but requires careful humidity management during flowering. The strain responds well to training techniques and produces dense, resinous flowers that are highly valued in the market. Support structures may be needed as the dense colas can become heavy.
