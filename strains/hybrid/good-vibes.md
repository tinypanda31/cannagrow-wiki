# Good Vibes

![Good Vibes photo](/uploads/images/strains/good-vibes.jpg)

## Overview

Good Vibes is a sativa-dominant hybrid reportedly created by crossing Mango Haze with unknown genetics. True to its name, Good Vibes is known for delivering an uplifting, positive experience characterized by bright cerebral effects, tropical-citrus aroma, and energetic daytime functionality. The Mango Haze parent contributes mango-tropical flavor genetics and sativa energy, while the unknown complementary genetics appear to enhance the uplifting, mood-elevating character of the strain. Good Vibes is particularly valued by users seeking a strain that promotes positivity and mental brightness without heavy body sedation. Its tropical flavor profile and clean sativa effects make it a popular choice for morning and daytime consumption.

## Genetics & Lineage

Good Vibes reportedly involves Mango Haze:

- **Mango Haze** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid (Mango x Haze x an unknown third parent) known for tropical mango flavor, energetic sativa effects, and Haze-influenced growing characteristics
- **Unknown Genetics** -- The complementary parent has not been publicly documented, but the effects suggest additional sativa-influenced genetics

**Parent lineage:** Mango Haze x Unknown

The Mango Haze lineage brings the tropical mango character and sativa energy that define Good Vibes. The unknown parent genetics appear to enhance the uplifting, positive mood effects that give the strain its name. While the exact complement genetics remain undocumented, the consistent reputation for bright, cheerful effects suggests sativa-forward breeding.

## Effects & Experience

Good Vibes is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting mood elevation** -- The defining characteristic; a bright, cheerful mental lift that promotes positivity
- **Energy and alertness** -- The sativa-dominant genetics promote daytime functionality and mental engagement
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, happy cerebral effect that is engaging and social
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration
- **Light body warmth** -- A subtle physical component that does not detract from the cerebral focus

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The sativa-dominant cerebral effects are noticeable and engaging, particularly for users with moderate tolerance.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The uplifting cerebral effects may be stimulating for anxiety-prone users at higher doses.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Tropical and citrus-forward with sweet undertones. The terpinolene and limonene combination gives it a bright, mango-tropical aroma with noticeable citrus brightness. The aroma is clean and refreshing, with the tropical character being the most prominent note.
- **Taste:** Tropical and citrus on the inhale, with sweet and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The mango-tropical flavor inherited from Mango Haze is prominent, with citrus brightening adding complexity.
