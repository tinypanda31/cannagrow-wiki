# Gorilla Glue #4 (GG4, Original Glue)

![Gorilla Glue #4 photo](/uploads/images/strains/gorilla-glue-4.jpg)

## Overview

Gorilla Glue #4, commonly abbreviated as GG4 and also known as Original Glue, is a hybrid cultivar created by crossing Chem's Sister, Sour Dubb, and Chocolate Diesel. It is one of the most potent cannabis strains widely available, with THC levels routinely reaching 25-30% and occasionally exceeding 30% in optimal conditions. The strain's name derives from the heavy resin production that causes buds to "glue" together during trimming -- a characteristic so pronounced that the original "Gorilla Glue" name was subject to trademark restrictions, leading to the "Original Glue" rebranding. GG4 delivers deeply relaxing, couch-lock effects that are best suited to experienced users and evening consumption.

## Genetics & Lineage

Gorilla Glue #4 is a three-way cross:

- **Chem's Sister** -- A Chemdawg-relative strain contributing high THC potency and fuel-forward genetics
- **Sour Dubb** -- A Sour Diesel-influenced strain contributing diesel character and sativa energy
- **Chocolate Diesel** -- A fuel-and-chocolate flavored strain contributing depth and complexity to the terpene profile

**Parent lineage:** Chem's Sister x Sour Dubb x Chocolate Diesel

GG4 was developed by Joesy Whales and Lonnie Burnz, who were working with these genetics in the United States. The strain quickly gained recognition for its extraordinary potency and heavy resin production. The "Gorilla Glue" name referenced how the resinous buds would stick to trimming scissors like the famous adhesive brand. When the Gorilla Glue company raised trademark objections, the breeders rebranded as "Original Glue," though GG4 remains the most commonly used designation. The strain has since been widely distributed and has produced numerous derivative crosses.

## Effects & Experience

Gorilla Glue #4 is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep relaxation** -- A heavy, full-body physical relaxation that is among the most pronounced of commonly available strains
- **Couch-lock** -- At moderate to high doses, a heavy sensation that limits desire and ability for physical activity
- **Euphoria** -- A noticeable mental euphoria that accompanies the heavy body effects
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that leads to sleep, making this a quintessential evening/nighttime strain

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 3-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 6-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Very strong. GG4 is one of the most potent strains available and is best suited to experienced users with established tolerance. Newer users should start with very low doses.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy, pine, sour, and chocolate with pungent undertones. The aroma is deep and complex, with a sour-pungent quality layered over an earthy foundation and faint chocolate notes inherited from the Chocolate Diesel parent.
- **Taste:** Earthy and pine on the inhale, with sour and faint chocolate notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and complex, appealing to users who appreciate deep, traditional cannabis taste profiles.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Severe pain management** -- The deeply relaxing body effect is commonly cited for severe chronic pain relief
- **Insomnia** -- The powerful sedating effect makes GG4 a frequent choice for individuals with significant sleep difficulties
- **Stress and anxiety** -- The heavy calming effect is reported to reduce severe stress and tension
- **Appetite stimulation** -- High THC content is associated with notable appetite increase
- **Muscle spasm relief** -- Deep physical relaxation is used for severe muscle spasms and spasticity

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Heavy couch-lock and sedation
- Dizziness, particularly at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia at high doses, more likely due to the extreme THC content
- Impaired coordination and cognitive function during the experience
- Grogginess the following morning at high doses

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience, particularly with the heavy resin management |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-60 days (indoor) -- relatively fast for a strain of this potency |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- moderate stretch during early flower |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The extremely heavy resin production can create humidity-trapping conditions within dense buds, requiring careful airflow management. Responds well to topping and LST. Trimming is notably difficult due to the sticky resin. |

GG4 is moderately challenging to grow. Its relatively fast flowering time (55-60 days) is an advantage, but the extreme resin production creates unique growing challenges. The sticky resin coats everything in the grow space and makes trimming exceptionally difficult -- hence the "Gorilla Glue" name. Adequate air circulation is essential to prevent mold within the dense, resinous buds. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
