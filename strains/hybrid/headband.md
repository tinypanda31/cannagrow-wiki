# Headband

![Headband](/images/strains/headband.png)

## Overview

Headband is a hybrid cultivar created by crossing OG Kush with Sour Diesel, combining two of the most influential and aromatic genetics in cannabis history. The strain is named for its signature effect: a distinctive pressure sensation around the crown of the head, as if wearing a headband. This unique sensation, combined with the diesel-lemon-earthy aroma inherited from its legendary parents, has made Headband one of the most recognizable and sought-after hybrids in the cannabis community. The strain delivers balanced cerebral and body effects, making it versatile for various settings and times of day.

## Genetics & Lineage

Headband is a two-way cross of legendary genetics:

- **OG Kush** -- The iconic hybrid (Chemdawg x Hindu Kush x Lemon Thai) from Santa Cruz, California, contributing fuel-earth-pine aroma, balanced euphoric-body effects, and the foundational Kush genetics present throughout modern cannabis.
- **Sour Diesel** -- The legendary sativa-dominant hybrid originating from the East Coast of the United States, contributing its unmistakable diesel fuel aroma, cerebral energizing effects, and sativa structure.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush x Sour Diesel

Headband emerged from the U.S. cannabis breeding scene as breeders sought to combine the two most influential regional strains: West Coast OG Kush and East Coast Sour Diesel. The cross brings together the earthy, pine potency of OG with the sharp, fuel-forward cerebral energy of Sour Diesel. The name "Headband" directly references the strain's most distinctive reported effect -- the pressure sensation around the head. This effect is believed to result from the unique terpene combination of both parent strains interacting in ways that produce a localized sensory experience uncommon in other cultivars.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Share | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Myrcene | 25-40% | Primary terpene; earthy, herbal base inherited from both parents |
| Caryophyllene | 15-25% | Spicy, peppery depth contributing to the fuel and earthy character |
| Limonene | 10-20% | Significant presence; citrus, lemon notes from both the OG Kush Lemon Thai and Sour Diesel influence |
| Pinene | 5-10% | Pine undertones from the OG Kush side |
| Humulene | 3-8% | Earthy, hoppy accents |

The myrcene-caryophyllene-limonene combination produces Headband's distinctive diesel-lemon-earthy aroma. The lemon character is particularly notable, coming from both the Lemon Thai component of OG Kush and the citrus-forward expression of Sour Diesel. The diesel fuel character comes from the interaction of caryophyllene and myrcene at elevated concentrations.

## Effects & Experience

Headband is widely reported to produce:

- **Signature headband pressure** -- A distinctive pressure sensation around the crown of the head, the strain's defining characteristic
- **Cerebral euphoria** -- A pleasant mental euphoria that is cheerful and engaging
- **Balanced body relaxation** -- Comfortable physical relaxation that accompanies the cerebral effects without heavy sedation
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking, influenced by the Sour Diesel parentage
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally. The headband pressure sensation is typically felt within the first few minutes of inhalation.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The 17-24% THC range and balanced effect profile make Headband accessible to both newer and experienced consumers.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The signature headband pressure sensation is commonly reported but not universally experienced.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Diesel, lemon, and earthy with pine undertones. The diesel fuel character inherited from Sour Diesel is prominent, while the lemon citrus from both parents adds a bright top note. The earthy OG Kush base provides depth. The overall profile is pungent, complex, and immediately recognizable as a cross of two legendary aromatic strains.
- **Taste:** Diesel and lemon on the inhale, with earthy, pine, and subtle citrus notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and layered, with the sharp diesel character softening into a lemon-earthy finish.

The strain rewards attentive cultivation with aromatic, resinous buds carrying the distinctive diesel-lemon profile.
