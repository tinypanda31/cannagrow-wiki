# Hypno

![Hypno photo](/uploads/images/strains/hypno.jpg)

## Overview

Hypno is a potent hybrid strain of unknown heavy genetics, widely recognized for its deeply sedating effects and its fuel, earthy, and sweet aromatic profile. While the exact parent lineage remains undocumented, Hypno's effects and terpene expression suggest genetics rooted in the heavy indica-dominant tradition, possibly involving Kush, OG, or other body-forward genetic families. The strain earns its name from the progressive, sedating quality of its effects, which tend to draw users into a deep, calming body experience that is well-suited for evening and nighttime use. Hypno's mysterious genetics, combined with its reliably potent effects and distinctive aromatic profile, make it a cultivar of interest to both consumers seeking intensity and breeders exploring heavy genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Hypno's exact genetics are undocumented:

- **Unknown Heavy Genetics** -- The parent genetics of Hypno remain unidentified. The strain's effects and aromatic profile suggest lineage rooted in heavy indica-dominant cultivars, possibly involving Kush, OG, or other body-forward genetic families.
- **Sedating Phenotype Selection** -- Hypno was likely selected and stabilized specifically for its deeply sedating character, as the consistency of this effect across batches suggests deliberate phenotype selection.
- **Modern Cultivar** -- Hypno emerged in the modern cannabis market, where its sedating effects and fuel-earthy-sweet profile gained recognition among consumers seeking heavy, evening-appropriate genetics.

**Parent lineage:** Unknown (heavy indica-dominant genetics, selected for sedating effects)

The undocumented genetics of Hypno are part of a broader pattern in the cannabis market, where effective strains emerge from phenotype hunting and are stabilized for commercial distribution without detailed lineage records. What is clear from Hypno's expression is that it carries the genetic markers of heavy, body-forward cultivars: high myrcene content, sedating effects, and the fuel-earthy aromatic profile associated with Kush and OG-derived genetics. The consistency of the sedating character suggests careful phenotype selection and stabilization.

## Effects & Experience

Hypno is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent sedation** -- A deep, progressive drowsiness that intensifies as the experience develops, making it well-suited for evening and nighttime use
- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A full-body sensation of calm and relaxation that releases physical tension and promotes comfort
- **Mental calm** -- A quieting of mental activity that accompanies the body sedation, promoting a restful, peaceful state
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of body and mental calm is frequently cited for comprehensive stress reduction
- **Sleep support** -- The sedating quality makes Hypno a frequent choice for individuals dealing with insomnia

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 6-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The sedating effects build progressively and can be quite heavy, best suited to evening use and users with moderate tolerance.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The sedating effects make Hypno unsuitable for daytime use or activities requiring alertness.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fuel and earthy with sweet undertones. The fuel character is pungent and diesel-like, suggesting OG or Kush-derived genetics. The earthy myrcene base provides depth, while the sweet character adds a subtle indulgence that keeps the profile from becoming one-dimensional. The overall aroma is heavy, warm, and inviting.
- **Taste:** Fuel and earthy on the inhale, with sweet and slightly floral notes on the exhale. The flavor is robust and full-bodied, with the fuel character carrying through the experience and the sweet finish providing a pleasant contrast.
