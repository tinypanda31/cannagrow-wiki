# Ice

![Ice](/images/strains/ice.png)

## Overview

Ice is a hybrid cultivar created by Nirvana Seeds, crossing three legendary genetics: Afghani, Northern Lights, and Shiva Skunk. This three-way cross combines some of the most foundational cannabis genetics ever cultivated, producing a strain that embodies the best of classic indica and sativa breeding. The Afghani parent contributes pure landrace indica genetics from the Hindu Kush mountain region, bringing deep earthy character and heavy body relaxation. Northern Lights, itself a legendary Afghani x Thai cross, adds resin production, sweetness, and compact structure. Shiva Skunk (a Northern Lights x Afghani x Skunk #1 hybrid) contributes vigor, yield, and a skunky-sweet character. The result is a well-balanced hybrid that delivers comfortable physical relaxation paired with gentle cerebral calm, wrapped in an earthy-pine-sweet aroma. Created by Nirvana Seeds, one of the most established cannabis seed banks, Ice benefits from professional breeding and stable genetics that are consistent across generations.

## Genetics & Lineage

Ice is a three-way cross of foundational cannabis genetics:

- **Afghani** -- A pure landrace indica from the Hindu Kush mountain region spanning Afghanistan and Pakistan. Centuries of natural adaptation to the harsh, dry mountain environment produced a compact, resinous plant with deep earthy-musky character and heavy body relaxation effects. One of the most foundational genetics in all of cannabis breeding.
- **Northern Lights** -- A legendary indica hybrid (Afghani x Thai) developed in the Pacific Northwest and later refined in the Netherlands. One of the most famous and influential strains in cannabis history, known for resin production, compact structure, sweet-earthy character, and deeply relaxing effects.
- **Shiva Skunk** -- A Nirvana Seeds creation, itself a cross of Northern Lights x Afghani x Skunk #1. Combines the heavy indica genetics with Skunk #1's vigor, yield, and characteristic skunky-sweet aroma.

**Parent lineage:** Afghani x Northern Lights x Shiva Skunk

Ice was developed by Nirvana Seeds, a Dutch seed bank known for stabilizing classic genetics into reliable, consistent cultivars. The three-way cross of Afghani, Northern Lights, and Shiva Skunk represents a deliberate breeding strategy that combines multiple influential genetics sources into a single, well-balanced cultivar. The result is a strain that carries the heritage of some of the most important genetics in cannabis history, stabilized by professional breeding for consistent performance.

The myrcene-pinene-caryophyllene combination produces Ice's distinctive earthy-pine-sweet aroma. The pinene contribution is particularly notable, giving this strain a fresh pine character that distinguishes it from purely earthy Kush-dominant cultivars.

## Effects & Experience

Ice is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced relaxation** -- A comfortable combination of physical calm and gentle mental relaxation
- **Euphoria** -- A mild, pleasant mental uplift that is approachable and not overwhelming
- **Body calm** -- A noticeable physical relaxation inherited from the Afghani and Northern Lights genetics
- **Stress relief** -- The balanced calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Gentle sedation** -- At higher doses, a comfortable drowsiness that supports rest without heavy sedation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The 14-19% THC range delivers effects that are comfortable and approachable, making Ice suitable for beginners and experienced users alike.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy and pine with sweet undertones. The myrcene-dominant earthy base is brightened by noticeable pine freshness from pinene, creating a clean, forest-like aroma. Sweet notes round out the profile.
- **Taste:** Earthy and pine on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and clean, with the pine character adding a refreshing element to the earthy foundation.

The short flowering time of 50-60 days and compact structure are significant advantages. The strain is forgiving of common growing mistakes and produces consistent results across various environments. Nirvana Seeds' professional breeding ensures stable genetics that perform reliably. The moderate yields are consistent with the compact indica structure, and the quality is high due to the resinous heritage of all three parent genetics.
