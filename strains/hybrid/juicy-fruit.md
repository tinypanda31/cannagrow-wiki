# Juicy Fruit

![Juicy Fruit](/images/strains/juicy-fruit.png)

## Overview

Juicy Fruit is a hybrid cultivar created by crossing Tangerine with unknown sativa genetics. Taking its name from the iconic tropical fruit chewing gum, this strain delivers a remarkably candy-like, tropical-fruity-sweet aroma that closely resembles its namesake. The Tangerine parent (a phenotype of Tangie, itself derived from Orange Skunk and California Orange) contributes intense citrus sweetness, bright limonene expression, and uplifting sativa character. The unknown sativa genetics add cerebral depth, moderate structure, and a balanced effect profile that prevents the strain from being one-directionally citrus. The result is a cultivar that produces bright, cheerful, tropical effects paired with an unmistakably fruity aroma that is among the most candy-like and broadly appealing in cannabis. Its balanced hybrid character makes it suitable for both daytime and early evening use, and its moderate growing profile rewards cultivators with resinous, aromatic flowers.

## Genetics & Lineage

Juicy Fruit is a two-way cross:

- **Tangerine** -- A citrus-forward hybrid phenotype (a selection related to Tangie, which itself derives from Orange Skunk x California Orange). Known for intense tangerine-citrus aroma, high limonene content, and uplifting sativa character. Contributes the dominant tropical fruit terpene profile and cheerful cerebral effects.
- **Unknown sativa** -- Additional sativa genetics of undocumented origin were crossed with Tangerine to produce the final hybrid. The sativa contribution is evident in the uplifting effects, moderate plant structure, and balanced hybrid character.

**Parent lineage:** Tangerine x unknown sativa

Juicy Fruit was bred to amplify the tropical fruit character of Tangerine while adding balanced sativa effects and a more complete hybrid structure. The unknown sativa genetics add cerebral depth and prevent the strain from being purely citrus-forward. The result is a cultivar that delivers both the unmistakable candy-like fruit aroma from the Tangerine parent and a well-rounded effect profile that is uplifting and balanced.

The limonene-myrcene-terpinolene combination produces Juicy Fruit's distinctive tropical-fruity-sweet aroma. Limonene as the dominant terpene drives the intense citrus character, while terpinolene adds the sativa-associated floral-herbal brightness. Together with myrcene's earthy foundation, the profile closely resembles tropical fruit candy.

## Effects & Experience

Juicy Fruit is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting cerebral effects** -- A bright, cheerful mental uplift that is energetic and positive
- **Balanced body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm that accompanies the cerebral uplift without heavy sedation
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, happy mood that promotes sociability and cheerfulness
- **Creativity** -- Some users report enhanced creative thinking at moderate doses
- **Stress relief** -- The cheerful, balanced effect is frequently cited for stress reduction

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 16-21% THC range delivers effects that are engaging and satisfying while remaining approachable for most users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Tropical fruit, sweet, and citrus with candy-like character. The limonene-dominant profile produces an intense tangerine-citrus aroma that is remarkably close to tropical fruit chewing gum. Supporting terpinolene adds floral-herbal brightness.
- **Taste:** Sweet tropical fruit on the inhale, with tangerine citrus and candy-like notes on the mid-palate. The exhale carries earthy and slightly herbal undertones, rounding out the otherwise sweet profile.

The moderate yields are consistent with the hybrid structure, and the quality of the flower is high due to the intense terpene production.
