# Lemon Haze

![Lemon Haze](/images/strains/lemon-haze.png)

## Overview

Lemon Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by crossing Lemon Skunk with Super Silver Haze (or a similar Haze cross, as breeding accounts vary). This strain is best known for its intensely bright lemon-citrus aroma and its energetic, creative, and uplifting effects. Lemon Haze combines the sharp lemon character of Lemon Skunk with the classic Haze cerebral energy, resulting in a strain that is among the most citrus-forward and mentally engaging cultivars available. It has become a staple for daytime consumers seeking a clean, energizing experience with unmistakable lemon flavor.

## Genetics & Lineage

Lemon Haze is a two-way cross:

- **Lemon Skunk** -- A hybrid strain selected for its intense lemon aroma and flavor, itself derived from Skunk #1 (Afghani x Mexican x Colombian) genetics with a lemon-forward phenotype. Contributes the defining lemon character and Skunk vigor.
- **Super Silver Haze** -- The legendary sativa-dominant hybrid (Skunk #1 x Northern Lights x Haze) that won multiple High Times Cannabis Cup awards. Contributes classic Haze cerebral energy, sativa structure, and award-winning genetics.

**Parent lineage:** Lemon Skunk x Super Silver Haze

Lemon Haze emerged from the European and U.S. cannabis breeding scenes, where breeders sought to combine intense citrus character with classic Haze cerebral effects. Some breeding accounts describe the Haze parent as a similar Haze cross rather than specifically Super Silver Haze, but the result is consistent: a sativa-dominant strain with bright lemon aroma and energizing cerebral effects. The combination brings together two influential genetic lines -- the Skunk family (through both parents) and the Haze family -- creating a strain that is both flavorful and functionally energizing.

## Effects & Experience

Lemon Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic lift** -- A clear, energizing cerebral effect that promotes alertness and activity
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration, characteristic of Haze genetics
- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A cheerful, positive mood lift that is bright and engaging
- **Focus** -- Improved mental clarity and concentration
- **Social engagement** -- Increased sociability and conversation at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The moderate THC range and sativa-leaning effects make Lemon Haze suitable for daytime use and accessible to newer consumers.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Bright lemon, citrus, and herbal with earthy undertones. The lemon aroma is the defining characteristic -- sharp, clean, and unmistakably citrus. The Haze influence adds an herbal complexity, while the Skunk genetics provide an earthy base. The overall profile is fresh, clean, and intensely lemon-forward.
- **Taste:** Bright lemon on the inhale, with citrus, herbal, and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is crisp and refreshing, with a clean lemon character that is among the most citrus-forward in cannabis.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry eyes
- Mild anxiety possible in sensitive individuals due to the cerebral, energizing nature
- Restlessness at higher doses
- Dizziness
- Increased heart rate
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day

| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500-600 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- significant sativa stretch during early flower; training and height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions. The longer flowering time suits outdoor growers with extended seasons. |

Lemon Haze is a moderately manageable strain to grow. The longer flowering time and sativa stretch are the primary challenges, requiring patience and training. The yield potential is good, and the plant exhibits healthy vigor from its Haze and Skunk genetics. The strain rewards attentive cultivation with highly aromatic, resinous buds carrying the intense lemon profile.
