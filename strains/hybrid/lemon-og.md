# Lemon OG

![Lemon Og](/images/strains/lemon-og.jpg)

## Overview

Lemon OG is a hybrid cultivar created by crossing OG Kush with Lemon Skunk, combining the legendary fuel-earthy character of OG Kush with the intense citrus brightness of Lemon Skunk. The OG Kush parent contributes its foundational genetics -- the fuel-earthy-pine aroma, balanced euphoric-body effects, and compact structure that made it the most influential strain in modern cannabis breeding. The Lemon Skunk parent (a cross of Skunk #1 and an unknown lemon-scented selection) brings intense lemon-citrus character, high limonene content, and bright cerebral energy. The result is a cultivar that delivers a distinctive lemon-fuel-earthy aroma where the bright, sharp lemon character sits atop the deep, pungent OG Kush fuel base. Its balanced effect profile provides both cerebral stimulation and physical relaxation, making it suitable for a wide range of use cases from daytime activity to evening wind-down. Lemon OG has become one of the most popular OG Kush phenotype selections, valued for its unique combination of classic Kush depth and bright citrus freshness.

## Genetics & Lineage

Lemon OG is a two-way cross of influential cannabis genetics:

- **OG Kush** -- A legendary hybrid of debated origin, widely believed to involve Chemdawg, Hindu Kush, and a lemon-scented selection. OG Kush is the genetic foundation for an enormous family of modern hybrids. Contributes fuel-earthy-pine aroma, balanced effects, dense resin production, and compact structure.
- **Lemon Skunk** -- A hybrid cross of Skunk #1 and an unknown lemon-scented selection, known for intense lemon-citrus aroma, high limonene content, and bright, uplifting effects. Skunk #1 contributes vigor, yield, and resilience, while the lemon selection drives the dominant citrus terpene profile.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush x Lemon Skunk

Lemon OG was bred to amplify the citrus character within the OG Kush genetic framework. The cross combines the deep, pungent fuel-earthy base of OG Kush with the sharp, bright lemon-citrus profile of Lemon Skunk. The resulting cultivar is one of the most successful OG Kush phenotype selections, distinguished by its intense lemon character that sits distinctly atop the classic Kush foundation. Lemon OG has since become a valuable genetics source for further breeding, and its lemon-fuel combination has been replicated and refined by numerous breeders.

The interplay between bright citrus and deep fuel is the defining aromatic feature.

## Effects & Experience

Lemon OG is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced cerebral and body effects** -- A comfortable combination of mental clarity and physical relaxation
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging
- **Body calm** -- A noticeable physical relaxation from the OG Kush genetics
- **Stress relief** -- The balanced calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Focus** -- The bright limonene character is reported to support mental clarity at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 17-22% THC range delivers effects that are engaging and satisfying while remaining approachable for most users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Intense lemon and fuel with earthy undertones. The limonene-dominant lemon character is sharp and bright, sitting clearly atop the deep fuel-earthy OG Kush base. The combination is distinctive and easily recognizable.
- **Taste:** Lemon and fuel on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The lemon character is prominent throughout the flavor profile, with the fuel-earthy Kush base providing depth and complexity.
