# Marker #2

![Marker #2 photo](/uploads/images/strains/marker-2.jpg)

## Overview

Marker #2 is an indica-dominant hybrid phenotype derived from the Marker genetics (OG Kush x Afghani x Skunk) crossed with an unknown phenotype selection from the Seed Junky genetics program. As a phenotype selection within the broader Marker family, Marker #2 expresses a variation on the original Marker's fuel-forward character, with a slightly shifted balance toward body-heavy relaxation and a sweeter aromatic profile. The Seed Junky breeding approach emphasizes selecting specific phenotypes that express desirable characteristics, and Marker #2 represents a cut that emphasizes the deeper body effects while retaining the fuel-earthy foundation of the original Marker genetics. The strain is well-suited for evening use and appeals to consumers seeking the OG Kush-derived fuel character with enhanced body relaxation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Marker #2 is a phenotype selection within the Marker family from Seed Junky genetics:

- **Marker** -- An indica-dominant cultivar from Seed Junky genetics (OG Kush x Afghani x Skunk), known for fuel and earthy aromatics with heavy body relaxation. Marker provides the foundational genetics for the #2 phenotype selection.
- **Unknown Phenotype (Seed Junky)** -- The second parent in the Marker #2 cross is an undocumented phenotype selection from the Seed Junky genetics program. This unknown selection was chosen to shift the balance toward deeper body effects and a sweeter aromatic expression.

**Parent lineage:** Marker x unknown Seed Junky phenotype selection

Marker #2 was developed within the Seed Junky genetics program as a phenotype selection that emphasized different characteristics from the original Marker. While the original Marker is known for its balanced fuel-earthy-citrus profile, Marker #2 shifts the balance toward a sweeter, more body-heavy expression. The unknown Seed Junky phenotype was selected to complement the Marker genetics, enhancing the myrcene-driven body relaxation while retaining the fuel-forward OG Kush character. This approach of selecting and crossing specific phenotypes is a hallmark of the Seed Junky breeding methodology.

## Effects & Experience

Marker #2 is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced onset transitioning to body-heavy** -- Initial mental engagement and mild euphoria that progressively shifts into deep body relaxation
- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A full-body sensation of calm and relaxation that releases physical tension and promotes comfort
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that intensifies as the experience develops, making it well-suited for evening use
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental calm and deep body relaxation is frequently cited for comprehensive stress reduction
- **Sleep support** -- The sedating quality at higher doses supports sleep onset

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The THC range delivers a meaningful experience that transitions from balanced to body-heavy, best suited to evening use.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The body-heavy sedating effects make Marker #2 less suitable for daytime use.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fuel and sweet with earthy undertones. The OG Kush fuel character is prominent, but the #2 phenotype shifts the balance toward a sweeter expression compared to the original Marker. The earthy myrcene base provides depth, while the sweet character adds an indulgent quality. The overall aroma is warm, pungent, and layered.
- **Taste:** Fuel and sweet on the inhale, with earthy and slightly piney notes on the exhale. The flavor is robust and full-bodied, with the fuel character carrying through the experience and the sweet finish providing a pleasant contrast to the heavier earth tones.
