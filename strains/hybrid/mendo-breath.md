# Mendo Breath

![Mendo Breath](/images/strains/mendo-breath.png)

## Overview

Mendo Breath is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Mendo Purp with OG Kush Breath (a phenotype of OG Kush). This strain combines the rich purple grape heritage of the Mendo lineage with the deep, fuel-forward character of OG Kush Breath, producing a cultivar with a distinctive grape-vanilla-earthy aroma and heavy body relaxation effects. The Mendo Purp parent (originating from the Mendocino County cannabis tradition in Northern California) contributes grape sweetness, purple coloration potential, and indica-dominant relaxation. The OG Kush Breath parent brings the fuel-earthy depth, vanilla undertones, and heavy physical calm that characterize the OG Kush Breath phenotype selection. The result is a deeply relaxing indica-dominant cultivar with a complex dessert-like aroma that blends sweet grape and creamy vanilla over an earthy foundation. Its heavy body effects make it particularly well-suited for evening and nighttime use, and its moderate growing profile rewards experienced cultivators with dense, colorful flowers.

## Genetics & Lineage

Mendo Breath is a two-way cross:

- **Mendo Purp** -- An indica-dominant cultivar originating from the Mendocino County cannabis tradition in Northern California. The Mendo lineage is named after Mendocino County, one of California's most historically significant cannabis-growing regions. Mendo Purp is known for grape sweetness, potential purple coloration in cool conditions, indica-dominant relaxation effects, and earthy-sweet character. Contributes the grape-vanilla aromatic profile and heavy body genetics.
- **OG Kush Breath** -- A phenotype selection of OG Kush (itself involving Chemdawg, Hindu Kush, and lemon-scented genetics) that emphasizes deep fuel character with vanilla undertones and heavy physical relaxation. OG Kush Breath is part of the "Breath" family of strains (alongside Wedding Cake Breath and others) known for dessert-like vanilla character and sedating body effects.

**Parent lineage:** Mendo Purp x OG Kush Breath

Mendo Breath was bred to combine the grape-sweet heritage of the Mendo lineage with the vanilla-fuel depth of OG Kush Breath. The resulting cultivar inherits the purple grape character from Mendo Purp and the heavy, dessert-like vanilla-fuel profile from OG Kush Breath. The Mendocino County heritage connects this strain to one of California's most storied cannabis-growing regions, while the OG Kush Breath genetics tie it to the most influential breeding family in modern cannabis.

## Effects & Experience

Mendo Breath is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- The defining characteristic; deep physical calm that progresses through the body, ideal for evening use
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that precedes the body relaxation, providing initial cheerfulness
- **Sedation** -- At moderate to higher doses, pronounced drowsiness and sleepiness
- **Stress relief** -- The heavy body relaxation is frequently cited for deep stress release
- **Sleep support** -- Commonly used as a nighttime strain to assist with sleep onset

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The 18-23% THC range delivers potent effects that build into heavy body relaxation best suited for evening use.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the deeply sedating nature of this strain, it is not recommended for daytime use or when operating vehicles or machinery.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Grape, vanilla, and earthy with sweet dessert-like character. The myrcene-dominant earthy base is sweetened by grape and vanilla notes, creating a complex dessert-like aroma. The grape character is particularly noticeable, inherited from the Mendo Purp parent.
- **Taste:** Grape and vanilla on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and sweet, with the grape-vanilla combination being the most distinctive element.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- indica-dominant genetics require attention to density and humidity management |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 350-450 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions. Purple coloration may develop in cool nighttime temperatures during late flowering. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The dense indica flower structure requires adequate airflow and humidity control to prevent mold. The compact structure is manageable indoors. Purple coloration is temperature-dependent and develops best with cool nighttime temperatures (low 60s F / mid-teens C) during the final weeks of flowering. Responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. |

Mendo Breath is a moderately manageable strain with the compact indica-dominant structure that is well-suited to indoor cultivation. The moderate flowering time and compact height are advantages. The dense flower production requires careful humidity management to prevent mold. The potential for purple coloration is an attractive feature that develops under cool nighttime temperatures during late flowering. The moderate yields are consistent with the indica-dominant structure, and the quality and resin production are high.
