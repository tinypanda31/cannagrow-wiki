# Mimosa

![Mimosa Eva](/images/strains/mimosa-eva.png)

## Overview

Mimosa (also known as Mimosa Evo) is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by crossing Clementine with Purple Punch. This strain combines the bright citrus energy of Clementine with the sweet, relaxing genetics of Purple Punch, resulting in a cultivar with a vibrant citrus-orange-berry aroma and an effect profile that begins with uplifting cerebral energy before settling into gentle body relaxation. Named after the classic brunch cocktail, Mimosa delivers a bright, cheerful experience that is well-suited to daytime and social settings. The strain has gained recognition as part of the modern fruit-forward cannabis trend and as a standout among citrus-dominant cultivars.

## Genetics & Lineage

Mimosa is a two-way cross:

- **Clementine** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid (Tangie x Jack Herer lineage) known for its intense orange-citrus aroma and energizing cerebral effects. Clementine itself descends from the Tangerine Dream and Jack Herer families, contributing bright citrus character and sativa structure.
- **Purple Punch** -- An indica-dominant hybrid (Granddaddy Purple x Larry OG) known for its sweet grape-berry aroma and deep body relaxation. Contributes the berry-sweet character and a relaxing body effect to balance the sativa energy.

**Parent lineage:** Clementine x Purple Punch

Mimosa emerged from the modern U.S. cannabis breeding scene, continuing the trend of crossing bright citrus sativas with sweet, relaxing indicas to create balanced, flavorful hybrids. The cross brings together two contrasting genetic profiles: the energetic, citrus-forward Clementine and the sweet, relaxing Purple Punch. The result is a strain that begins with a bright cerebral lift (from Clementine) before settling into a comfortable body calm (from Purple Punch). The name "Mimosa" references the orange juice and champagne cocktail, reflecting the strain's bright citrus character and celebratory, uplifting effects.

## Effects & Experience

Mimosa is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting cerebral** -- A bright, cheerful mental lift that promotes positive mood and mental engagement
- **Energetic focus** -- Clear-headed energy from the Clementine parentage, suitable for daytime activities
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration
- **Gentle body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm from the Purple Punch influence that follows the cerebral lift without heavy sedation
- **Social engagement** -- Increased sociability and conversational energy at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 17-22% THC range and balanced sativa-leaning effects make Mimosa suitable for daytime use and accessible to a broad range of consumers.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Bright orange, citrus, and berry with sweet herbal undertones. The orange-citrus aroma inherited from Clementine is the dominant character, while the berry sweetness from Purple Punch adds depth. The overall profile is vibrant, fruity, and approachable.
- **Taste:** Citrus-orange on the inhale, with berry, sweet, and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is bright and fruit-forward, with a clean citrus character that transitions into a sweet berry finish.

Mimosa is a moderately manageable strain to grow. The relatively short flowering time for a sativa-dominant strain (55-65 days) is a practical advantage, as is the manageable plant height. The genetic diversity from crossing two contrasting cultivars contributes plant health and vigor. The strain rewards attentive cultivation with aromatic, resinous buds carrying the distinctive citrus-berry profile.
