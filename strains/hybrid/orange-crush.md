# Orange Crush

![Orange Crush](/images/strains/orange-crush.jpg)

## Overview

Orange Crush is a hybrid cultivar created through a cross involving California Orange and Blueberry lineage. This strain is best known for its vibrant orange-citrus aroma that is among the most intense and true-to-fruit citrus profiles in cannabis. The combination of the California Orange's bright citrus character with Blueberry's sweet berry genetics produces a strain with an approachable, universally appealing flavor profile and effects that deliver uplifting mental clarity with gentle body relaxation. Orange Crush has become a favorite among consumers seeking a clean, citrus-forward experience that is suitable for daytime and social settings.

## Genetics & Lineage

Orange Crush is a cross involving:

- **California Orange** -- A hybrid strain known for its intense orange-citrus aroma and balanced effects, descending from the broader Orange family of genetics. Contributes the defining orange citrus character and moderate vigor.
- **Blueberry** -- The legendary indica by DJ Short (Purple Thai x Thai x Afghani), contributing sweet berry aroma, comfortable body relaxation, and the genetics that have made Blueberry one of the most influential strains in cannabis breeding.

**Parent lineage:** California Orange x Blueberry

Orange Crush emerged from the U.S. hybrid breeding scene, where breeders sought to combine intense citrus character with the sweet, relaxing genetics of Blueberry. The cross brings together two fruit-forward genetic lines -- the bright, clean citrus of California Orange and the rich, sweet berry of Blueberry. The result is a strain with a vibrant, approachable aroma and a balanced effect profile that is accessible to a wide range of consumers. Like many modern hybrids, Orange Crush has been pheno-hunted by various breeders, resulting in selections that may lean slightly more toward the citrus or berry end of the spectrum.

## Effects & Experience

Orange Crush is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting clarity** -- A clean, clear-headed mental lift that promotes focus and positive mood
- **Gentle euphoria** -- A pleasant, approachable mood lift that is cheerful without being overwhelming
- **Creative engagement** -- Moderate mental clarity and creative thinking
- **Light body relaxation** -- A subtle physical calm from the Blueberry influence that accompanies the cerebral effects without sedation
- **Social comfort** -- Increased social ease and conversational energy at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The moderate THC range and balanced effects make Orange Crush one of the most universally accessible strains, suitable for beginners and experienced users alike.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Bright orange, citrus, and sweet with berry undertones. The orange aroma is the defining characteristic -- clean, vibrant, and unmistakably citrus. The Blueberry inheritance adds a subtle berry sweetness beneath the citrus top note. The overall profile is fresh, fruity, and highly approachable.
- **Taste:** Orange-citrus on the inhale, with sweet, berry, and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is bright and fruit-forward, with a clean citrus character that transitions into a sweet berry finish inherited from Blueberry.

The strain rewards attentive cultivation with aromatic, resinous buds carrying the intense orange-citrus profile. Cooler temperatures during late flower may enhance the citrus and berry character.
