# Pennywise

![Pennywise](/images/strains/pennywise.png)

## Overview

Pennywise is an indica-dominant, high-CBD hybrid created by crossing Harlequin with AC/DC. This strain was specifically bred for therapeutic users seeking the medical benefits of cannabis with a balanced CBD:THC ratio that minimizes psychoactive intensity while maximizing therapeutic relief. The Harlequin parent contributes its well-documented high-CBD genetics and clear-headed character, while the AC/DC parent brings exceptional CBD potency (AC/DC is one of the highest-CBD strains available, often testing at CBD:THC ratios of 20:1 or higher). The result is a cultivar that delivers meaningful therapeutic relief with a moderated psychoactive experience, making it suitable for patients who need daytime functionality. Its earthy-peppery-sweet aroma is pleasant and approachable, and its indica-dominant structure provides gentle body relaxation that complements the CBD-driven therapeutic effects.

## Genetics & Lineage

Pennywise is a two-way cross of high-CBD genetics:

- **Harlequin** -- A well-known high-CBD sativa-dominant hybrid (Colombian Gold x a Thai sativa x an unknown Swiss landrace) with a typical CBD:THC ratio of 5:2, renowned for clear-headed therapeutic relief
- **AC/DC** -- A high-CBD phenotype of the Cannatonic lineage (itself a cross of an unknown genetics), often testing at CBD:THC ratios of 20:1 or higher, one of the highest-CBD cultivars available

**Parent lineage:** Harlequin x AC/DC

Pennywise was bred to combine the best of two high-CBD lineages. The Harlequin genetics contribute the well-documented therapeutic profile and clear-headed character, while the AC/DC parent pushes the CBD ratio even higher. The result is a strain that delivers meaningful CBD-driven therapeutic relief with enough THC to provide the entourage effect (the synergistic interaction between cannabinoids and terpenes) without overwhelming psychoactive effects. This balanced CBD:THC ratio is considered ideal for many medical applications, as research suggests that the combination of CBD and THC together can be more effective than either cannabinoid alone for certain conditions.

The low THC and high CBD range is the defining feature of Pennywise. The CBD:THC ratio typically falls between 2:1 and 5:1, which is considered optimal for many therapeutic applications. The moderated THC provides enough psychoactive character to enhance the entourage effect while the high CBD delivers the primary therapeutic relief.

The caryophyllene-humulene-myrcene combination produces Pennywise's distinctive earthy-peppery-sweet aroma. Caryophyllene is notable as the only terpene known to directly interact with the body's endocannabinoid system (CB2 receptors), which may enhance the strain's therapeutic profile when combined with high CBD content.

## Effects & Experience

Pennywise is widely reported to produce:

- **Clear-headed therapeutic relief** -- The defining characteristic; meaningful symptom relief with preserved mental clarity and daytime functionality
- **Gentle body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm from the indica-dominant genetics that does not impair cognitive function
- **Minimal psychoactive intensity** -- The low THC:high CBD ratio produces mild psychoactive effects that are manageable for most users
- **Anxiety reduction** -- The CBD content is frequently reported to reduce anxiety without sedation
- **Pain relief** -- The combination of CBD and caryophyllene is cited for meaningful pain management

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild. The high CBD and low THC produce a gentle experience focused on therapeutic relief rather than intoxication.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Pennywise is bred for therapeutic use and is generally well-tolerated, but individual responses to CBD:THC ratios vary.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy and peppery with sweet undertones. The caryophyllene-dominant profile gives it a spicy, peppery aroma with an earthy foundation from humulene and myrcene. The sweet undertones round out the profile and make it more approachable than purely earthy-peppery strains.
- **Taste:** Earthy and peppery on the inhale, with sweet and slightly woody notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and earthy, with the peppery character being the most distinctive element.

## Medical Uses

:::danger
**Not Medical Advice** -- The following lists commonly reported therapeutic applications. This does not constitute medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before using cannabis therapeutically.
:::

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Chronic pain** -- The CBD:THC combination with caryophyllene is frequently cited for meaningful pain management
- **Anxiety disorders** -- The high CBD content is reported to reduce anxiety while preserving functionality
- **Inflammation** -- The CBD and caryophyllene/humulene combination is used for anti-inflammatory relief
- **Seizure disorders** -- High-CBD strains like Pennywise are explored for seizure management
- **PTSD** -- The clear-headed calming effect is reported to help with PTSD symptoms
- **Autoimmune conditions** -- The anti-inflammatory properties of CBD and caryophyllene are explored for autoimmune symptom management

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Mild dizziness at higher doses
- Occasional headache with elevated CBD consumption (rare)
- Drowsiness at higher doses, though less commonly reported than with high-THC strains
- Digestive changes with oral consumption at higher CBD doses
