# Pineapple Express

![Pineapple Express](/images/strains/pineapple-express.png)

## Overview

Pineapple Express is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by crossing Trainwreck with Hawaiian. While the strain existed before the 2008 comedy film of the same name, the movie's popularity catapulted Pineapple Express into mainstream recognition, making it one of the most widely recognized cannabis strain names in popular culture. Beyond its cultural fame, Pineapple Express is a genuinely quality sativa-dominant hybrid with a distinctive pineapple-tropical-citrus aroma, energetic and euphoric effects, and a balanced profile that makes it suitable for daytime use. The Trainwreck parent contributes potency and complexity, while the Hawaiian parent brings tropical flavor and uplifting sativa genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Pineapple Express is a two-way cross:

- **Trainwreck** -- A potent sativa-dominant hybrid (Mexican x Thai x Afghani) known for intense cerebral effects and earthy-spicy aroma
- **Hawaiian** -- A sativa-dominant strain from Hawaii, contributing tropical flavor, uplifting effects, and island sativa genetics

**Parent lineage:** Trainwreck x Hawaiian

The strain existed in the cannabis community before the 2008 Sony Pictures film "Pineapple Express," which featured the strain prominently. The film's success created massive consumer demand for the strain, leading to widespread cultivation and distribution. The exact original breeder is not definitively documented, but the Trainwreck x Hawaiian cross is the most widely accepted genetic account.

## Effects & Experience

Pineapple Express is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- An invigorating cerebral effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Euphoria** -- A bright, cheerful mood lift that is engaging and social
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental engagement
- **Sociability** -- Increased social comfort and engagement, making it popular for social settings

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and functional, making Pineapple Express well-suited to daytime and social use.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Pineapple, tropical fruit, and citrus with earthy and spicy undertones. The tropical aroma is pronounced and easily recognizable, closely resembling fresh pineapple and tropical fruits.
- **Taste:** Pineapple and tropical citrus on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and vibrant, delivering a full tropical experience that is both sweet and complex.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Dizziness
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day

## Growing Information

Pineapple Express is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa characteristics require some height management, and the moderate-to-long flowering time (60-70 days) demands patience. The tropical flavor and aroma are phenotype-dependent, so selecting quality genetics is important. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October.
