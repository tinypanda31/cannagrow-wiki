# Rodelizer

![Rodelizer photo](/uploads/images/strains/rodelizer.jpg)

## Overview

Rodelizer is a sativa-dominant hybrid strain of unknown genetics with notable ruderalis influence, creating a cultivar that bridges the gap between traditional cannabis and autoflowering genetics. The name "Rodelizer" references the rodelization process -- a stress-induced hermaphroditic breeding technique -- combined with the autoflowering character inherited from ruderalis genetics. The strain is known for its sweet, earthy, and floral aroma profile, with effects that are energetic and creative, making it well-suited for daytime use. Rodelizer's unique genetic composition, combining unknown traditional genetics with ruderalis influence, makes it a cultivar of interest to breeders exploring autoflowering genetics and consumers seeking an uplifting, functional experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

Rodelizer's genetics combine unknown traditional cultivars with ruderalis influence:

- **Unknown Genetics** -- The primary parent in the Rodelizer cross remains undocumented, but the sweet, earthy, and floral aromatic profile suggests possible landrace-derived or heirloom genetics with sativa-dominant character. The unknown parent contributes the cerebral, energetic effects and the core terpene profile.
- **Ruderalis Influence** -- Cannabis ruderalis is a subspecies of cannabis native to Central and Eastern Europe, known for its autoflowering trait (flowering based on age rather than light cycle). The ruderalis influence in Rodelizer contributes faster flowering, environmental resilience, and a slightly different terpene expression compared to pure sativa or indica genetics.

**Parent lineage:** Unknown (sativa-dominant) x ruderalis-influenced genetics

Rodelizer was developed to combine the energetic, creative effects of sativa-dominant genetics with the autoflowering resilience and fast-flowering character of ruderalis. The resulting strain expresses a unique sweet-earthy-floral aromatic profile that reflects both the unknown sativa parent and the distinct terpene contribution of the ruderalis genetics. The rodelization process used in breeding may have contributed to the strain's genetic stability and the consistency of its sativa-forward effects. This cross represents the ongoing interest in incorporating ruderalis genetics into photoperiod cultivars for their resilience and unique characteristics.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with floral undertones. The terpinolene-dominant profile produces a floral, herbal sweetness that is layered with earthy depth. The overall aroma is bright and inviting, with a sweetness that is more floral and herbal than dessert-like. The floral character is the strain's most distinctive feature.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with floral and slightly piney notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and refreshing, with the floral character carrying through the experience and providing a crisp, sativa-forward taste.
