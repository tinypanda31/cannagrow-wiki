# Runtz Muffin

![Runtz Muffin photo](/uploads/images/strains/runtz-muffin.jpg)

## Overview

Runtz Muffin is a hybrid cultivar created by crossing Zkittlez with Wedding Cake, combining two of the most influential dessert-forward genetics in modern cannabis breeding. The Zkittlez parent (Grape Ape x Grapefruit) contributes an intensely fruity, candy-like terpene profile that is among the most sweet and tropical in cannabis. The Wedding Cake parent (Triangle Kush x Animal Mints) brings dense resin production, heavy body relaxation, and a sweet-vanilla-earthy character that complements the Zkittlez fruitiness. The result is a cultivar with a distinctive sweet-fruity-candy aroma that closely resembles confectionery, paired with balanced euphoric and body effects that satisfy both the head and the body. The "Muffin" designation reflects the dessert-forward character inherited from both parents, combining Zkittlez's candy fruit with Wedding Cake's vanilla sweetness. Runtz Muffin has gained a strong following among consumers seeking dessert-like strains and cultivators who appreciate the influential genetic pedigree.

## Genetics & Lineage

Runtz Muffin is a two-way cross of influential modern genetics:

- **Zkittlez** -- A hybrid strain created by crossing Grape Ape (Mendocino Purps x Skunk #1 x Afghani) with Grapefruit (a sativa-dominant hybrid). Zkittlez is known for its intensely fruity, candy-like aroma and is one of the most commercially valuable genetics in modern cannabis. Contributes the dominant sweet-fruity-candy terpene profile and balanced effects.
- **Wedding Cake** -- A hybrid cross of Triangle Kush x Animal Mints, known for sweet-vanilla-earthy character, dense resin production, and heavy body relaxation. Wedding Cake is one of the most widely cultivated and commercially successful dessert strains. Contributes vanilla sweetness, body relaxation, and resin density.

**Parent lineage:** Zkittlez x Wedding Cake

Runtz Muffin was bred to combine the candy-like fruit character of Zkittlez with the vanilla-sweet body depth of Wedding Cake. The resulting cultivar inherits the best of both parents: an intensely sweet, fruity, candy-like aroma paired with balanced euphoric and body effects. The Zkittlez genetics bring the tropical fruit candy profile, while Wedding Cake adds the heavy, dessert-like vanilla depth. The cross is part of the broader "Runtz" family of strains (Runtz itself being a Zkittlez x Gelato cross), and Runtz Muffin represents an alternative expression of the Zkittlez genetics paired with a different dessert-forward parent.

The caryophyllene-myrcene-limonene combination, with supporting linalool, produces Runtz Muffin's distinctive sweet-fruity-candy aroma. The interplay between the Zkittlez-derived tropical fruit candy and the Wedding Cake-derived vanilla sweetness creates a remarkably confectionery-like profile.

## Effects & Experience

Runtz Muffin is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- A strong, pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and engaging
- **Body relaxation** -- A comfortable physical calm from the Wedding Cake genetics that accompanies the cerebral euphoria
- **Mood elevation** -- A bright, happy mood lift that promotes positivity
- **Stress relief** -- The balanced calm is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Social engagement** -- Some users report increased sociability at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The 19-24% THC range delivers potent effects best suited to experienced users, though the balanced character makes the intensity more manageable than one-directional high-THC strains.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, fruity, and candy-like with vanilla undertones. The Zkittlez-derived tropical fruit candy character is the most prominent element, supported by Wedding Cake's vanilla sweetness. The aroma is rich and indulgent, closely resembling confectionery.
- **Taste:** Sweet tropical fruit and candy on the inhale, with vanilla and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and dessert-forward, with the candy-like character being the most distinctive element.

| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |

| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The dense flower structure inherited from Wedding Cake requires adequate airflow to prevent moisture buildup. Runtz Muffin is a moderately manageable strain that rewards cultivators with dense, resinous, intensely aromatic flowers. The balanced hybrid structure produces moderate stretch and manageable height. The sweet-fruity-candy terpene profile is best developed under stable, warm conditions with careful humidity management during flowering. Proper curing is essential to preserve the candy-like aroma. The moderate yields are consistent with the hybrid structure, and the quality and market value of the flower are high.
