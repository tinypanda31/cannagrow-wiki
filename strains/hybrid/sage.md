# Sage

![Sage](/images/strains/sage.png)

## Overview

Sage is a sativa-dominant hybrid of unknown Northern European genetics that has earned a reputation among cannabis connoisseurs for its distinctive herbal-pine-earthy aroma, focused and creative cerebral effects, and manageable growing profile. The strain's name is a direct reference to its herbal, sage-like aroma, which is one of the most distinctive and recognizable profiles in the cannabis world. Sage delivers clean, clear-headed sativa energy that promotes focus and creative thinking, making it a popular choice for daytime use among creative professionals and anyone seeking mental clarity. Its herbal character sets it apart from the sweeter, fruitier strains that dominate the modern market, offering users a more traditional, earth-forward cannabis experience that harkens back to classic landrace and heritage cultivars.

## Genetics & Lineage

The exact genetics of Sage are unknown, but the strain is believed to originate from Northern European breeding programs:

- **Unknown Northern European Genetics** -- The strain is believed to be a selection from European breeding programs, possibly involving Haze genetics common in the Netherlands and surrounding regions
- **Sativa-forward Heritage** -- The effects and growing characteristics strongly suggest sativa-dominant genetics typical of European breeding programs

**Parent lineage:** Unknown (Northern European sativa-dominant selection)

Sage emerged from the European cannabis breeding tradition, which has produced many of the world's most influential and genetically refined hybrid strains. While the exact parentage remains undocumented, the strain's sativa-dominant character, herbal aroma, and focused effects are consistent with quality European breeding priorities. The strain has maintained a consistent reputation for distinctive herbal character and clean sativa effects despite the uncertainty around its specific genetic origins.

## Effects & Experience

Sage is widely reported to produce:

- **Focused mental clarity** -- The defining characteristic; a clean, clear-headed sativa effect that promotes concentration and mental engagement
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration, making it popular among creative professionals
- **Energy and alertness** -- The sativa-dominant genetics promote daytime functionality and mental engagement
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, bright mental lift that accompanies the focused energy
- **Light body warmth** -- A subtle physical component that does not detract from the cerebral focus

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The sativa-dominant cerebral effects are clear and focused, with an approachable intensity suitable for a broad range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The focused cerebral effects may be stimulating for anxiety-prone users at higher doses.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Herbal and pine-forward with earthy undertones. The terpinolene-dominant profile gives it a distinctive herbal, sage-like aroma that is immediately recognizable. The pinene adds a fresh pine character, while the caryophyllene provides a subtle spicy warmth. The overall aroma is clean, fresh, and distinctly herbal.
- **Taste:** Herbal and pine on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The sage-like herbal flavor is prominent and distinctive, with the pine character adding freshness and the earthy exhale providing depth.

- Dry eyes
