# SFV OG

![Sfv Og](/images/strains/sfv-og.png)

## Overview

SFV OG (San Fernando Valley OG) is a phenotype selection of OG Kush that originated in the San Fernando Valley, California. This strain represents one of the most influential and widely distributed OG Kush phenotype selections, known for its intense fuel-pine-citrus aroma and potent effects that begin with a strong cerebral lift before settling into deep body relaxation. SFV OG has become a foundational genetics in modern cannabis breeding, serving as a parent in numerous subsequent hybrid crosses including Fire OG, Ghost OG, and many others. Its status as a keeper pheno of OG Kush means that SFV OG expresses the classic OG profile at an elevated intensity.

## Genetics & Lineage

SFV OG is a phenotype selection of OG Kush:

- **OG Kush** -- The legendary hybrid (Chemdawg x Hindu Kush x Lemon Thai) from Santa Cruz, California. SFV OG is not a cross but rather a specific phenotype (individual plant selection) from the OG Kush population that was identified and cloned in the San Fernando Valley.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush phenotype selection from the San Fernando Valley, California

SFV OG was identified in the San Fernando Valley, California, during the era when OG Kush was being extensively cloned and pheno-hunted by growers throughout Southern California. When a strain is widely cloned, individual plants (phenotypes) express slightly different characteristics -- some are more earthy, some more fuel-forward, some more citrus. The SFV OG phenotype was selected for its particularly intense fuel aroma, potent effects, and slightly more cerebral-leaning experience compared to other OG selections. The strain was then propagated through cloning and distributed throughout the cannabis community, becoming one of the most widely recognized OG phenotype selections alongside Tahoe OG, Fire OG, and Ghost OG.

## Effects & Experience

SFV OG is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent cerebral lift** -- A strong initial mental euphoria that is slightly more cerebral than other OG phenotype selections
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, engaging mental state that is cheerful and clear
- **Progressive body relaxation** -- As the cerebral effects settle, a deep physical relaxation develops
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported significant reduction in mental and physical tension
- **Sedation at higher doses** -- Drowsiness and heavy relaxation at elevated doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The 18-23% THC range and OG-derived effects make SFV OG suitable for experienced users, though the moderate range keeps it accessible.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Intense fuel, pine, and citrus with earthy undertones. The fuel aroma is the defining characteristic -- sharp, pungent, and unmistakably OG. The pine character adds a fresh counterpoint, while the citrus limonene provides brightness. The overall profile is bold, complex, and deeply OG.
- **Taste:** Fuel and pine on the inhale, with citrus, earthy, and subtle spice notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and classic, with an intense fuel character that transitions into a pine-citrus finish.

SFV OG is a moderately challenging strain to grow. The OG genetics require experienced cultivation, particularly regarding nutrient management and humidity control. The strain is sensitive to overfeeding and prone to powdery mildew in high-humidity environments. The moderate flowering time and manageable height are practical advantages. The quality and aromatic intensity of the harvest generally compensate for the growing challenges.
