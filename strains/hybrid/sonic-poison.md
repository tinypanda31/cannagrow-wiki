# Sonic Poison

![Sonic Poison photo](/uploads/images/strains/sonic-poison.jpg)

## Overview

Sonic Poison is a sativa-dominant hybrid strain of unknown genetics, widely recognized for its energetic and focused effects and its distinctive citrus, sweet, and spicy aromatic profile. While the exact parent lineage remains undocumented, Sonic Poison's effects and terpene expression suggest genetics rooted in the sativa-dominant tradition, possibly involving Haze, landrace sativa, or other cerebral-forward genetic families. The strain earns its name from the fast-acting, energizing quality of its effects, which tend to deliver a quick, stimulating mental lift that is well-suited for daytime use and productive activities. Sonic Poison's mysterious genetics, combined with its reliably uplifting effects and bright, citrus-forward aromatic profile, make it a cultivar of interest to both consumers seeking functional stimulation and breeders exploring sativa genetics.
