# Sour Apple

![Sour Apple](/images/strains/sour-apple.png)

## Overview

Sour Apple is a sativa-dominant hybrid carrying the lineage of the legendary Sour Diesel, crossed with unknown apple-scented genetics. The Sour Diesel parent contributes its iconic fuel-diesel character, sativa vigor, and energetic cerebral effects that made it one of the most recognizable and beloved strains in cannabis history. The unknown apple-scented parent brings bright green apple sweetness, crisp citrus notes, and a sharpening of the overall aromatic profile. The result is a cultivar that delivers a distinctive diesel-apple-citrus aroma where the pungent, sharp diesel base is brightened by crisp green apple and fresh citrus accents. Its sativa-dominant effects are energetic and creative, making it well-suited for daytime use, artistic activities, and social engagement. The moderate potency in the 16-21% THC range provides enough strength for meaningful effects while remaining approachable for most users.

## Genetics & Lineage

Sour Apple is a two-way cross:

- **Sour Diesel** -- A legendary sativa-dominant hybrid of debated origin, widely believed to involve Chemdawg 91 and a Northern Lights or Super Skunk selection. Originating on the U.S. East Coast in the early 1990s, Sour Diesel became one of the most iconic and culturally significant strains in cannabis history. Known for its pungent fuel-diesel aroma, energetic cerebral effects, and sativa-dominant structure. Contributes the dominant fuel character, sativa vigor, and energetic creative effects.
- **Unknown apple-scented genetics** -- The second parent carries apple-scented genetics of undocumented origin. The phenotypic expression suggests a cultivar with crisp green apple sweetness and bright citrus character, possibly related to apple-scented selections that have appeared in various breeding programs.

**Parent lineage:** Sour Diesel x unknown apple-scented genetics

Sour Apple was bred to combine the iconic fuel-diesel character of Sour Diesel with bright green apple sweetness. The cross takes the pungent, sharp diesel base that made Sour Diesel legendary and adds a crisp, fresh apple character that creates a distinctive and recognizable aroma profile. The sativa-dominant structure and energetic effects are inherited primarily from the Sour Diesel parent, while the apple character adds aromatic complexity and a sharpening of the overall profile.

## Effects & Experience

Sour Apple is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic uplift** -- The defining characteristic; a noticeable increase in energy and mental alertness suitable for daytime activities
- **Creative engagement** -- Enhanced creative thinking and idea generation, frequently cited by artistic users
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that is cheerful and motivating
- **Focus and clarity** -- Mental sharpness that supports task-oriented activities
- **Social engagement** -- Some users report increased sociability and conversational energy

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 16-21% THC range delivers effects that are engaging and stimulating while remaining approachable for most users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Diesel, apple, and citrus with sharp, pungent character. The caryophyllene-dominant fuel base from Sour Diesel is brightened by crisp green apple and fresh citrus notes. The combination is distinctive, with the diesel and apple characters both clearly present.
- **Taste:** Diesel and apple on the inhale, with citrus and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The diesel character is prominent, with the green apple sweetness adding a sharp, fresh counterpoint.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Mild anxiety at higher doses, more likely due to the sativa-dominant genetics
- Restlessness if used late in the day
- Dizziness at elevated doses
- Increased heart rate, particularly in inexperienced users
- Difficulty sleeping if used in the evening

Sour Apple is a moderately manageable strain with the sativa-dominant structure that requires attention to height management. The moderate flowering time is favorable for a sativa-leaning cultivar. The open canopy promotes good airflow. The sativa stretch requires training to manage indoors, but the structure is less extreme than pure sativas. The diesel-apple terpene profile is best developed under stable, warm conditions with careful curing to preserve both the fuel and apple characters.
