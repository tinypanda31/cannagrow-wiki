# Strawberry Banana

![Strawberry Banana](/images/strains/strawberry-banana.png)

## Overview

Strawberry Banana is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Bubblegum with Banana, combining two fruit-forward genetics to produce a cultivar with a remarkably sweet, berry-and-banana aromatic profile. The Bubblegum parent (originating from Indiana and later refined in the Netherlands) contributes sweet berry character, balanced effects, and resilient growing genetics. The Banana parent (related to Banana Kush, itself a cross of OG Kush and an unknown banana-scented selection) brings dense tropical banana sweetness, indica-dominant relaxation, and heavy resin production. The result is a cultivar with a distinctive strawberry-banana-sweet aroma that closely resembles a fruit smoothie, paired with relaxing body effects that are well-suited for evening use. The indica-dominant genetics provide comfortable physical calm that progresses into gentle sedation at higher doses. Its intensely fruity character and approachable effects make Strawberry Banana broadly appealing to consumers seeking dessert-forward strains, while its moderate growing profile rewards cultivators with resinous, aromatic flowers.

## Genetics & Lineage

Strawberry Banana is a two-way cross:

- **Bubblegum** -- A hybrid strain originating from Indiana in the 1970s-1980s, later refined and stabilized by Dutch breeders. Known for sweet berry character, balanced effects, and resilient growing genetics. Bubblegum was one of the earliest fruity-forward strains and has been a consistent favorite among consumers seeking sweet, approachable cultivars. Contributes the sweet berry aroma, balanced genetics, and resilient growing character.
- **Banana** -- A cultivar related to Banana Kush (OG Kush x an unknown banana-scented selection), known for dense tropical banana sweetness, indica-dominant relaxation, and heavy resin production. Contributes the ripe banana fruit character, body relaxation genetics, and indica structure.

**Parent lineage:** Bubblegum x Banana

Strawberry Banana was bred to combine the sweet berry character of Bubblegum with the tropical banana sweetness of the Banana lineage. The resulting cultivar inherits the best of both fruit-forward parents: a remarkably smoothie-like strawberry-banana aroma paired with relaxing indica-dominant body effects. The cross amplifies the fruity character from both parents while adding the physical calming genetics from the Banana side, creating a strain that is both aromatically distinctive and therapeutically useful.

## Effects & Experience

Strawberry Banana is widely reported to produce:

- **Relaxing body effects** -- The defining characteristic; comfortable physical calm that progresses through the body, ideal for evening use
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental uplift that precedes the body relaxation
- **Stress relief** -- The body relaxation is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Mood elevation** -- A happy, positive mood driven by the sweet terpene profile
- **Sleep support** -- At higher doses, gentle sedation that supports sleep onset

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The 18-23% THC range delivers potent effects that build into comfortable body relaxation.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Strawberry, banana, and sweet with fruity smoothie-like character. The myrcene-dominant banana base is sweetened by strawberry-berry notes from limonene, creating a remarkably fruit smoothie-like aroma. The sweetness is prominent and approachable.
- **Taste:** Strawberry and banana on the inhale, with sweet and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and fruit-forward, with both berry and banana characters clearly present.
