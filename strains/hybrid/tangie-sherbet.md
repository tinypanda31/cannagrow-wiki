# Tangie Sherbet

![Tangie Sherbet photo](/uploads/images/strains/tangie-sherbet.jpg)

## Overview

Tangie Sherbet is a balanced hybrid created by crossing Tangie with Sherbert. This strain brings together the bright, citrus-forward character of Tangie (itself a phenotype selection of Tangerine Dream, which crosses G13, Afghan Skunk, and an unknown citrus parent) with the sweet, dessert-like quality of Sherbert (a phenotype of Sunset Sherbet, which descends from the Cookie Family genetics). The result is a cultivar with a vibrant citrus-sweet-berry aroma, effects that transition from uplifting cerebral energy to comfortable body relaxation, and a growing profile that is manageable for growers with some experience. Tangie Sherbet represents the modern trend in cannabis breeding that combines fruit-forward flavor genetics with potent, well-balanced hybrid effects. The strain appeals to users who want the best of both worlds -- the bright, energizing citrus character of Tangie and the sweet, comforting dessert quality of Sherbert -- in a single, cohesive experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

Tangie Sherbet is a two-way cross:

- **Tangie** -- A citrus-forward phenotype selection of Tangerine Dream (G13 x Afghan Skunk x unknown citrus), known for intense tangerine-citrus aroma, uplifting effects, and sativa-leaning character
- **Sherbert** -- A phenotype of Sunset Sherbet (Pink Panties x unknown), known for sweet, dessert-like flavor, balanced effects, and Cookie Family genetics

**Parent lineage:** Tangie x Sherbert

The Tangie parent contributes the bright citrus genetics and uplifting sativa-leaning energy, while the Sherbert parent brings the sweet, dessert-like flavor and balanced hybrid structure from the influential Cookie Family lineage. This pairing creates a strain that delivers a two-stage experience -- the initial cerebral lift from the Tangie genetics transitions into the comfortable body relaxation from the Sherbert side. The combination of citrus and sweet-berry genetics produces one of the most complex and layered flavor profiles among modern hybrids.

## Effects & Experience

Tangie Sherbet is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting initial cerebral lift** -- The Tangie genetics deliver an initial bright, cheerful mental energy that is engaging and social
- **Transition to body relaxation** -- As the experience progresses, the Sherbert genetics introduce a comfortable body calm
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, happy mood that carries through both the cerebral and body phases
- **Stress relief** -- The combined effect provides stress reduction across both mental and physical dimensions
- **Balanced versatility** -- Suitable for afternoon and early evening use, transitioning from active to relaxed

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The balanced hybrid genetics produce effects that are noticeable and evolving, with the cerebral phase giving way to body relaxation.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The initial cerebral effects may be stimulating, while the later body effects can be sedating at higher doses.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus-forward with sweet and berry undertones. The limonene-dominant profile gives it a bright, tangerine-like citrus aroma that is immediately noticeable. The Sherbert genetics add a sweet, berry-rich undertone that rounds out the citrus brightness with dessert-like depth. The overall aroma is layered and complex, with the citrus character being most prominent but the sweet-berry foundation clearly present.
- **Taste:** Citrus on the inhale, with sweet berry and dessert-like notes on the exhale. The tangerine-citrus flavor from Tangie is bright and clean on the initial taste, while the Sherbert inheritance brings a sweet, berry-rich exhale that adds depth and complexity. The transition from citrus to sweet berry on the palate mirrors the effect progression.
