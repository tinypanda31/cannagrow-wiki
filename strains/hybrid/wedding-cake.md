# Wedding Cake (Pink Cookies)

![Wedding Cake](/images/strains/wedding-cake.jpg)

## Overview

Wedding Cake, also known as Pink Cookies, is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Girl Scout Cookies with Cherry Pie. This cross places Wedding Cake firmly within the Cookies family of genetics, inheriting the sweet, dessert-like flavor profile and high THC potency that define that lineage. The strain is known for its sweet-vanilla-earthy aroma, relaxing yet euphoric effects, and moderate-to-high THC content. Wedding Cake has gained popularity among consumers seeking the Cookies flavor profile with a more indica-leaning effect profile, making it well-suited to evening use.

## Genetics & Lineage

Wedding Cake is a two-way cross within the Cookies family:

- **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** -- The legendary Bay Area hybrid (OG Kush x Durban Poison), contributing high THC, sweetness, and the Cookies terpene profile
- **Cherry Pie** -- A hybrid strain (Granddaddy Purple x Durban Poison F1), contributing cherry sweetness, indica relaxation, and purple coloration potential

**Parent lineage:** Girl Scout Cookies x Cherry Pie

Wedding Cake was developed within the broader Cookies breeding ecosystem. The Cherry Pie parent brings Granddaddy Purple genetics (and thus purple coloration potential) alongside Durban Poison sativa influence, while the GSC parent contributes the signature Cookies sweetness and high potency. The result is an indica-dominant hybrid that combines the best of both lineages: dessert-like sweetness, relaxing body effects, and euphoric mental lift.

## Effects & Experience

Wedding Cake is widely reported to produce:

- **Relaxation** -- A deep, comfortable physical relaxation inherited from the indica-dominant genetics
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental euphoria from the GSC lineage
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical stress
- **Mild sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness at higher doses, making it well-suited to evening use

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC content delivers potent effects best suited to experienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, vanilla, and earthy with subtle cherry and spicy undertones. The aroma is dessert-like and indulgent, with a noticeable vanilla sweetness that gives the strain its "Wedding Cake" name.
- **Taste:** Sweet vanilla on the inhale, with earthy and slightly cherry notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and dessert-forward, appealing to users who appreciate sweet, indulgent cannabis profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience with Cookies-family sensitivity |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact structure, inherits GSC compactness |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry, Mediterranean-like climate |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Inherits some Cookies-family sensitivity to nutrient imbalances. Responds well to topping and LST. Purple coloration may appear with cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering, inherited from the GDP line via Cherry Pie. |

Wedding Cake is moderately challenging to grow. It is somewhat more forgiving than pure GSC but still requires attention to nutrient management and environmental conditions. The compact structure is an advantage for indoor growing. The potential for purple coloration adds visual appeal when growing conditions support it. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
