# XJ-13

![Xj 13](/images/strains/xj-13.jpeg)

## Overview

XJ-13 is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by crossing Jack Herer with G13 Haze. The name references both parent strains -- "X" for the crossover, "J" for Jack Herer, and "13" for G13. This strain combines the legendary cerebral energy and pine character of Jack Herer with the potent, intense genetics of G13 Haze, resulting in a cultivar with a pine-citrus-earthy aroma and effects that are energetic, creative, and focused. XJ-13 has gained recognition as a daytime strain that delivers clean cerebral energy without excessive stimulation, making it suitable for productive and creative activities.

## Genetics & Lineage

XJ-13 is a two-way cross of influential sativa genetics:

- **Jack Herer** -- The legendary sativa-dominant hybrid named after the cannabis activist and author, with genetics including Haze, Northern Lights, and Shiva Skunk. Contributes classic cerebral energy, pine aroma, creative effects, and some of the most recognized sativa genetics in cannabis history.
- **G13 Haze** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid combining the mysterious G13 genetics (rumored to have origins in a U.S. government cultivation program) with Haze (Colombian x Mexican x Thai x South Indian). Contributes potency, intensity, and classic Haze cerebral structure.

**Parent lineage:** Jack Herer x G13 Haze

XJ-13 emerged from the U.S. cannabis breeding scene, where breeders sought to combine two of the most respected sativa-dominant genetics. The cross brings together the approachable, creative energy of Jack Herer with the intense, potent character of G13 Haze. The name "XJ-13" is a direct reference to the parentage: X (cross) + J (Jack Herer) + 13 (G13). This combination creates a strain that amplifies the cerebral, creative, and focused effects of both parents while maintaining a manageable THC range and a practical flowering time.

## Effects & Experience

XJ-13 is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic focus** -- A clear, focused cerebral energy that promotes productivity and mental engagement
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration, characteristic of both parent strains
- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A cheerful, positive mood lift that is bright and engaging
- **Mental clarity** -- Clean-headed alertness without the racing thoughts sometimes associated with sativa strains
- **Light body calm** -- A subtle physical calm that accompanies the cerebral effects without sedation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The moderate THC range and sativa-leaning effects make XJ-13 suitable for daytime use and accessible to both newer and experienced consumers.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Pine, citrus, and earthy with herbal undertones. The pine aroma inherited from Jack Herer is the dominant character, while the citrus terpinolene adds brightness. The earthy base provides depth. The overall profile is fresh, clean, and natural.
- **Taste:** Pine and citrus on the inhale, with earthy, herbal, and subtle spice notes on the exhale. The flavor is crisp and refreshing, with a clean pine character that transitions into a citrus-earthy finish.
