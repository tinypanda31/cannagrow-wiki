# Yellow OG

![Yellow OG photo](/uploads/images/strains/yellow-og.jpg)

## Overview

Yellow OG is an indica-dominant hybrid strain that represents a specific phenotype selection within the OG Kush family, one of the most influential genetic lineages in modern cannabis history. The strain is known for its fuel, citrus, and earthy aroma profile, carrying the characteristic OG Kush diesel punch with a noticeable citrus brightness that distinguishes it from other OG phenotype selections. The effects are balanced between cerebral engagement and body relaxation, transitioning progressively toward body-heavy calm as the experience develops. Yellow OG inherits the legendary status of the OG Kush genetics and the high-potency, complex terpene expression that has made the OG family a cornerstone of modern cannabis breeding. The strain's balanced character and distinctive citrus-fuel profile make it appealing to both consumers and cultivators.

## Genetics & Lineage

Yellow OG is a phenotype selection within the OG Kush family:

- **OG Kush** -- The legendary Southern California cultivar that defined West Coast cannabis culture. OG Kush genetics are known for their fuel, earthy, and pine aroma with potent, balanced effects. OG Kush has served as a parent to hundreds of modern cultivars and remains one of the most influential genetic families in cannabis breeding. The specific OG Kush parent in Yellow OG contributes the fuel-forward character and the indica-dominant structure.
- **Phenotype Selection** -- Yellow OG represents a specific phenotype selection within the broader OG Kush family, distinguished by its elevated limonene expression (producing the citrus character) and its balanced-to-body effect profile. The "Yellow" designation likely references the phenotype's tendency toward yellowish-green coloration or its citrus-yellow terpene expression.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush phenotype selection (Yellow OG cut)

Yellow OG was selected from the OG Kush family for its distinctive citrus-fuel profile and balanced effect character. Among the many OG Kush phenotype selections (including Tahoe OG, SFV OG, and others), Yellow OG stands out for its elevated limonene content, which produces a noticeable citrus brightness against the fuel-earthy OG foundation. The indica-dominant character reflects the OG Kush family's typical body-forward expression, while the citrus contribution adds a distinctive aromatic dimension. This phenotype selection represents the ongoing exploration of OG Kush genetic diversity and the pursuit of novel expressions within this foundational lineage.

## Effects & Experience

Yellow OG is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced cerebral onset** -- Initial mental engagement and mild euphoria that is cheerful and engaging
- **Progressive body relaxation** -- As the cerebral effects settle, a comfortable body calm develops that intensifies over time
- **Mood elevation** -- A positive mood lift that accompanies the cerebral effects, promoting optimism
- **Stress relief** -- The combination of mental engagement and body relaxation is frequently cited for stress reduction
- **Functional versatility** -- Effects that are suitable for both daytime and evening use, depending on dose

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The THC range delivers a meaningful experience that transitions from balanced to body-heavy, making it versatile for different times of day.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fuel and citrus with earthy undertones. The OG Kush fuel character is prominent, but the Yellow OG phenotype shifts the balance toward a brighter, citrus-forward expression. The earthy myrcene base provides depth, while the citrus limonene adds a refreshing brightness. The overall aroma is layered, with fuel pungency, citrus brightness, and earthy warmth.
- **Taste:** Fuel and citrus on the inhale, with earthy and slightly piney notes on the exhale. The flavor is robust and full-bodied, with the fuel character carrying through the experience and the citrus finish providing a refreshing contrast to the heavier earth tones.
