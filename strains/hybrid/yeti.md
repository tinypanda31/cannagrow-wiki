# Yeti

![Yeti photo](/uploads/images/strains/yeti.jpg)

## Overview

Yeti is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing the legendary Northern Lights with an unknown heavy indica genetics. The strain's name is a direct reference to its imposing, resin-coated bud structure and the heavy, almost overwhelming body relaxation it delivers. The Northern Lights parent contributes its famous Afghani indica genetics, fast flowering time, and proven reliability, while the unknown heavy indica parent amplifies the body effects, resin production, and overall potency. The result is a strain with an earthy-pine-sweet aroma, deeply sedating body relaxation, and a growing profile that benefits from the Northern Lights' legendary ease of cultivation. Yeti is valued by users seeking heavy indica effects -- the kind that deliver profound physical relaxation and sleep support -- and by growers who want the reliability of Northern Lights genetics combined with an extra dimension of potency and resin production.

## Genetics & Lineage

Yeti is a two-way cross:

- **Northern Lights** -- The legendary Afghani indica (pure Afghani landrace, stabilized by Sensi Seeds and others), contributing deep body relaxation, fast flowering, compact structure, earthy-sweet aroma, and proven reliability
- **Unknown Heavy Indica** -- The complementary parent has not been publicly documented, but the effects suggest a heavy, sedating indica genetics that amplifies the Northern Lights body effects

**Parent lineage:** Northern Lights x Unknown Heavy Indica

The Northern Lights parent brings one of the most trusted and proven indica genetics in cannabis history, while the unknown heavy indica parent pushes the body relaxation and potency to a higher level. The combination creates a strain that delivers the reliability and ease of Northern Lights cultivation with an amplified sedating effect that appeals to heavy indica enthusiasts. The unknown parent genetics contribute to the strain's heavy, almost overwhelming body character that earned it the "Yeti" name.

The pinene adds a fresh pine character that brightens the profile, while caryophyllene provides spicy warmth.

## Effects & Experience

Yeti is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- The defining characteristic; a deep, powerful physical calm that spreads through the entire body
- **Sedation** -- Pronounced sedating effects, particularly at moderate to higher doses
- **Mental quieting** -- A calming of mental activity that accompanies the heavy body effects
- **Sleep support** -- One of the most commonly cited strains for sleep onset, particularly among users with sleep difficulties
- **Couch-lock** -- The heavy indica character can produce noticeable couch-lock at moderate doses

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The heavy indica genetics deliver potent body relaxation that is best suited to evening use and experienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The heavy sedating effects may significantly impair coordination and are not suitable for any activities requiring alertness.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy and pine-forward with sweet undertones. The myrcene-dominant profile gives it a deep, earthy aroma with noticeable pine freshness from the pinene content. The sweet undertones from the Northern Lights inheritance round out the profile and add a comforting quality. The overall aroma is classic heavy indica -- deep, resinous, and immediately recognizable.
- **Taste:** Earthy and pine on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The earthy flavor is prominent and grounding, while the pine character adds freshness and the sweet exhale provides a comforting finish.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Muscle spasms** -- The indica relaxation is reported to ease severe muscle tension and spasticity

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness, often pronounced even at moderate doses
- Dizziness with elevated consumption
- Impaired coordination during the experience, often significant
- Grogginess the following morning with higher evening doses
- Couch-lock at moderate doses, limiting mobility

Yeti is a straightforward strain to grow, primarily due to the Northern Lights genetics in its lineage. The indica-dominant compact structure and moderate flowering time make it suitable for both beginners and experienced growers. The unknown heavy indica parent may contribute additional resin production, making the buds visually striking and heavily coated. The yields are consistent with quality indica genetics, and the Northern Lights inheritance ensures reliability and resilience.
