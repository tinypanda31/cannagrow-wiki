# Zkittlez

![Zkittlez](/images/strains/zkittlez.jpg)

## Overview

Zkittlez is an indica-dominant hybrid cultivar created by crossing Grape Ape, Grapefruit, and an undisclosed third parent. Developed by the team at 3rd Gen Family and Terp Hogz, Zkittlez quickly gained recognition for its extraordinarily fruity-candy-tropical flavor profile that closely resembles the popular candy, and its calming full-body indica effects. The strain won Best Indica at the 2016 Emerald Cup, cementing its reputation as a quality indica-dominant cultivar. Zkittlez has since become a foundational genetics in flavor-focused breeding programs, contributing to numerous fruity and candy-flavored derivative crosses.

## Genetics & Lineage

Zkittlez is a three-way cross:

- **Grape Ape** -- An indica-dominant strain (Mendocino Purps x Skunk x Afghani) known for grape flavor, purple coloration, and deep relaxation
- **Grapefruit** -- A sativa-dominant strain contributing citrus brightness and fruity character
- **Undisclosed third parent** -- The breeders have not revealed the complete genetic composition, adding an element of mystery to the strain's full profile

**Parent lineage:** Grape Ape x Grapefruit x Undisclosed

Zkittlez was developed by 3rd Gen Family and Terp Hogz, breeding teams known for their focus on exceptional terpene profiles and flavor. The strain was selected from extensive pheno-hunting to find the most intensely fruity and candy-flowered expression. The undisclosed third parent genetics remain a closely guarded secret, which is common in competitive breeding programs. Zkittlez has since been used to create numerous derivative crosses, including Zkittlez Cream, Zkittlez Auto, and many other "Zkittlez" branded cultivars.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Share | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Humulene | 20-35% | Primary terpene; earthy, woody, hoppy character -- notably high compared to most strains |
| Caryophyllene | 15-25% | Spicy, peppery depth adding complexity to the fruit profile |
| Limonene | 15-25% | Significant presence; citrus notes enhancing the fruity character |
| Myrcene | 10-15% | Earthy, herbal base |
| Linalool | 3-8% | Floral, lavender notes contributing to the candy sweetness |

The humulene-caryophyllene-limonene combination is unusual -- humulene as the dominant terpene is less common than myrcene or limonene dominance. This unique profile contributes to Zkittlez's distinctive candy-fruit character.

## Effects & Experience

Zkittlez is widely reported to produce:

- **Calming relaxation** -- A comfortable full-body calm that eases physical tension
- **Mild euphoria** -- A pleasant mood lift that accompanies the body relaxation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical stress
- **Gentle sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness at moderate to higher doses, making it well-suited to evening use

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The moderate THC range and calming effects make Zkittlez accessible to a wide range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Fruity, candy, and tropical with grape and citrus undertones. The aroma is intensely sweet and fruity, closely resembling fruit-flavored candy. It is one of the most candy-like strains in cannabis.
- **Taste:** Fruity candy on the inhale, with tropical, grape, and slightly citrus notes on the exhale. The flavor is sweet and indulgent, closely mirroring the aroma and delivering a full candy experience.

- Dry eyes

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions. Purple coloration may appear with cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering, inherited from the Grape Ape line. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Responds well to topping and LST. The compact structure is an advantage for indoor growing. The Grape Ape heritage can bring purple coloration in the right conditions. |

Zkittlez is moderately challenging to grow. Its indica-dominant compact structure makes it suitable for indoor spaces. The potential for purple coloration adds visual appeal when growing conditions support it (cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering). Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
