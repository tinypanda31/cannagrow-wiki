# 9 Pound Hammer

![9 Pound Hammer](/images/strains/9-pound-hammer.jpg)

## Overview

9 Pound Hammer is a pure indica cultivar developed from a three-way cross of Gooberry, Snipe, and Sweet Skunk. Created by breeder Josh D at Serious Seeds, this strain earned its name from the heavy, hammer-like body effects it produces. The combination of grape and candy-like sweetness in its aroma profile, paired with deeply sedating indica effects, has made 9 Pound Hammer a favorite among consumers seeking powerful nighttime relaxation. Its straightforward growing profile and dense, resinous buds have also made it popular among cultivators.

## Genetics & Lineage

9 Pound Hammer is a three-way indica cross combining distinct genetics:

- **Gooberry** -- An indica-dominant strain with grape and berry characteristics
- **Snipe** -- A potent indica cultivar contributing heavy body effects
- **Sweet Skunk** -- An indica-dominant strain adding sweetness and skunky depth to the flavor profile

**Parent lineage:** Gooberry x Snipe x Sweet Skunk

The blend of these three indica-heavy parents produces a strain that consistently expresses grape-forward aromas, dense bud structure, and deeply relaxing effects across multiple phenotypes.

## Effects & Experience

9 Pound Hammer is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A powerful, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and releases physical tension
- **Deep sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that typically leads to sleep, making this strain best suited for late evening or nighttime use
- **Mild euphoria** -- A gentle mental lift that accompanies the heavy physical effects
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Notable increase in hunger commonly reported

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The sedating effects can be overwhelming for inexperienced users or those with low tolerance.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Do not operate vehicles or machinery after consumption.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

9 Pound Hammer presents a distinctly sweet and fruity sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced grape and grape candy sweetness with earthy, skunky undertones. The aroma is noticeably sweet and can fill a room when buds are ground.
- **Taste:** Sweet grape and candy-like flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly skunky notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who prefer sweeter profiles.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Insomnia and sleep support** -- The powerful sedating quality makes 9 Pound Hammer a frequent choice for individuals with significant sleep difficulties
- **Pain management** -- Deep physical relaxation is commonly cited for relief from chronic pain, muscle spasms, and tension
- **Stress reduction** -- The heavy calming effect is reported to reduce mental and physical stress
- **Appetite loss** -- THC-dominant profile is associated with increased appetite

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Heavy drowsiness and couch-lock
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for impaired coordination during the experience
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less common than with sativa-dominant strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy -- resilient and forgiving, suitable for beginner growers |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500-600 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; performs well in both environments |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold, pests, and common growing issues. Dense buds benefit from adequate airflow to prevent bud rot in high-humidity conditions. |

9 Pound Hammer is considered an easy strain to grow. Its indica structure remains compact, and the genetics are resilient to common cultivation mistakes. The strain responds well to basic training techniques like topping and LST. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
