# Animal Mints

![Animal Mints](/images/strains/animal-mints.png)

## Overview

Animal Mints is an indica-dominant cultivar resulting from the cross of Animal Cookies and Mints. Combining the heavy, sedating genetics of the Cookies family with the sharp, minty terpene profile of Mints, this strain delivers a potent and complex experience. The fuel-forward aroma is balanced by sweet mint and earthy undertones, while the effects lean heavily toward deep body relaxation. Animal Mints has gained recognition among experienced cannabis consumers for its high THC content and long-lasting physical effects.

## Genetics & Lineage

Animal Mints combines two well-regarded modern genetics:

- **Animal Cookies** -- An indica-dominant hybrid derived from Girl Scout Cookies crossed with Fire OG, known for its heavy body effects and sweet, earthy flavor
- **Mints** -- A potent cultivar recognized for its distinctive minty, fuel-forward terpene profile and high THC content

**Parent lineage:** Animal Cookies x Mints

The cross merges the Cookies family's sedating indica genetics with the sharp, aromatic profile of Mints, producing a strain that captures the best of both parent lines. The result is a cultivar with elevated THC levels, complex aromatics, and deeply relaxing effects.

## Effects & Experience

Animal Mints is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent body high** -- A deep, heavy physical sensation that envelops the body in relaxation and eases muscular tension
- **Euphoria** -- An initial wave of mental uplift that transitions into a more subdued, body-focused calm
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that builds over time, making this strain well-suited for evening or nighttime use
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported melting away of mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC range makes Animal Mints a potent strain that may overwhelm inexperienced users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Animal Mints presents a complex and distinctive sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Fuel and mint dominate the initial aroma, with sweet and earthy undertones developing as the buds are broken apart. The caryophyllene-heavy profile produces a pungent, diesel-like scent tempered by cooling mint notes.
- **Taste:** Mint and fuel on inhalation, with sweet, creamy, and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is complex and layered, with limonene providing citrus brightness that balances the heavier fuel character.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential anxiety or paranoia in THC-sensitive individuals due to the elevated THC range
- Couch-lock at high doses, limiting mobility

| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |

Animal Mints rewards experienced growers who can manage the plant's nutrient demands and maintain optimal environmental conditions. The strain benefits from training techniques such as topping and low-stress training to maximize yield and light penetration. Resin production is heavy, making this strain well-suited for extraction. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
