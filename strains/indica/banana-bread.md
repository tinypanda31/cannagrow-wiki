# Banana Bread

![Banana Bread](/images/strains/banana-bread.png)

## Overview

Banana Bread is an indica-dominant cultivar created by crossing Banana OG with an unknown indica parent. Named for its pronounced banana and sweet baked-good aroma, Banana Bread combines the tropical fruit character of Banana OG with the heavy body relaxation of classic indica genetics. The result is a strain that delivers a soothing, body-focused experience wrapped in a sweet, banana-forward terpene profile dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and limonene.

## Genetics & Lineage

Banana Bread is a two-way cross combining documented and undocumented genetics:

- **Banana OG** -- Also known as "Banana Kush," an indica-dominant cultivar believed to be a cross of Ghost OG and Skunk Haze (though some sources cite different parentage). Known for its pronounced tropical banana aroma, bright yellow-green coloration, and relaxing body effects. Banana OG has established itself as a recognizable genetics in the indica market.
- **Unknown Indica** -- The second parent is an undocumented indica variety that contributes additional body relaxation, structural compactness, and earthy-sweet undertones to complement the Banana OG fruit character.

**Parent lineage:** Banana OG x unknown indica

This cross was designed to amplify the sweet banana character of Banana OG while anchoring it with the deep body effects of traditional indica genetics. The unknown parent likely contributes the earthy-sweet baked-good quality that gives Banana Bread its name, suggesting an indica with earthy and sweet undertones.

## Effects & Experience

Banana Bread is widely reported to produce:

- **Relaxing body effects** -- A soothing, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and eases physical tension without overwhelming heaviness
- **Mild sedation** -- A gentle progression into drowsiness that is noticeable but not debilitating, making this strain suitable for early evening use
- **Mood elevation** -- A mild mental uplift that accompanies the physical relaxation, contributing to an overall sense of well-being
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the body effects settle in

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The moderate THC range makes Banana Bread approachable for users of varying experience levels while still providing satisfying relaxation.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Banana Bread presents a sweet, tropical sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced banana and sweet aromas with earthy undertones. The Banana OG parent is immediately apparent in the unmistakable tropical fruit character, while the unknown indica contributes an earthy, slightly baked-good sweetness that gives the strain its name. The overall aroma is sweet and inviting, with a dessert-like quality.
- **Taste:** Sweet banana flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly nutty notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, with limonene providing faint citrus accents that enhance the tropical fruit character. The finish carries a faint warmth reminiscent of freshly baked banana bread.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires standard environment control and attention to humidity management for dense buds |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact indica-dominant structure with moderate stretch during early flower |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with consistent temperatures. The Banana OG genetics favor a slightly warmer environment for optimal tropical fruit expression. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense buds require adequate air circulation to prevent bud rot in high-humidity environments. The indica-dominant structure benefits from support during late flower as colas develop weight. |

Banana Bread benefits from attentive growing practices. The strain responds well to topping and low-stress training to manage its compact structure and improve light distribution to lower bud sites. Consistent nutrient management and humidity control are important for producing clean, well-cured buds. The strain produces resinous buds with solid trichome coverage suitable for both flower and extraction purposes. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
