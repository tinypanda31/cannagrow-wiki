# Biscotti

![Biscotti](/images/strains/biscotti.png)

## Overview

Biscotti is an indica-dominant cultivar resulting from the cross of Gelato #25 and South Florida OG. Named for its sweet, cookie-like aroma, Biscotti has quickly gained recognition among cannabis enthusiasts for its potent effects and complex flavor profile. The strain combines the dessert-like sweetness of the Gelato family with the fuel-forward punch of South Florida OG genetics, producing a cultivar that delivers heavy sedation and a richly layered sensory experience. Biscotti is widely regarded as an evening strain due to its deeply relaxing properties.

## Genetics & Lineage

Biscotti merges two highly regarded modern genetics:

- **Gelato #25** -- Also known as "Gelato 25" or "Larry Bird," a phenotype of the original Gelato cross (Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies). Known for its sweet, dessert-like aroma and balanced effects.
- **South Florida OG** -- A potent indica-dominant cultivar from the South Florida breeding scene, recognized for its fuel-forward terpene profile and heavy body effects

**Parent lineage:** Gelato #25 x South Florida OG

The cross brings together the Cookie family's sweet aromatic complexity with the raw potency of South Florida OG, resulting in a strain that is both flavorful and deeply sedating.

## Effects & Experience

Biscotti is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy sedation** -- A deep, body-locking sensation that progresses from physical relaxation to pronounced drowsiness
- **Euphoria** -- An initial mental uplift that quickly merges with the body-heavy effects
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported melting away of mental tension and worry
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Notable increase in hunger, particularly as the effects mature

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC range and indica-dominant genetics produce intense effects that are best reserved for evening use.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Biscotti presents a rich and complex sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet, nutty, and earthy with fuel undertones. The Gelato inheritance provides a dessert-like sweetness that is balanced by the diesel punch of South Florida OG. The cured buds carry a biscuit-like aroma that is warm and inviting.
- **Taste:** Sweet and nutty on inhalation, with earthy and fuel notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, with limonene adding citrus brightness that prevents the profile from becoming one-dimensional.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential anxiety or paranoia in THC-sensitive individuals
- Couch-lock at high doses, limiting mobility

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to difficult -- requires experience with nutrient-heavy cultivars and environment control |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 350-450 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact structure with moderate stretch; benefits from training |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor preferred for optimal terpene expression and yield consistency |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense, resinous buds require excellent air circulation to prevent bud rot. Sensitive to overfeeding. |

Biscotti rewards attentive growing. The strain benefits from controlled nutrient regimes, as it can be sensitive to overfeeding. Training techniques such as topping and low-stress training help manage the plant's structure and improve light penetration. The heavy resin production makes this strain well-suited for hash and extraction work. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
