# Black Domina

![Black Domina](/images/strains/black-domina.png)

## Overview

Black Domina is a deeply indica-dominant cultivar developed by Dutch Passion in the Netherlands. Despite sometimes being described as a simple cross, Black Domina is actually a complex polyhybrid combining Northern Lights, Afghan, Hash Plant, and White Widow genetics into a single stabilized indica line. The result is an exceptionally dark, resin-coated strain with a pronounced hashy aroma and effects so sedating that the name Domina aptly reflects its powerful, commanding nature. Black Domina is frequently used in hash production and is valued by cultivators for its straightforward growing profile and consistent expression.

## Genetics & Lineage

Black Domina is a multi-parent indica polyhybrid developed by Dutch breeders:

- **Northern Lights** -- Legendary Pacific Northwest indica refined in the Netherlands, contributing compact structure and deep relaxation
- **Afghan** -- Landrace indica from the Hindu Kush mountain region, providing resin production and hash-quality genetics
- **Hash Plant** -- A stabilized indica selected specifically for hash production, adding dense resin gland coverage
- **White Widow** -- Dutch-bred hybrid (Brazilian sativa x South Indian indica) contributing vigor and trichome density

**Parent lineage:** Northern Lights x Afghan x Hash Plant x White Widow

The combination of these four genetics into a single indica-dominant expression produces a strain that excels in resin production, hash-making quality, and deeply sedating effects. Dutch Passion stabilized the genetics to produce a consistent, reliable indica cultivar.

## Effects & Experience

Black Domina is widely reported to produce:

- **Deeply sedating body effects** -- A heavy, full-body sensation that progressively relaxes muscles and leads toward sleep
- **Physical calm** -- A grounding, calming effect on the body that releases tension and physical stress
- **Mild mental euphoria** -- A gentle mood lift that accompanies the physical relaxation without intense cerebral stimulation
- **Sleep induction** -- At moderate doses, a strong tendency toward drowsiness and sleep onset

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. While the sedating effects are pronounced, the moderate THC range makes Black Domina accessible to users with some cannabis experience.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Do not operate vehicles or machinery after consumption.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Black Domina presents a classic hash-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Deeply earthy and hashy with peppery and pine undertones. The aroma is pungent and reminiscent of traditional hashish, with a musky quality inherited from its Afghan and Hash Plant genetics.
- **Taste:** Earthy and hashy on inhalation, with peppery and pine notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and traditional, appealing to users who appreciate classic indica profiles over modern fruity or dessert-like cultivars.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Heavy drowsiness (undesirable during daytime hours)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential couch-lock limiting mobility
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less frequently reported than with sativa strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy -- resilient, fast-flowering, and forgiving of common mistakes |
| **Flowering Time** | 45-55 days (indoor) -- notably fast for an indica |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500-600 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica structure well-suited to indoor growing |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; performs well in both. Prefers a stable environment with moderate temperatures. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold, pests, and diseases. The Afghan and Hash Plant genetics contribute hardiness. Dense buds require adequate airflow to prevent bud rot. |

Black Domina is considered one of the easier indica strains to cultivate. Its fast flowering time (45-55 days) is notably short for a pure indica, and the plants are compact and resilient. The strain is particularly valued for hash production due to its heavy trichome coverage inherited from Hash Plant and White Widow genetics. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September.
