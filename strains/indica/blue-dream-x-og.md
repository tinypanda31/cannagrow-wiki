# Blue Dream x OG (Indica Phenotype)

![Blue Dream x OG photo](/uploads/images/strains/blue-dream-x-og.jpg)

## Overview

Blue Dream x OG is an indica-leaning hybrid cultivar resulting from the cross of Blue Dream and OG Kush. While Blue Dream is traditionally known as a sativa-dominant strain, specific indica-leaning phenotypes emerge when it is crossed with the deeply relaxing OG Kush genetics. This particular phenotype selection emphasizes the body-heavy, sedating qualities of OG Kush while retaining Blue Dream's signature berry sweetness, producing a well-rounded cultivar that transitions from initial euphoria into deep physical relaxation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Blue Dream x OG combines two legendary cultivars:

- **Blue Dream** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid originally from California, crossing Blueberry with Haze. Known for its berry aroma and balanced effects. Indica-leaning phenotypes are selectively bred to emphasize the Blueberry indica influence.
- **OG Kush** -- The foundational indica-dominant strain from Southern California, renowned for its fuel-earth aroma and deeply relaxing body effects

**Parent lineage:** Blue Dream x OG Kush

The cross captures Blue Dream's berry sweetness and OG Kush's fuel-forward depth, with phenotype selection favoring indica-dominant expressions that emphasize body relaxation over cerebral stimulation.

## Effects & Experience

Blue Dream x OG (indica phenotype) is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced onset then body-heavy transition** -- Initial mild euphoria and mental clarity that gradually gives way to deep physical relaxation
- **Body relaxation** -- A warming, full-body sensation that loosens muscles and eases physical tension
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the indica genetics take hold
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness at higher doses or as the experience matures

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The balanced onset makes this strain accessible to a wide range of users, while the body-heavy transition can produce strong sedation at higher doses.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Blue Dream x OG presents a layered sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Berry sweetness from the Blue Dream parent sits atop a foundation of fuel and pine from OG Kush. The result is a complex aroma that is simultaneously sweet and pungent, with earthy undertones.
- **Taste:** Berry and fuel on inhalation, with pine and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor transitions from sweet to savory, reflecting the hybrid nature of the cross.
