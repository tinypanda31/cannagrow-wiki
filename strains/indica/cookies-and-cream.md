# Cookies and Cream

![Cookies And Cream](/images/strains/cookies-and-cream.jpg)

## Overview

Cookies and Cream is an indica-leaning hybrid cultivar resulting from the cross of Starfighter and Girl Scout Cookies. True to its name, this strain delivers a sweet, vanilla-earthy flavor profile reminiscent of its dessert-inspired namesake. The effects balance initial euphoria with deep body relaxation, making it a versatile cultivar that appeals to a wide range of cannabis consumers. Cookies and Cream has established itself as a staple in the Cookies family of strains, carrying forward the genetic legacy of Girl Scout Cookies while adding the unique terpene contribution of Starfighter.

## Genetics & Lineage

Cookies and Cream combines two influential modern genetics:

- **Starfighter** -- A hybrid cultivar known for its unique terpene profile and contributions to several notable crosses. Starfighter brings earthy, sweet, and slightly floral characteristics to the cross.
- **Girl Scout Cookies (GSC)** -- One of the most influential strains in modern cannabis breeding, a cross of OG Kush and Durban Poison. Known for its sweet, earthy aroma and powerful euphoric effects

**Parent lineage:** Starfighter x Girl Scout Cookies

The cross merges Starfighter's unique aromatic complexity with the legendary Girl Scout Cookies genetics, producing a strain that captures the dessert-like sweetness and body relaxation that has made the Cookies family so popular.

## Effects & Experience

Cookies and Cream is widely reported to produce:

- **Euphoric body relaxation** -- An initial wave of mental euphoria that quickly merges with a warm, full-body relaxation sensation
- **Mood elevation** -- A noticeable lift in mood and sense of well-being during the early phase of the experience
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the indica genetics settle in
- **Mild sedation** -- Progressive calming that can lead to drowsiness at higher doses or as the experience matures

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The balanced euphoric onset makes this strain approachable for a wide range of users, while the body relaxation provides satisfying depth.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Cookies and Cream presents a sweet and inviting sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet vanilla and earthy notes dominate, with subtle spicy undertones from caryophyllene. The aroma is reminiscent of baked goods, with a creamy sweetness that is immediately recognizable.
- **Taste:** Sweet and vanilla-forward on inhalation, with earthy and slightly nutty notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, with limonene providing citrus accents that prevent the profile from becoming overly sweet.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours at higher doses)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential anxiety or paranoia in THC-sensitive individuals
- Mild hunger increase
