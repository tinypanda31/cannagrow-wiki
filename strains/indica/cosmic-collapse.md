# Cosmic Collapse

![Cosmic Collapse photo](/uploads/images/strains/cosmic-collapse.jpg)

## Overview

Cosmic Collapse is a pure indica cultivar sourced from unknown heavy indica genetics. The name reflects the strain's overwhelming physical impact -- an experience so deeply sedating it feels as though gravity itself has intensified. With a fuel, earthy, and pungent terpene profile dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and limonene, Cosmic Collapse delivers the most intense body-heavy experience in its class.

## Genetics & Lineage

Cosmic Collapse traces its origins to unknown heavy indica genetics:

- **Unknown Heavy Indica** -- The genetics are believed to derive from heavily sedating indica varieties selected for their profound body effects and pungent, fuel-forward terpene profile. The exact provenance is undocumented, but the strain's consistent expression of classic heavy indica traits -- deep earthy and fuel aromas, compact structure, and profoundly sedating effects -- points to genetics selected specifically for maximum physical impact. The unknown parentage may include traditional landrace varieties, stabilized modern crosses, or a carefully preserved phenotype identified for its exceptional sedating power.

**Parent lineage:** Unknown heavy indica genetics

The preservation of these genetics suggests a deliberate effort to maintain the raw sedating potency that characterized the most powerful traditional indica varieties. Unlike modern crosses that often blend potency with cerebral effects, Cosmic Collapse represents a singular focus on deep, body-heavy relaxation.

## Effects & Experience

Cosmic Collapse is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep sedation** -- A profound, body-locking sensation that progresses rapidly from physical relaxation to pronounced drowsiness, making this strain best suited for late evening use
- **Full-body heaviness** -- An intense physical weight that relaxes muscles completely and can make movement feel effortful at moderate to high doses
- **Mental calm** -- A gentle mental quieting that accompanies the physical sedation, reducing mental chatter and promoting stillness
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported dissolution of both mental and physical tension as the heavy effects take hold

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The moderate-to-high THC range and pure heavy indica genetics produce intense sedating effects that are best reserved for evening use and may be overwhelming for inexperienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the deeply sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery. Start with a low dose to assess individual tolerance.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Cosmic Collapse presents a pungent, fuel-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced fuel and earthy aromas with a pungent, almost aggressive intensity. The fuel character is immediate and unmistakable, while the earthy undertones add depth and weight. The pungent quality is distinctive -- an almost animalic richness that sets Cosmic Collapse apart from more refined or subtle cultivars. Limonene provides a faint citrus lift that is barely perceptible beneath the heavier notes.
- **Taste:** Fuel and earthy flavors on inhalation, with pungent and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is robust and unapologetically pungent, appealing to users who appreciate the raw, diesel-forward profile that defines classic heavy indica character.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (highly undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Pronounced couch-lock at moderate to high doses, significantly limiting mobility
- Anxiety or paranoia possible in THC-sensitive individuals
- Disorientation possible at high doses due to the intensity of the sedating effects

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires standard environment control and attention to the needs of heavy indica genetics |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact, dense indica structure with minimal stretch; very manageable size |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with consistent temperatures. Heavy indica genetics generally benefit from stable environmental parameters. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The compact, dense structure requires excellent air circulation to prevent bud rot, particularly given the dense bud formation typical of heavy indica genetics. |

Cosmic Collapse benefits from attentive growing practices typical of heavy indica cultivars. The compact structure requires minimal training, though topping can improve light distribution to lower bud sites. The dense bud formation makes humidity control critical during late flower to prevent mold. The strain produces resinous buds with solid trichome coverage suitable for both flower and extraction purposes. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
