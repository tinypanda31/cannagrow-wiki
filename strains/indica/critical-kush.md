# Critical Kush

![Critical Kush](/images/strains/critical-kush.png)

## Overview

Critical Kush is an indica cultivar created by crossing Critical Mass with OG Kush. Developed by the Dutch seed company Barney's Farm, this strain combines the generous yields and fast flowering of Critical Mass with the potency and aromatic complexity of OG Kush. The result is a heavy indica that delivers deep body relaxation with an earthy, sweet, and citrus-forward terpene profile. Critical Kush has become a staple in both commercial and home grow operations due to its straightforward cultivation requirements and reliable phenotype expression.

## Genetics & Lineage

Critical Kush is a two-way cross between two well-documented cultivars:

- **Critical Mass** -- An indica-dominant strain known for its exceptionally large, heavy colas and fast flowering time. Critical Mass itself descends from Afghani and Skunk lineage and was popularized for its commercial viability.
- **OG Kush** -- The legendary California indica-dominant strain with a fuel-forward, earthy terpene profile that has become foundational in modern cannabis breeding.

**Parent lineage:** Critical Mass x OG Kush

Barney's Farm created Critical Kush by combining the yield potential and speed of Critical Mass with the potency and terpene complexity of OG Kush. The resulting strain inherits the best attributes of both parents: generous yields, fast flowering, and a recognizable OG-influenced aroma.

## Effects & Experience

Critical Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A deep, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and eases physical tension
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that intensifies over the experience, making this strain well-suited to evening or nighttime use
- **Mild euphoria** -- A gentle mood lift that accompanies the physical effects without intense cerebral stimulation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The upper end of the THC range can produce intense effects, particularly for inexperienced users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Critical Kush presents a classic OG-influenced sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Earthy and sweet with noticeable citrus undertones. The myrcene-dominant profile gives it a musky, herbal quality, while limonene adds a bright orange-citrus accent that lifts the heavier earthy notes.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on inhalation, with citrus and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and recognizable, appealing to users who appreciate traditional indica profiles with a citrus twist.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential couch-lock at high doses
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less commonly reported than with sativa-dominant strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy -- resilient and forgiving, suitable for beginner and experienced growers alike |
| **Flowering Time** | 45-55 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 450-550 g/m² indoor; 550-650 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; compact indica structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; performs well in both. Prefers a warm, dry climate with good air circulation. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold, pests, and diseases inherited from the Critical Mass parent. Heavy buds may require support to prevent branch sagging. |

Critical Kush is considered one of the easier indica strains to grow, making it an excellent choice for beginners. Its fast flowering time (inherited from Critical Mass), generous yields, and resilience to common cultivation issues have made it popular in both commercial and home grow operations. The OG Kush parent contributes some sensitivity to humidity during flowering, so adequate air circulation is important. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
