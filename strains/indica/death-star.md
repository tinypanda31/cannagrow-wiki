# Death Star

![Death Star](/images/strains/death-star.png)

## Overview

Death Star is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing the potent indica Sensi Star with the legendary sativa Sour Diesel. This combination produces a strain that carries the heavy, sedating body effects of Sensi Star while incorporating the fuel-forward, pungent aroma characteristic of Sour Diesel. Despite its sativa parent, Death Star leans heavily indica in its effects, delivering a potent body high that is best suited to evening consumption. The strain has gained popularity among consumers seeking intense physical relaxation with a recognizable diesel aroma profile.

## Genetics & Lineage

Death Star is a two-way cross combining two well-known cultivars:

- **Sensi Star** -- A pure indica cultivar (Ottawa Valley lineage, reported Afghan/Pakistani origins) known for extremely heavy body effects and resinous buds
- **Sour Diesel** -- A legendary sativa-dominant strain (Chemdawg 91 x Super Skunk x Northern Lights) famous for its fuel-forward aroma and energizing cerebral effects

**Parent lineage:** Sensi Star x Sour Diesel

The combination of these two influential strains produces a cultivar that leans strongly indica in its effects while carrying the unmistakable diesel aroma from the Sour Diesel parent. The Sensi Star contribution ensures dense, resin-coated buds and powerful physical relaxation.

## Effects & Experience

Death Star is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent body high** -- A heavy, deep physical relaxation that begins shortly after consumption and intensifies over time
- **Euphoria** -- A noticeable mental lift that accompanies the body effects, contributed by the Sour Diesel parentage
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that typically leads to sleep at moderate to high doses
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in both mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 6-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Death Star is best suited to experienced users or those with established tolerance. The high THC range combined with heavy indica effects can be overwhelming for inexperienced consumers.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Do not operate vehicles or machinery after consumption.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Death Star presents a bold, fuel-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Strong diesel fuel and skunk with earthy undertones. The Sour Diesel influence is unmistakable in the pungent fuel aroma, while the Sensi Star parent adds earthy depth. The smell is noticeable and can fill a room.
- **Taste:** Fuel and skunky flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly sweet notes on the exhale. The smoke is moderately smooth, though the potency of the flower can make the experience intense.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Heavy drowsiness and couch-lock
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia at high doses, more likely due to the elevated THC content
- Impaired coordination and cognitive function during the experience

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to environment and nutrient management |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; may exhibit some sativa stretch from Sour Diesel |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry, Mediterranean-like climate |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Sour Diesel lineage can bring some sensitivity to humidity. Adequate airflow is essential, particularly given the dense bud structure inherited from Sensi Star. |

Death Star is moderately challenging to grow. It benefits from careful attention to humidity control and stable feeding schedules. The strain responds well to topping and SCROG techniques. The Sour Diesel parentage may introduce some height stretch during early flowering, so growers should plan accordingly. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
