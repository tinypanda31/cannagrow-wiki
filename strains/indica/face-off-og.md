# Face Off OG

![Face Off Og](/images/strains/face-off-og.png)

## Overview

Face Off OG is an indica-dominant cultivar that emerged as a distinct phenotype of the OG Kush family. Recognized for its potent fuel-earth aroma and deeply sedating effects, Face Off OG has earned a reputation as one of the heavier-hitting OG derivatives. The strain's name reportedly references its intense, face-altering effects that can leave users with a fixed, relaxed expression. With its classic OG terpene profile and powerful body-focused effects, Face Off OG is a staple among indica enthusiasts who prioritize deep relaxation and sedation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Face Off OG is a phenotype selection from the broader OG Kush family:

- **OG Kush** -- The foundational indica-dominant strain from Southern California, itself believed to be a cross of Chemdawg, Hindu Kush, and a Lemon Thai phenotype. OG Kush is the genetic backbone of countless modern cultivars.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush phenotype

Face Off OG was identified through phenotype hunting within an OG Kush grow, where a particularly heavy, sedating expression was isolated and stabilized. The strain carries the classic OG genetic signature with an emphasis on the most relaxing, body-heavy traits of the lineage.

## Effects & Experience

Face Off OG is widely reported to produce:

- **Deeply sedating body effects** -- A heavy, full-body sensation that progresses from relaxation to pronounced sedation
- **Euphoria** -- An initial mental lift that quickly merges with the body-heavy effects
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported melting away of mental and physical tension
- **Couch-lock** -- At moderate to high doses, a noticeable reduction in motivation to move, making this strain unsuitable for active use

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The sedating nature of Face Off OG makes it best suited for evening or nighttime use and for users with moderate tolerance.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Face Off OG presents a classic OG sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Fuel and earthy pine dominate the aroma, with citrus lemon notes from limonene providing brightness. The classic OG Kush scent is unmistakable, with added depth from the heavy myrcene expression.
- **Taste:** Fuel and pine on inhalation, with earthy and lemon notes on the exhale. The flavor is pungent and satisfying, with the characteristic OG Kush taste that cannabis enthusiasts recognize immediately.

- Dry eyes

Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
