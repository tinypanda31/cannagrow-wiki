# Frankenstein (Funky Charms)

![Frankenstein](/images/strains/frankenstein.jpg)

## Overview

Frankenstein, also known by the name Funky Charms in some circles, is a heavy indica cultivar with unknown or undocumented genetics. Despite its uncertain lineage, this strain has earned a reputation among experienced indica enthusiasts for its deeply sedating effects and pungent, fuel-forward terpene profile. The name "Frankenstein" is thought to reference the strain's unpredictable and powerful effects, which can feel like a patchwork of heavy indica characteristics. The alternative name "Funky Charms" hints at the strain's distinctively funky, fuel-heavy aroma. Frankenstein represents the kind of cultivar that persists through clone-only networks rather than commercial seed distribution, making it something of an underground strain in cannabis culture.

## Genetics & Lineage

The exact genetics of Frankenstein (Funky Charms) are undocumented. The strain is believed to descend from heavy indica landrace or landrace-derivative genetics, possibly with Afghani or Hindu Kush influence given its effect profile and flowering characteristics. It persists primarily through clone distribution networks rather than commercial seed lines, which has contributed to the uncertainty around its precise parentage.

**Parent lineage:** Unknown heavy indica genetics (possibly Afghani-derived)

Phenotypic expression suggests strong indica characteristics: compact plant structure, dense bud formation, and a myrcene-dominant terpene profile typical of landrace indica varieties. The strain's persistence in underground cannabis culture suggests it has been valued for its effects long enough to be maintained through cloning over multiple generations.

## Effects & Experience

Frankenstein is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep sedation** -- A heavy, full-body sensation that progresses toward sleepiness, making this strain best suited for evening or nighttime use
- **Physical relaxation** -- Intense muscle relaxation that eases physical tension and discomfort
- **Mental calm** -- A quieting of mental chatter that accompanies the physical effects
- **Couch-lock** -- At moderate to higher doses, a pronounced inability or lack of motivation to move

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The sedating effects can be intense, particularly for inexperienced users or at higher doses.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the deeply sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Frankenstein presents an intense, fuel-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pungent and fuel-forward with earthy, musky undertones. The myrcene-dominant profile gives it a heavy, herbal quality, while caryophyllene adds a peppery sharpness. The overall aroma is intense and noticeable, often described as "funky" or "gassy."
- **Taste:** Earthy and fuel-like on inhalation, with pungent, peppery notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and not for those who prefer sweet or fruity profiles. Users who enjoy heavy, traditional indica flavors tend to appreciate this strain.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Significant drowsiness (undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Couch-lock that may limit mobility
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less commonly reported with heavy indica strains
