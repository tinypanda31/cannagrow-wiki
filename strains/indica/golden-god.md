# Golden God

![Golden God photo](/uploads/images/strains/golden-god.jpg)

## Overview

Golden God is a classic indica cultivar tracing its roots to Afghan landrace genetics. This strain represents a traditional indica expression with sweet, earthy, and hash-forward aromas that echo the character of classic Central Asian cannabis varieties. The golden-hued trichome coverage that gives the strain its name sits atop dense, resinous buds typical of landrace-derived indicas. Golden God delivers deeply relaxing effects that are characteristic of pure indica genetics, making it well-suited for evening use and consumers seeking a traditional indica experience. Its straightforward growing profile and reliable expression have made it a steady presence among heritage indica enthusiasts.

## Genetics & Lineage

Golden God descends from Afghan landrace indica genetics:

- **Afghan landrace** -- Indigenous indica cultivars from the Hindu Kush mountain region spanning Afghanistan and Pakistan, selected over generations for resin production, compact structure, and deeply relaxing effects

**Parent lineage:** Afghan landrace indica

The strain represents a preserved expression of traditional Afghan indica genetics, with selection focused on maintaining the classic hashy aroma, golden trichome coverage, and deeply relaxing body effects that characterize landrace-derived cultivars. Unlike many modern hybrids, Golden God's lineage remains close to its landrace origins.

## Effects & Experience

Golden God is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep physical relaxation** -- A heavy, calming body sensation that relaxes muscles and releases physical tension
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that typically leads to sleep at moderate doses, making this strain suited to evening or nighttime use
- **Mental calm** -- A grounding, calming effect on the mind that accompanies the physical relaxation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The THC range makes Golden God accessible to users with some cannabis experience while still delivering meaningful indica effects.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Do not operate vehicles or machinery after consumption.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Golden God presents a classic landrace indica sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with hashy undertones. The aroma is warm and musky, reminiscent of traditional Afghan hashish, with a sweetness that rounds out the earthy base. Golden trichomes give the buds a distinctive visual appeal.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on inhalation, with hashy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and traditional, appealing to users who appreciate classic indica profiles.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (undesirable during daytime hours)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential couch-lock at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less common than with sativa strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to Moderate -- resilient landrace-derived genetics, forgiving of common mistakes |
| **Flowering Time** | 50-60 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica structure typical of Afghan landrace genetics |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; performs well in both. Prefers a warm, dry climate similar to its Afghan origins. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold, pests, and diseases. Afghan landrace genetics are naturally hardy and adapted to challenging growing conditions. Dense buds benefit from adequate airflow. |

Golden God is relatively easy to grow, benefiting from the hardiness of its Afghan landrace genetics. The compact structure makes it well-suited to indoor cultivation, and the plants are resilient to temperature fluctuations and common growing issues. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
