# Grease Monkey

![Grease Monkey](/images/strains/grease-monkey.png)

## Overview

Grease Monkey is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing GG4 (formerly known as Gorilla Glue #4) with Cookies and Cream. This strain combines the resinous, fuel-forward potency of GG4 with the sweet, creamy vanilla character of Cookies and Cream to produce a heavy indica that delivers deep body relaxation with a complex sweet, fuel, and vanilla terpene profile. Grease Monkey has gained a following among users seeking the potency and resin production of modern hybrid genetics with a dessert-forward flavor profile.

## Genetics & Lineage

Grease Monkey is a two-way cross between two well-known modern cultivars:

- **GG4 (Gorilla Glue #4)** -- A powerful indica-dominant hybrid known for its exceptionally resinous buds, fuel-forward aroma, and heavy physical effects. GG4 itself is a cross of Chem's Sister, Sour Dubb, and Chocolate Diesel.
- **Cookies and Cream** -- A hybrid strain (Starfighter x an unknown Girl Scout Cookies phenotype) known for its sweet, creamy vanilla profile and balanced effects.

**Parent lineage:** GG4 x Cookies and Cream

By combining GG4's renowned potency and fuel-influenced character with the sweet vanilla cream of Cookies and Cream, breeders produced a strain that appeals to users seeking high-THC potency with a more palatable, dessert-like flavor profile than straight fuel-forward genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Grease Monkey is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy body relaxation** -- A deep, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and eases physical tension
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental lift that arrives initially before the body effects take over
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that typically intensifies, making this strain best suited for evening or nighttime use
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Grease Monkey's high THC range can produce intense effects, particularly for inexperienced users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the high THC content, start with a low dose if you are inexperienced.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Grease Monkey presents a complex, dessert-meets-gas-station sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet and fuel-forward with vanilla and earthy undertones. The GG4 parent contributes a recognizable diesel, gasoline-like quality, while the Cookies and Cream side adds a sweet, creamy vanilla character. The result is a complex aroma that balances sweetness with pungency.
- **Taste:** Sweet and vanilla-forward on inhalation, with fuel and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who appreciate dessert-like profiles with underlying fuel complexity.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to environment and nutrient management; GG4 genetics can bring some sensitivity |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with consistent temperatures and good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The GG4 lineage is known for heavy resin production, which can attract dust and require careful handling. Dense buds require adequate airflow. |

Grease Monkey is moderately challenging to grow. The GG4 genetics bring exceptional resin production, which is desirable for extraction but requires careful environmental management. The strain responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
