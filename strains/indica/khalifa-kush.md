# Khalifa Kush

![Khalifa Kush](/images/strains/khalifa-kush.jpg)

## Overview

Khalifa Kush (often abbreviated as KK) is an indica-dominant cultivar selected and popularized by rapper and entrepreneur Wiz Khalifa. Emerging from the OG Kush family, Khalifa Kush was carefully chosen through phenotype hunting to meet specific standards for aroma, potency, and effect profile. The strain features a fuel-citrus-earthy aromatic signature and delivers effects that begin with euphoria before settling into deep body relaxation. Khalifa Kush gained widespread attention through its association with its celebrity namesake and has since earned recognition on its own merits as a high-quality OG derivative.

## Genetics & Lineage

Khalifa Kush is an OG Kush phenotype selection:

- **OG Kush** -- The foundational indica-dominant strain from Southern California, believed to be a cross of Chemdawg, Hindu Kush, and a Lemon Thai phenotype. OG Kush is the genetic foundation of countless modern cultivars and one of the most influential strains in cannabis history.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush phenotype (selected by Wiz Khalifa)

Khalifa Kush was identified through an extensive phenotype hunting process, in which numerous OG Kush expressions were evaluated for their aromatic profile, potency, and effect characteristics. The selected phenotype exhibited a particularly well-balanced fuel-citrus-earthy aroma and a reliable euphoric-then-relaxing effect progression.

## Effects & Experience

Khalifa Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Euphoric onset** -- An initial wave of mental uplift and mood elevation that is distinctly noticeable
- **Body relaxation** -- The euphoria transitions into a warm, full-body relaxation that eases physical tension
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the effects mature
- **Sedation** -- Progressive calming and drowsiness at higher doses or as the experience progresses

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The balanced onset makes Khalifa Kush accessible to a wide range of users, while the relaxing transition provides satisfying depth for regular consumers.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Khalifa Kush presents a classic OG-derived sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Fuel and citrus dominate the initial aroma, with earthy undertones developing as the buds are broken apart. The limonene expression is particularly pronounced, giving the strain a bright, lemony character that distinguishes it from heavier OG derivatives.
- **Taste:** Fuel and citrus on inhalation, with earthy and slightly piney notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and satisfying, with the citrus character carrying through from aroma to taste.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours at higher doses)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential anxiety or paranoia in THC-sensitive individuals
- Mild hunger increase

Khalifa Kush benefits from attentive growing practices typical of the OG Kush family. The strain responds well to topping and low-stress training to manage its structure. Nutrient sensitivity is common among OG genetics, so a conservative feeding approach is recommended. The plant produces resinous buds with solid trichome coverage. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
