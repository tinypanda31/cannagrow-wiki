# LA Confidential

![La Confidential](/images/strains/la-confidential.png)

## Overview

LA Confidential is an indica cultivar created by crossing OG LA Affie with Afghani genetics. Developed in the Los Angeles area, this strain combines the West Coast kush character of its OG LA Affie parent with the reliable, resinous genetics of Afghani. The result is a smooth-smoking indica with a pine and earthy aroma, calming euphoric effects, and moderate growing requirements. LA Confidential gained popularity in the Southern California medical cannabis community before achieving broader recognition and remains a respected choice for indica enthusiasts seeking a refined, smooth experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

LA Confidential is a two-way cross:

- **OG LA Affie** -- An OG Kush phenotype selected in the Los Angeles area, contributing West Coast kush genetics
- **Afghani** -- The classic Afghan landrace indica, bringing resilience, resin production, and compact structure

**Parent lineage:** OG LA Affie x Afghani

The strain was developed in Los Angeles and reflects the breeding priorities of the Southern California cannabis community in the 2000s: smooth smoke, appealing aroma, and reliable indica effects. The Afghani parentage contributes hardiness and shorter flowering time, while the OG LA Affie parent provides the characteristic kush flavor and potency.

## Effects & Experience

LA Confidential is widely reported to produce:

- **Calming euphoria** -- A pleasant mental lift combined with physical calm, creating a balanced indica experience
- **Physical relaxation** -- Full-body muscle relaxation that eases tension without complete sedation at moderate doses
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical stress
- **Progressive sedation** -- Drowsiness that increases over time, making it well-suited to evening use

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are smooth and manageable, making LA Confidential accessible to users across experience levels.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Pine and earthy aromas with skunky undertones. The pinene presence gives it a fresh, sharp quality that is layered over the classic earthy indica base. The aroma is moderately pungent but refined.
- **Taste:** Smooth pine and earthy flavors on the inhale, with a clean, slightly skunky exhale. LA Confidential is particularly noted for the smoothness of its smoke, which is frequently described as clean and non-irritating.

Commonly reported uses include:

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness
- Mild dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for anxiety at high doses, though less common than with sativa-dominant strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact indica structure with moderate stretch |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Benefits from topping and LST to open the canopy. Adequate airflow is important to protect dense buds from bud rot. |

LA Confidential is a moderately challenging strain to grow. It is less forgiving than pure landrace indicas like Afghani or Hindu Kush but does not present the difficulties of more sensitive modern hybrids. The strain responds well to topping and low-stress training. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
