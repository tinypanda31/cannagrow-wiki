# MAC-1 (Miracle Alien Cookies)

![Mac 1](/images/strains/mac-1.jpg)

## Overview

MAC-1, short for Miracle Alien Cookies, is an indica-dominant cultivar resulting from a three-way cross of Alien Cookies, Columbian, and Starfighter genetics. Created by Capulator of the Exotic Genetix breeding program, MAC-1 has become one of the most recognizable modern strains, known for its distinctive rounded calyxes, heavy resin production, and complex sweet-floral-citrus aroma. The strain delivers a balanced effect profile that begins with euphoria and settles into satisfying body relaxation, making it versatile for both recreational and therapeutic use. MAC-1 has spawned an entire family of derivative crosses, including MAC variants and countless "Alien" themed cultivars.

## Genetics & Lineage

MAC-1 is a complex three-way cross from the Exotic Genetix breeding program:

- **Alien Cookies** -- A hybrid derived from Girl Scout Cookies crossed with an unknown "Alien" phenotype, known for its sweet, earthy profile and potent effects
- **Columbian** -- A landrace-influenced genetics trace to Colombian landrace varieties, contributing unique terpene characteristics and vigor
- **Starfighter** -- A hybrid cultivar recognized for its unique aromatic contribution and presence in several notable crosses

**Parent lineage:** Alien Cookies x Columbian x Starfighter

The three-way cross was designed to combine the potency of Alien Cookies, the unique landrace character of Columbian genetics, and the aromatic complexity of Starfighter. The result is a strain with a highly distinctive appearance -- MAC-1 is known for its unusually round, compact calyxes that are almost spherical in shape.

## Effects & Experience

MAC-1 is widely reported to produce:

- **Balanced euphoria** -- An initial wave of mental uplift and mood elevation that is clear and pleasant
- **Body relaxation** -- The euphoria transitions into a warm, full-body relaxation that eases physical tension without overwhelming sedation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the effects mature
- **Creative focus** -- Some users report a period of creative focus during the euphoric phase before the body effects fully settle in

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The balanced effect profile makes MAC-1 accessible to a wide range of users, from moderate to experienced consumers.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

MAC-1 presents a unique and complex sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet, floral, and citrus with earthy undertones. The linalool-heavy profile produces a distinctly floral aroma that is unusual among modern strains, complemented by citrus brightness from limonene and a spicy depth from caryophyllene.
- **Taste:** Sweet and floral on inhalation, with citrus and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and complex, with the floral character carrying through from aroma to taste in a way that is uniquely MAC-1.

| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 350-450 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact structure with distinctive round calyxes; benefits from training |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor preferred for optimal structure and terpene expression |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense, resinous buds require excellent air circulation to prevent bud rot. Sensitive to nutrient imbalances. |

MAC-1 rewards experienced growers who can manage its specific needs. The strain's compact, round calyx structure is distinctive but requires careful humidity management. Training techniques such as topping and low-stress training help maximize yield and light penetration. The heavy resin production makes this strain exceptionally well-suited for hash and extraction work. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
