# Mendo Moon Rock

![Mendo Moon Rock photo](/uploads/images/strains/mendo-moon-rock.jpg)

## Overview

Mendo Moon Rock is a pure indica cultivar created by crossing Mendo Purps with Moon Rock genetics. This cross combines the iconic purple, grape-sweet character of Mendo Purps -- a legendary Northern California indica from Mendocino County -- with the resin-heavy, potent character of Moon Rock genetics to produce a deeply sedating indica with a rich grape, berry, and earthy terpene profile. Its dark purple buds, heavy trichome coverage, and unmistakable grape-berry aroma make Mendo Moon Rock visually and aromatically striking.

## Genetics & Lineage

Mendo Moon Rock is a two-way cross between two distinctly characterized genetics:

- **Mendo Purps** -- A legendary indica cultivar from Mendocino County in Northern California's Emerald Triangle, known for its deep purple coloration, grape-and-berry sweetness, and heavy body effects.
- **Moon Rock** -- Heavily resinous, potent varieties selected for exceptional trichome production and concentrated effects.

**Parent lineage:** Mendo Purps x Moon Rock genetics

This cross merges the grape-sweet, purple character of Mendo Purps with the resin-heavy potency of Moon Rock genetics. The Mendo Purps parent contributes its recognizable grape-berry sweetness and purple coloration, while the Moon Rock genetics amplify resin production and deepen the body-heavy sedating effects.

## Effects & Experience

Mendo Moon Rock is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy sedation** -- A deep, body-locking sensation that progresses from physical relaxation to pronounced drowsiness, making this strain best suited for late evening use
- **Full-body relaxation** -- A thorough easing of muscle tension and physical discomfort from head to toe
- **Mild euphoria** -- A gentle mental calm that accompanies the physical sedation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the heavy body effects settle in

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The moderate-to-high THC range and pure indica genetics produce effects that are best suited for evening use and may be overwhelming for inexperienced users at higher doses.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the heavily sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Mendo Moon Rock presents a grape-berry-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced grape and berry sweetness with earthy undertones. The Mendo Purps parent is immediately apparent in the recognizable grape character, while the Moon Rock genetics contribute an earthy depth and resinous richness. The cured buds carry a jam-like, fruity aroma that is sweet and inviting, with the heavy trichome coverage adding a hash-like complexity.
- **Taste:** Sweet grape and berry flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly resinous notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and layered, with limonene providing faint citrus accents that enhance the grape character. The finish carries an earthy warmth that grounds the sweeter top notes.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to temperature management for optimal purple expression and humidity control for resinous buds |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Short to medium -- compact pure indica structure with minimal stretch; very manageable size |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; cooler nighttime temperatures (60-65°F / 15-18°C) during flowering enhance purple pigmentation inherited from Mendo Purps. The Moon Rock genetics benefit from consistent environmental parameters. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Mendo Purps genetics, developed in outdoor Northern California conditions, contribute some resilience. However, the heavy resin and dense bud formation from the Moon Rock parent require excellent air circulation to prevent bud rot. |

Mendo Moon Rock benefits from attentive growing practices typical of purple indica cultivars. The strain rewards growers who can manage temperature fluctuations during flowering, as cooler nighttime temperatures trigger the vivid purple coloration inherited from Mendo Purps. The heavy resin production makes this strain well-suited for hash and extraction work. The compact structure requires minimal training, though topping can improve light distribution. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October, with purple coloration intensifying as nighttime temperatures drop.
