# Orange Kush

![Orange Kush](/images/strains/orange-kush.png)

## Overview

Orange Kush is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Orange Juice with OG Kush. This strain combines the bright, citrus-forward profile of Orange Juice with the fuel-influenced potency and earthy depth of OG Kush to produce a distinctive indica that delivers euphoric cerebral onset followed by deep body relaxation. The orange-citrus character of Orange Kush makes it one of the more recognizably fruity OG-derived strains, appealing to users who appreciate the classic Kush structure with a vibrant citrus twist.

## Genetics & Lineage

Orange Kush is a two-way cross between two documented cultivars:

- **Orange Juice** -- A citrus-forward strain known for its bright orange aroma and flavor, contributing the distinctive citrus terpene profile to this cross.
- **OG Kush** -- The legendary California indica-dominant strain with a fuel-forward, earthy terpene profile that has become foundational in modern cannabis breeding. OG Kush contributes potency, structure, and aromatic complexity.

**Parent lineage:** Orange Juice x OG Kush

By combining the bright citrus character of Orange Juice with the renowned potency and earthy depth of OG Kush, breeders produced a strain that offers the best of both worlds: recognizable fruity sweetness with classic Kush body effects.

## Effects & Experience

Orange Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Euphoric cerebral onset** -- An initial mood lift and mental clarity that arrives before the body effects
- **Body relaxation** -- A gradual transition to full-body relaxation as the experience progresses
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension
- **Sedation** -- At higher doses, drowsiness that makes this strain suited to evening or nighttime use

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The upper end of the THC range can produce intense effects, particularly for inexperienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Orange Kush presents a vibrant, citrus-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced orange and citrus sweetness with earthy and fuel undertones. The limonene-dominant profile gives it a bright, almost fresh-squeezed orange quality, while the OG Kush parent contributes an underlying earthy, slightly fuel-like sharpness that grounds the sweetness.
- **Taste:** Orange and citrus flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly fuel-forward notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who appreciate fruity profiles with underlying Kush complexity.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for anxiety at higher doses in THC-sensitive individuals
- Mild headache possible in some users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to environment and nutrient management; OG Kush genetics can bring some sensitivity |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with consistent temperatures and good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense buds require adequate airflow to prevent bud rot. The OG Kush lineage may bring some sensitivity to humidity fluctuations. |

Orange Kush is moderately challenging to grow. The OG Kush genetics require attentive environmental management, particularly humidity control during flowering. The strain responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
