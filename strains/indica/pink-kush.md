# Pink Kush

![Pink Kush](/images/strains/pink-kush.png)

## Overview

Pink Kush is a pure indica cultivar that is widely recognized as a phenotype of OG Kush. This strain is known for its distinctive sweet, floral, and vanilla terpene profile that sets it apart from the more fuel-forward OG Kush expressions, as well as its heavy sedating effects that make it one of the most potent indica options for evening and nighttime use. Pink Kush has earned a reputation as a premium OG Kush cut, with its pink-tinged buds (when expressed under the right growing conditions) and dessert-like aroma making it instantly recognizable among experienced cannabis consumers.

## Genetics & Lineage

Pink Kush is a selected phenotype of OG Kush:

- **OG Kush** -- The legendary California indica-dominant strain with a fuel-forward, earthy terpene profile. OG Kush's exact origins are debated, but it is widely believed to be a cross of Chemdawg with a Hindu Kush landrace, with possible Lemon Thai influence. Pink Kush represents a specific cut of OG Kush that expresses elevated sweet, floral, and vanilla terpenes rather than the more common fuel-forward profile.

**Parent lineage:** OG Kush (sweet floral phenotype)

Pink Kush has been used as a parent in further breeding, contributing its distinctive sweet profile and potent indica effects to various "Pink" crosses.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Share | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Myrcene | 30-50% | Dominant terpene; earthy, herbal base associated with heavy sedation |
| Caryophyllene | 15-25% | Peppery, spicy notes adding depth to the sweet profile |
| Limonene | 10-18% | Citrus brightness that enhances the sweet character |
| Linalool | 5-10% | Floral, lavender notes contributing to the vanilla-like sweetness |
| Pinene | 2-5% | Subtle pine undertones from the OG Kush lineage |

The myrcene-caryophyllene-limonene combination, with notable linalool, produces Pink Kush's signature sweet, floral, and vanilla aroma -- a profile that distinguishes it from fuel-forward OG Kush phenotypes.

## Effects & Experience

Pink Kush is widely reported to produce:

- **Heavy sedation** -- A deep, full-body sensation that progresses toward sleepiness, making this strain best suited for evening or nighttime use
- **Physical relaxation** -- Intense muscle relaxation that eases physical tension and discomfort
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental lift that arrives initially before the heavy body effects take over
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Pink Kush's high THC range and deeply sedating effects can be intense, making it best suited for experienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the heavy sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Pink Kush presents a sweet, dessert-like sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet and floral with vanilla and earthy undertones. The linalool-influenced profile gives it a soft, almost candy-like quality that is noticeably different from the fuel-forward OG Kush standard. The sweetness is prominent and recognizable, with a floral character that hints at rose or lavender.
- **Taste:** Sweet and floral on inhalation, with vanilla and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who appreciate sweet, candy-like indica profiles.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Significant drowsiness (undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Couch-lock that may limit mobility
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, particularly given the elevated THC range

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to difficult -- OG Kush genetics require attentive environmental management |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; compact indica structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with consistent temperatures, controlled humidity, and good air circulation. The pink coloration is enhanced by cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. OG Kush genetics are known to be somewhat sensitive to powdery mildew and humidity fluctuations. Dense buds require adequate airflow to prevent bud rot. |

Pink Kush is moderately challenging to grow. The OG Kush genetics require attentive environmental management, particularly humidity control during flowering. The strain responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. Growers who manage a temperature drop at night may see pink or rose-tinged coloration in the buds.
