# Purple Mango

![Purple Mango](/images/strains/purple-mango.png)

## Overview

Purple Mango is an indica-dominant cultivar created by crossing Mango genetics with Purple lineage. This cross combines the tropical, mango-forward sweetness of its Mango parent with the grape-sweet, color-enhancing character of Purple genetics to produce a relaxing indica-dominant strain with a distinctive tropical-grape terpene profile. The strain's dual fruit character -- tropical mango and sweet grape -- creates a unique aromatic identity that stands apart from single-note fruit profiles.

## Genetics & Lineage

Purple Mango is a two-way cross between two fruit-forward genetics:

- **Mango** -- An indica or indica-dominant cultivar known for its pronounced tropical mango aroma and sweet, fruity flavor. Mango genetics carry the rich, tropical fruit character that has made mango-named strains popular in markets that value exotic, dessert-like profiles. The parent is valued for its ability to pass on both its distinctive mango terpene profile and its relaxing body effects.
- **Purple Lineage** -- Grape-sweet, color-enhancing indica genetics known for purple pigmentation and sweet grape-berry aroma, potentially including Granddaddy Purple, Purple Kush, or related varieties.

**Parent lineage:** Mango x Purple lineage

This cross was designed to merge the tropical sweetness of Mango with the grape-sweet, purple character of its indica-dominant parent. The result is a strain that delivers relaxing body effects with a dual-fruit aromatic profile that is both tropical and grape-sweet.

## Effects & Experience

Purple Mango is widely reported to produce:

- **Relaxing body effects** -- A soothing, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and eases physical tension without overwhelming heaviness
- **Mild sedation** -- A gentle progression into drowsiness that is noticeable but not debilitating, making this strain suitable for early evening use
- **Mood elevation** -- A mild mental uplift and sense of well-being that accompanies the physical relaxation, enhanced by the sweet aromatic profile
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the body effects settle in

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The moderate THC range makes Purple Mango one of the more approachable indica-dominant options, suitable for users of varying experience levels.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Purple Mango presents a tropical-grape sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced tropical mango and grape sweetness with earthy undertones. The Mango parent is immediately apparent in the rich, tropical fruit character, while the Purple genetics contribute a sweet grape note that creates a layered, dual-fruit aroma. The cured buds carry a fresh, fruity aroma that is sweet and inviting, with myrcene providing an earthy foundation that grounds the sweeter top notes.
- **Taste:** Sweet mango and grape flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly citrus notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, with limonene providing citrus accents that bridge the tropical and grape characters. The finish carries a faint herbal sweetness that is characteristic of myrcene-dominant profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to moderate -- the fruit-forward genetics tend to be consistent and manageable, making this strain accessible to newer growers |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact indica-dominant structure with moderate stretch during early flower |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with consistent temperatures. Cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering may enhance purple coloration inherited from the Purple lineage. The Mango genetics favor slightly warmer conditions for optimal tropical fruit expression. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense buds require adequate air circulation to prevent bud rot in high-humidity environments. The indica-dominant structure benefits from support during late flower as colas develop weight. |

Purple Mango benefits from attentive growing practices. The strain responds well to topping and low-stress training to manage its compact structure and improve light distribution to lower bud sites. Temperature management during flowering is important for those seeking the purple coloration from the Purple parent lineage. Consistent nutrient management and humidity control produce clean, well-cured buds with the characteristic tropical-grape aroma. The strain produces resinous buds with solid trichome coverage suitable for both flower and extraction purposes. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
