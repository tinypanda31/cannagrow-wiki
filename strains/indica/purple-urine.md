# Purple Urine

![Purple Urine photo](/uploads/images/strains/purple-urine.jpg)

## Overview

Purple Urine is an indica-dominant cultivar derived from Purple Skunk genetics, known for its pronounced grape and berry aroma alongside deeply relaxing body effects. The strain's name references both its vivid purple coloration and the visual impact it makes. Purple Urine carries forward the landrace-influenced purple genetics of its ancestry while delivering consistent indica effects that have made it a reliable choice for evening and nighttime cannabis consumers. Its grape-berry-earthy profile and moderate THC range make it accessible to a wide range of users.

## Genetics & Lineage

Purple Urine traces its genetics to the purple cultivar family:

- **Purple Skunk** -- A cross of Skunk #1 with purple-varietal genetics, itself influenced by Mendocino Purps landrace genetics. Purple Skunk brought purple coloration and grape-berry aromatics into mainstream cannabis breeding.
- **Unknown genetics** -- The full lineage of Purple Urine includes undocumented genetics that were part of the original breeding program, contributing additional indica characteristics.

**Parent lineage:** Purple Skunk x unknown

The exact identity of the unknown genetics remains undocumented, but the resulting strain exhibits consistent indica-dominant characteristics with enhanced purple coloration and a pronounced grape-berry aromatic profile.

## Effects & Experience

Purple Urine is widely reported to produce:

- **Relaxing body effects** -- A warm, full-body sensation that loosens muscles and eases physical tension
- **Mild euphoria** -- A gentle mental lift that accompanies the body relaxation without overwhelming cerebral stimulation
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental tension as the indica effects settle in
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness at higher doses or as the experience matures, making this strain suitable for evening use

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The moderate THC range makes Purple Urine accessible to a wide range of users while still providing satisfying depth.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Purple Urine presents a distinctive grape-forward sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Grape and berry sweetness dominate the aroma, with earthy undertones from the myrcene base. The cured buds carry a wine-like, fruity aroma that is immediately recognizable as characteristic of the purple genetics family.
- **Taste:** Sweet grape and berry flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, with limonene providing faint citrus accents that enhance the fruit character.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to temperature management for optimal purple expression |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450-550 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact to moderate stretch; manageable structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; cooler nighttime temperatures (60-65°F / 15-18°C) during flowering enhance purple pigmentation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Skunk-influenced genetics contribute some vigor. Dense buds require adequate air circulation to prevent bud rot. |

Purple Urine rewards growers who can manage temperature fluctuations during flowering. The purple coloration intensifies when nighttime temperatures drop to approximately 60-65°F (15-18°C). The strain benefits from support structures during late flower as the buds add weight. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
