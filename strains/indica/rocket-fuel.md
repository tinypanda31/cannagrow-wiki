# Rocket Fuel

![Rocket Fuel](/images/strains/rocket-fuel.png)

## Overview

Rocket Fuel is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Tropicana Cookies with Jet Fuel Gelato. This strain combines the bright, citrus-forward profile of Tropicana Cookies (itself a cross of Orange Punch and Girl Scout Cookies) with the fuel-influenced potency of Jet Fuel Gelato (a Gelato and Jet Fuel cross) to produce a complex indica that delivers an initial energetic cerebral lift followed by deep body relaxation. The name "Rocket Fuel" is fitting for a strain that launches with a bright, uplifting onset before transitioning into heavy indica body effects.

## Genetics & Lineage

Rocket Fuel is a two-way cross between two modern, well-documented cultivars:

- **Tropicana Cookies** -- A citrus-forward hybrid created by crossing Orange Punch (itself a cross of Tangie and an unknown OG phenotype) with Girl Scout Cookies. Known for its bright orange, citrus aroma and balanced effects.
- **Jet Fuel Gelato** -- A hybrid cross of Jet Fuel (Aspen OG x High Country OG) and Gelato (Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint GSC). Contributes fuel-forward potency and the creamy, sweet Gelato character.

**Parent lineage:** Tropicana Cookies x Jet Fuel Gelato

By combining the bright citrus character of Tropicana Cookies with the fuel-influenced potency and dessert sweetness of Jet Fuel Gelato, breeders produced a strain that offers an energetic, uplifting onset followed by deep indica body relaxation -- a dynamic effect profile that appeals to users seeking versatility from a single strain.

## Effects & Experience

Rocket Fuel is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic cerebral onset** -- An initial mood lift and mental energy that arrives before the body effects
- **Body relaxation** -- A gradual transition to full-body relaxation as the experience progresses
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental lift that accompanies the energetic onset
- **Sedation** -- At higher doses, drowsiness that makes this strain suited to evening or nighttime use

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The upper end of the THC range can produce intense effects, particularly for inexperienced users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Rocket Fuel presents a complex, citrus-meets-fuel sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Citrus-forward with fuel and sweet undertones. The limonene-dominant profile gives it a bright, orange-citrus quality, while the caryophyllene and underlying Gelato genetics contribute a fuel-like sharpness and creamy sweetness. The overall aroma is complex and noticeable.
- **Taste:** Citrus and sweet on inhalation, with fuel and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who appreciate complex profiles that balance fruity sweetness with fuel-forward depth.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth (cottonmouth)
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours at higher doses)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential for anxiety at higher doses in THC-sensitive individuals
- Mild headache possible in some users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |

| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with consistent temperatures and good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense buds require adequate airflow to prevent bud rot. The Gelato lineage may bring some sensitivity to humidity fluctuations. |

Rocket Fuel is moderately challenging to grow. The complex modern genetics require attentive environmental management, particularly humidity control during flowering. The strain responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
