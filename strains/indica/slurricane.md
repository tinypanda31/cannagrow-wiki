# Slurricane

![Slurricane](/images/strains/slurricane.jpg)

## Overview

Slurricane is an indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Do-Si-Dos with Purple Punch. This strain combines the earthy, sweet complexity of Do-Si-Dos (itself a cross of Girl Scout Cookies and Face Off OG) with the grape-and-berry sweetness of Purple Punch (Granddaddy Purple x Larry OG) to produce a deeply relaxing indica with a pronounced grape, berry, and sweet terpene profile. Slurricane has become popular among consumers seeking heavy body relaxation with a dessert-forward flavor that appeals to those who enjoy fruity, candy-like cannabis profiles.

## Genetics & Lineage

Slurricane is a two-way cross between two well-documented modern cultivars:

- **Do-Si-Dos** -- An indica-dominant hybrid created by crossing Girl Scout Cookies with Face Off OG. Known for its earthy, sweet, and slightly floral profile with deeply relaxing effects. Do-Si-Dos has become one of the most influential modern genetics, serving as a parent in numerous popular crosses.
- **Purple Punch** -- An indica-dominant hybrid (Granddaddy Purple x Larry OG) known for its pronounced grape, blueberry, and vanilla sweetness with deep relaxation effects.

**Parent lineage:** Do-Si-Dos x Purple Punch

By combining the earthy, GSC-derived complexity of Do-Si-Dos with the grape-and-berry sweetness of Purple Punch, breeders produced a strain that delivers deep body relaxation with a rich, dessert-like terpene profile. Both parents are individually recognized as highly influential modern genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Slurricane is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep body relaxation** -- A heavy, full-body sensation that relaxes muscles and eases physical tension
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant mental lift that arrives initially before the body effects take over
- **Sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that typically leads to sleep at moderate doses, making this strain best suited for evening or nighttime use
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported reduction in mental and physical tension

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. Slurricane is best suited to evening use and may be overwhelming for inexperienced users at higher doses.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the deeply sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Slurricane presents a rich, dessert-like sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced grape and berry sweetness with earthy and floral undertones. The Purple Punch parent contributes the recognizable grape character, while the Do-Si-Dos side adds an earthy, slightly floral complexity. The overall aroma is sweet and fruity, with a dessert-like quality.
- **Taste:** Sweet grape and berry flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly floral notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and palatable, making it popular among users who appreciate sweet, fruity indica profiles.

- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (may be undesirable during daytime hours)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Potential couch-lock at higher doses
- Anxiety or paranoia possible at high doses, though less common than with sativa-dominant strains

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires attention to environment, particularly humidity management; dense buds from both parents require airflow |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 400-500 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- stretches moderately during early flower; compact indica-dominant structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers a warm, dry climate with consistent temperatures. Cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering may enhance purple coloration inherited from the Purple Punch lineage. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Dense, resinous buds from both parent strains require adequate airflow to prevent bud rot in high-humidity environments. |

Slurricane is moderately challenging to grow. Both parent strains (Do-Si-Dos and Purple Punch) produce dense, resinous buds that require careful humidity management. The strain responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
