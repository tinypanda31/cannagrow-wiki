# Toad Breath

![Toad Breath photo](/uploads/images/strains/toad-breath.jpg)

## Overview

Toad Breath is an indica-dominant cultivar created by crossing Toad with Gelato Breath. This cross combines the raw potency and unique character of Toad genetics with the sweet, dessert-like complexity of Gelato Breath to produce a deeply sedating indica with a sweet, fuel, and earthy terpene profile.

## Genetics & Lineage

Toad Breath is a two-way cross between two potent modern genetics:

- **Toad** -- A potent indica-dominant cultivar known for its heavy body effects and unique terpene expression. Toad genetics have become increasingly valued in the breeding community for their ability to pass on potency and distinctive aromatic character to offspring.
- **Gelato Breath** -- A derivative of the Gelato family (originally Sunset Sherbet x Thin Mint Girl Scout Cookies), Gelato Breath carries the sweet, dessert-like aromatic complexity and balanced effects that have made the Gelato line one of the most influential in modern breeding.

**Parent lineage:** Toad x Gelato Breath

This cross was designed to merge the raw potency of Toad with the aromatic refinement of Gelato Breath. The result is a strain that delivers deep sedation through the Toad genetics while the Gelato Breath parent contributes a sweet, layered terpene profile that softens and complicates the heavier fuel character.

## Effects & Experience

Toad Breath is widely reported to produce:

- **Deep sedation** -- A profound, body-locking sensation that progresses from physical relaxation to pronounced drowsiness, making this strain best suited for late evening use
- **Euphoria** -- An initial mental uplift that arrives before the heavy body effects take over
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported melting away of mental tension as the sedating effects settle in
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Notable increase in hunger, particularly as the effects mature

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The high THC range and indica-dominant genetics produce intense effects that are best reserved for evening use and may be overwhelming for inexperienced users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the deeply sedating nature of this strain, avoid use before driving or operating machinery.**

## Aroma & Flavor

Toad Breath presents a rich, layered sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Sweet and fuel-forward with earthy undertones. The Toad genetics contribute a pungent, diesel-like character that is balanced by the Gelato Breath parent's sweet, dessert-like complexity. The cured buds carry a rich aroma that is simultaneously sweet and pungent.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on inhalation, with fuel notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, with limonene adding citrus brightness that prevents the profile from becoming one-dimensional. The Gelato Breath influence is most apparent in the sweet, slightly creamy finish.

- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses

- Couch-lock at moderate to high doses, limiting mobility
- Disorientation possible at very high doses due to the elevated THC range

Toad Breath rewards attentive growing. The strain benefits from controlled nutrient regimes and consistent environmental parameters. Training techniques such as topping and low-stress training help manage the plant's structure and improve light penetration to lower bud sites. The heavy resin production makes this strain well-suited for hash and extraction work. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
