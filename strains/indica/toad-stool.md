# Toad Stool

![Toad Stool photo](/uploads/images/strains/toad-stool.jpg)

## Overview

Toad Stool is an indica-dominant cultivar created by crossing Toad with GMO Cookies. Toad brings its raw, heavy-bodied indica power, while GMO Cookies (Girl Scout Cookies x Animal Cookies) contributes its unmistakable garlic, diesel, and earthy profile. The result is a strain that delivers a potent body high wrapped in a fuel, garlic, and earthy terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, myrcene, and limonene. Toad Stool appeals to experienced users seeking maximum potency and the pungent gas profile that defines the modern craft breeding movement.

## Genetics & Lineage

Toad Stool is a two-way cross between two highly potent modern genetics:

- **Toad** -- A potent indica-dominant cultivar known for heavy body effects and distinctive terpene expression.
- **GMO Cookies** -- Also known as "GMBO," an indica-dominant hybrid (Girl Scout Cookies x Animal Cookies) known for pungent garlic, diesel, and earthy aroma. One of the most influential gas-profile genetics in modern breeding.

**Parent lineage:** Toad x GMO Cookies

This cross combines the raw sedating power of Toad with the pungent garlic-diesel punch of GMO Cookies.

The caryophyllene-myrcene foundation carries the full weight of Toad Stool's fuel, garlic, and earthy character. The elevated caryophyllene from the GMO parent and the heavy myrcene from both parents combine to produce one of the more pungent profiles in the indica-dominant category.

## Effects & Experience

Toad Stool is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent body high** -- An intense, full-body sensation that hits hard and fast, relaxing muscles and easing physical tension with significant force
- **Heavy sedation** -- Progressive drowsiness that intensifies rapidly, making this strain best suited for late evening use and inexperienced users should approach with caution
- **Euphoria** -- A brief mental uplift that arrives before the heavy body effects take over, quickly subsumed by the physical intensity
- **Stress relief** -- Commonly reported dissolution of mental and physical tension as the potent effects settle in

**Onset:** Effects typically begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong to very strong. The high THC range and the combined potency of both parent genetics produce intense effects that are best reserved for evening use and may be overwhelming for inexperienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Due to the potent body high and high THC content, start with a low dose and avoid use before driving or operating machinery.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

Toad Stool presents a pungent, fuel-and-garlic sensory profile:

- **Smell:** Pronounced fuel and garlic aromas with earthy undertones. The GMO parent is immediately apparent in the unmistakable garlic-diesel character, while the Toad genetics add a raw, pungent depth. The cured buds carry a rich, aggressive aroma that is both pungent and complex -- a striking combination that defines the strain's identity. Limonene provides a faint citrus lift that is barely perceptible beneath the heavier fuel and garlic notes.
- **Taste:** Fuel and garlic flavors on inhalation, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and unapologetically pungent, appealing to users who appreciate the raw diesel-garlic profile that defines modern gas genetics. The finish carries an earthy warmth that is characteristic of both parent strains.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Drowsiness (highly undesirable for daytime use)
- Dizziness at higher doses
- Pronounced couch-lock at moderate to high doses, significantly limiting mobility
- Anxiety or paranoia possible in THC-sensitive individuals, more likely given the high THC range
- Disorientation possible at high doses due to the intensity of the effects

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to difficult -- requires experience with high-potency gas genetics and careful environment control |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 350-450 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- compact indica-dominant structure with moderate stretch during early flower |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor preferred for optimal terpene expression and yield consistency. Gas genetics often require precise environmental parameters for best results. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The GMO genetics can be somewhat sensitive to environmental fluctuations. Dense, heavily resinous buds require excellent air circulation to prevent bud rot. May be sensitive to nutrient imbalances. |

Toad Stool benefits from attentive growing practices. The strain responds well to topping and low-stress training. Consistent nutrient management is critical, as both parent genetics can be sensitive to overfeeding. The heavy resin production makes this strain well-suited for hash and extraction work. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
