# Amnesia Haze

![Amnesia Haze](/images/strains/amnesia-haze.png)

## Overview

Amnesia Haze is a sativa-dominant cultivar that combines Jamaican, Laotian, Hawaiian, and Afghani genetics into a complex and potent Haze variety. With a notably long flowering time of 70-80 days, Amnesia Haze demands patience from cultivators but rewards them with a strain that has become a European favorite and a global benchmark for quality sativa effects. Its citrus-earthy-sweet aroma, combined with intensely uplifting and creative cerebral effects, has made it one of the most respected Haze-dominant strains in the cannabis world.

## Genetics & Lineage

Amnesia Haze is a multi-origin cross:

- **Jamaican** -- A sativa landrace from Jamaica contributing tropical sativa genetics
- **Laotian x Hawaiian** -- Southeast Asian and Pacific sativa landraces contributing cerebral, creative effects
- **Afghani** -- The Afghan landrace indica contributing some structure and resin production

**Parent lineage:** Jamaican x (Laotian x Hawaiian) x Afghani

The strain was developed in the Netherlands and gained particular popularity across Europe, where it became a staple of coffee shops and dispensary menus. The complex multi-landrace genetics produce a strain with a uniquely layered terpene profile and potent sativa effects. Amnesia Haze has been widely cloned and pheno-hunted, and several phenotype selections (including Amnesia Haze Original, Amnesia Haze x Lost Farmer, and others) exist across different seed banks.

## Effects & Experience

Amnesia Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A bright, intense mood lift that is cheerful and long-lasting
- **Creativity and mental stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental engagement, popular among artists and creatives
- **Energy and sociability** -- Increased energy and social engagement
- **Cerebral intensity** -- At higher doses, the cerebral effects can be quite intense, producing a powerful head high

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 6-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Amnesia Haze delivers potent sativa effects that are best suited to experienced users, particularly at the upper end of the THC range.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus, earthy, and sweet with herbal undertones. The aroma is bright and complex, with a noticeable citrus brightness layered over an earthy Haze foundation.
- **Taste:** Citrus and earthy on the inhale, with sweet and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and layered, with a pleasant sweetness that complements the classic Haze earthiness.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or racing thoughts at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia, particularly in users sensitive to potent sativa effects
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day
- Dizziness and disorientation at high doses

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Difficult -- requires experience, patience, and adequate space |
| **Flowering Time** | 70-80 days (indoor) -- notably long, requiring commitment |
| **Yield** | 500-600 g/m² indoor; 650+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall to very tall -- significant sativa stretch; extensive height management required indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with ample vertical space. Best suited to experienced growers. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The long flowering period requires consistent environmental control and monitoring. Responds well to SCROG and extensive training. |

Amnesia Haze is a challenging strain to grow. The very long flowering time (70-80 days) demands patience and consistent environmental management. The significant sativa stretch requires aggressive training and height control indoors. The multi-landrace genetics produce a vigorous but demanding plant that rewards experienced growers with substantial yields. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late October to early November, and the plant can grow very large. The extended flowering window means growers must be vigilant about mold and bud rot as the season progresses.
