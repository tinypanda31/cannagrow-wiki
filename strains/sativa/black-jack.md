# Black Jack

![Black Jack](/images/strains/black-jack.jpg)

## Overview

Black Jack is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the heavy, resinous Black Domina with the legendary cerebral stimulant Jack Herer. The result is a strain that blends the best of both worlds: the dense, resin-laden structure of Black Domina with the creative, uplifting sativa effects of Jack Herer. Black Jack delivers THC levels in the 15-20% range with a terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, pinene, and terpinolene, producing an earthy, pine-forward aroma with spicy undertones. The strain is prized for its energetic and creative effects, making it a popular choice for daytime use among users who want functional cerebral stimulation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Black Jack combines two well-established genetics:

- **Black Domina** -- A heavy indica created from Afghani, Northern Lights, Ortega, and Hash Plant genetics, known for extreme resin production and dense bud structure
- **Jack Herer** -- The legendary sativa-dominant strain (Haze x Northern Lights #5 x Shiva Skunk) renowned for creative, uplifting cerebral effects

**Parent lineage:** Black Domina x Jack Herer

The cross was designed to introduce the heavy resin production and structural density of Black Domina into the creative, energetic framework of Jack Herer. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid retains the mental clarity and creative drive of its Jack Herer parent while gaining some of the physical resin and bud density from Black Domina.

## Effects & Experience

Black Jack is widely reported to produce:

- **Creative stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental focus, inherited from the Jack Herer lineage
- **Energy and alertness** -- A noticeable increase in energy and wakefulness suitable for daytime use
- **Uplifting mood lift** -- A bright, cheerful elevation that promotes positivity
- **Functional cerebral high** -- The effects are pronounced but allow users to remain productive and engaged

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, making Black Jack suitable for a wide range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy, pine, and spicy with herbal undertones. The pinene-caryophyllene combination gives it a fresh, forest-like quality with a peppery edge inherited from both parent strains.
- **Taste:** Earthy and pine on the inhale, with spicy and slightly sweet notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional cannabis taste with added complexity.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the Black Domina heritage keeps height more manageable than pure sativas |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. The Black Domina heritage contributes resilience. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Black Jack is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the Black Domina genetics keep the plant more compact and manageable than pure sativa varieties. The reasonable flowering time (55-65 days) is an advantage. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The dense bud structure inherited from Black Domina means growers should monitor for mold in high-humidity environments.
