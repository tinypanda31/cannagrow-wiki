# Blue Dream Haze

![Blue Dream Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/blue-dream-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Blue Dream Haze is a sativa cultivar created by crossing the popular Blue Dream with classic Haze genetics. The result is a strain that takes the berry-sweet fruitiness of Blue Dream and amplifies it with the cerebral intensity and complex terpene profile of Haze lineage. Blue Dream Haze is distinct from standard Blue Dream -- the addition of Haze genetics pushes the effects toward a more creative, energetic, and head-focused experience. This strain appeals to users who enjoy the approachable flavor of Blue Dream but want the more stimulating and intellectually engaging character associated with Haze breeding.

## Genetics & Lineage

Blue Dream Haze is a deliberate cross of two well-known genetic lines:

- **Blue Dream** -- The widely popular Blue Dream cultivar itself (Blueberry x Haze), contributing berry sweetness, reliable genetics, and broad appeal
- **Haze** -- Classic Haze genetics (believed to include Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian landrace components), adding cerebral intensity, creative effects, and extended flowering complexity

**Parent lineage:** Blue Dream x Haze

The Haze component shifts the balance away from Blue Dream's hybrid comfort zone toward a more distinctly sativa-forward experience. The terpinolene and pinene expression increases relative to standard Blue Dream, producing the more energizing and creative effect profile that defines Blue Dream Haze.

## Effects & Experience

Blue Dream Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Creativity and mental stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and intellectual engagement, making it popular among artists and thinkers
- **Energy and alertness** -- A stimulating cerebral effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Euphoria** -- An uplifting, cheerful mood lift characteristic of quality sativa genetics
- **Focus** -- Some users report improved mental clarity for work and creative projects

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, suitable for a wide range of users seeking daytime stimulation.

Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Berry sweetness with citrus brightness and herbal undertones. The Haze influence adds a complex herbal-spicy layer to the Blue Dream fruitiness.
- **Taste:** Berry and citrus flavors on the inhale, with herbal and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is layered and complex, combining fruit sweetness with classic Haze character.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience with sativa characteristics and longer flowering times |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-75 days (indoor) -- longer than Blue Dream due to Haze influence |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 550+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- sativa stretch is significant; training recommended indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The extended flowering time requires consistent environmental management. Responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG. |

Blue Dream Haze is moderately challenging to grow. The Haze influence extends the flowering time compared to standard Blue Dream (which typically flowers in 60-70 days), requiring more patience and consistent environment control. The plant stretches during early flowering, requiring height management techniques. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The Blue Dream genetics contribute reliable growing characteristics, while the Haze influence demands additional attention to flowering duration and plant structure.

## Awards & Recognition

- Blue Dream Haze is a more recent creation and has not accumulated the extensive awards history of its parent strains

- Blue Dream Haze has gained recognition among cultivators seeking a more cerebral, creative alternative to standard Blue Dream
- The strain appears in dispensary menus across legal markets as a distinct offering from Blue Dream
