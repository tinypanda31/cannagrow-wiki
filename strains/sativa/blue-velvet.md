# Blue Velvet

![Blue Velvet](/images/strains/blue-velvet.png)

## Overview

Blue Velvet is a sativa-dominant hybrid that combines the legendary Blueberry genetics with classic Haze lineage, producing a strain that marries the sweet, fruity berry character of its Blueberry parent with the cerebral, creative energy of Haze. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, pinene, and caryophyllene, producing a distinctive berry, pine, and sweet aroma that sets it apart from both of its parent lines. Blue Velvet is valued for its ability to deliver the uplifting, imaginative stimulation of Haze while retaining the recognizable berry sweetness that has made Blueberry one of the most enduring genetics in cannabis history.

## Genetics & Lineage

Blue Velvet unites two iconic cannabis families:

- **Blueberry** -- A legendary indica-dominant strain originally bred by DJ Short in the 1970s, crossing Purple Thai, Thai, and Afghani genetics. Blueberry is renowned for its deep purple coloration, sweet berry aroma, and relaxed body effects
- **Haze** -- A foundational sativa landrace hybrid originating in California during the 1960s, crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian genetics. Haze is the genetic backbone of the modern sativa category

**Parent lineage:** Blueberry x Haze

The cross was designed to introduce the cerebral, creative energy of Haze into the sweet, fruity framework of Blueberry. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the berry sweetness and visual appeal of Blueberry while gaining the energetic, imaginative effects from the Haze genetics. The combination produces a cultivar with both recognizable heritage genetics and a distinctive sensory profile.

## Effects & Experience

Blue Velvet is widely reported to produce:

- **Creative stimulation** -- A noticeable increase in imaginative thinking and artistic energy, making it suitable for creative pursuits
- **Energy and alertness** -- A sustained uplift in wakefulness suitable for daytime use
- **Elevated mood** -- A cheerful, positive elevation that promotes optimism
- **Conversational sociability** -- Increased engagement and social flow

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, making Blue Velvet suitable for extended daytime sessions.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet berry with fresh pine undertones and a subtle spicy edge. The terpinolene contributes a floral, fruity sweetness, while the pinene adds a crisp, clean pine freshness. The caryophyllene provides a peppery depth that prevents the profile from being one-dimensionally sweet.
- **Taste:** Berry-forward on the inhale, with pine and slight spice on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and layered, with the Blueberry sweetness carrying through the Haze backbone.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the Haze genetics add complexity, but Blueberry contributes manageable structure |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- slightly longer due to Haze influence |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- Haze genetics contribute noticeable sativa stretch; height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions. The berry terpenes are most pronounced in warm, dry conditions with good light exposure. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Blueberry heritage may contribute purple coloration under cooler temperatures. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

Blue Velvet is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require height management during the flowering stretch, and the Haze influence extends the flowering period to 60-70 days. The Blueberry heritage contributes structural density and attractive coloration. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The berry and pine aromas during growing may require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.

## Awards & Recognition

- Blueberry was a High Times Cannabis Cup winner for Best Indica
- The Haze family has produced numerous award-winning cultivars over decades
- Blue Velvet remains a respected cultivar among growers who appreciate berry-flavored sativa-dominant strains
