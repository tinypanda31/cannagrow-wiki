# Bluehead

![Bluehead photo](/uploads/images/strains/bluehead.jpg)

## Overview

Bluehead is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the legendary fruity Blueberry with the intensely cerebral Neville's Haze. This combination produces a strain that marries the sweet, berry-forward flavor profile of Blueberry with the powerful, long-lasting sativa effects of one of the most potent Haze varieties ever created. Bluehead delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by myrcene, terpinolene, and pinene, producing a berry-citrus-pine aroma that is both sweet and complex. The strain is prized for its uplifting and cerebral effects, making it a popular choice for users seeking a powerful daytime sativa with excellent flavor.

## Genetics & Lineage

Bluehead combines two iconic genetics:

- **Blueberry** -- The legendary indica-dominant strain created by DJ Short, known for its distinctive berry aroma and flavor, derived from Afghani, Thai, and Purple Thai genetics
- **Neville's Haze** -- A pure sativa created by Mr. Nice Seeds from original Haze x Northern Lights #5, known for extremely cerebral and long-lasting effects

**Parent lineage:** Blueberry x Neville's Haze

The cross was designed to introduce the flavorful berry profile and some structural density of Blueberry into the powerful sativa framework of Neville's Haze. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid retains the intense cerebral effects of its Neville's Haze parent while gaining the sweet berry-citrus flavor from Blueberry.

## Effects & Experience

Bluehead is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting cerebral effects** -- A pronounced head high that is bright and cheerful
- **Energy and motivation** -- Increased drive and mental stimulation suitable for daytime activity
- **Creative thinking** -- Enhanced creative flow and mental exploration, inherited from the Haze genetics
- **Sociability** -- Increased social engagement and conversational energy

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The Neville's Haze heritage ensures a long-lasting, pronounced cerebral experience.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Berry and citrus with pine undertones. The Blueberry heritage produces a sweet, fruity aroma that is immediately recognizable, while the Haze genetics add a fresh, herbal complexity.
- **Taste:** Berry and citrus on the inhale, with sweet, pine, and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, appealing to users who appreciate complex, fruity cannabis profiles.

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or racing thoughts at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Dizziness
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day
- The Neville's Haze heritage can produce overly intense cerebral effects in sensitive users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to Challenging -- the Neville's Haze heritage requires patience and experience |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-75 days (indoor) -- longer flowering due to Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- significant sativa stretch; training and height management required |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with good air circulation. The longer flowering period requires consistent environmental management. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Blueberry heritage contributes some resilience, but the Haze genetics require attentive care. Responds well to SCROG and topping. |

Bluehead is moderately challenging to grow. The significant sativa stretch from the Neville's Haze parent requires height management, and the longer flowering time (65-75 days) demands patience. The Berry heritage contributes some structural density and resin production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. Growers should plan for significant vertical space or employ training techniques to manage height.
