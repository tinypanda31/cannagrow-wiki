# Bubba Kush x Haze

![Bubba Kush x Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/bubba-kush-x-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Bubba Kush x Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid that bridges two of the most genetically distinct families in cannabis breeding: the heavy, sedating Bubba Kush indica and the cerebral, energizing Haze sativa lineage. This cross produces a cultivar with a complex effect profile that begins with energetic, head-focused sativa effects before settling into a relaxing body finish contributed by the Bubba Kush parent. With a THC range of 16-21%, a terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, and sweet, earthy, and spicy aromatics, Bubba Kush x Haze offers a layered consumption experience that appeals to consumers seeking both mental stimulation and physical relaxation in a single cultivar.

## Genetics & Lineage

Bubba Kush x Haze is a deliberate hybrid cross between two legendary but genetically opposite cultivars.

**Parent lineage:** Bubba Kush x Haze

Bubba Kush is a famously heavy indica strain originating from the Los Angeles area, with genetics tracing back to an OG Kush phenotype crossed with an unknown indica from the Matteson, Louisiana area. It is known for its dense, resinous buds, sweet-and-earthy aroma, and deeply sedating body effects. Haze refers to the family of sativa cultivars developed in California during the 1960s and 1970s, originally combining South American (Colombian, Mexican) and Southeast Asian (Thai) landraces. Haze genetics are characterized by long flowering times, tall plant structure, and intensely cerebral, energizing effects. Crossing these two produces a sativa-dominant hybrid estimated at approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica that carries the cerebral energy of Haze with a measurable body relaxation from the Bubba Kush parent.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Share | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Terpinolene | 25-40% | Floral, herbal, citrus notes; drives the energetic, creative sativa effects |
| Caryophyllene | 10-20% | Spicy, peppery, woody depth; contributes to body relaxation |
| Myrcene | 8-15% | Earthy, musky undertones from the Bubba Kush parent; associated with calming effects |
| Pinene | 3-8% | Fresh pine notes that complement the terpinolene |

The terpene profile reflects both parent strains: terpinolene from the Haze side drives the cerebral energy, while myrcene and caryophyllene from the Bubba Kush side provide the grounding body relaxation. This dual-action profile is what creates the strain's characteristic two-phase effect.

## Effects & Experience

Bubba Kush x Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Initial energetic uplift** -- A burst of cerebral energy and mental alertness that arrives quickly
- **Creative engagement** -- Enhanced creative thinking and ideation during the sativa-dominant phase
- **Transitioning body relaxation** -- As the experience progresses, a gradual physical calmness sets in, softening the energy without eliminating it
- **Balanced euphoria** -- A sustained mood lift that bridges the cerebral and physical phases

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The two-phase effect means the experience evolves over time rather than remaining static.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The transition from energetic to relaxing effects means dosing should be approached carefully -- a dose that feels uplifting initially may become more sedating as the experience progresses.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with spicy undertones. The Haze genetics contribute a bright, floral sweetness while the Bubba Kush parent adds deep earthy and musky notes. The caryophyllene content introduces a noticeable peppery spice.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale with a spicy, slightly peppery exhale. The flavor carries the earthy-sweet character of Bubba Kush with the brighter, more floral notes of Haze layered on top. The finish is warm and slightly spicy.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Stress and tension** -- The two-phase effect is frequently cited for addressing both mental and physical stress

- **Depression and low mood** -- The energetic sativa onset is reported to improve mood and motivation
- **Afternoon or early evening use** -- The transition from energy to relaxation makes it suitable for use during the transition from work to evening

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety possible during the initial energetic phase, particularly in inexperienced users
- Dizziness when transitioning between the energetic and relaxing phases
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia possible at high doses
- Sedation if consumed in larger quantities or later in the day than intended

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience managing hybrid genetics with conflicting parent traits |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- longer than typical due to Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 350-500 g/m² indoor; 450-600 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- significant sativa stretch from Haze genetics |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, stable conditions with good airflow |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Haze genetics can make the plant more susceptible to humidity-related issues during flowering. Good air circulation is essential. |

Bubba Kush x Haze presents moderate growing challenges due to the conflicting characteristics of its parent strains. The Haze genetics drive a significant sativa stretch and longer flowering time, while the Bubba Kush influence promotes denser, more compact bud development. Growers should plan for height management through topping, LST, or SCROG techniques. The extended flowering time (60-70 days) rewards with resinous, aromatic buds displaying characteristics of both parents. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The strain benefits from consistent feeding and stable environmental conditions.
