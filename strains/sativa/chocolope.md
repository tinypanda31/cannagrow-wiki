# Chocolope

![Chocolope](/images/strains/chocolope.jpg)

## Overview

Chocolope is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the legendary Chocolate Thai landrace with Cannalope Haze, producing a strain that delivers a genuinely unique chocolate and coffee-like aroma alongside the powerful, focused sativa effects that have made it one of the most recognized cultivars in the cannabis industry. The strain delivers THC levels in the 17-22% range with a terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, terpinolene, and myrcene, producing a rich chocolate, earthy, and sweet aroma that is genuinely distinctive. Chocolope has won multiple Cannabis Cup awards and is valued for its ability to deliver clear, functional sativa energy with a sensory profile unlike almost anything else in the cannabis landscape.

## Genetics & Lineage

Chocolope unites two distinguished sativa lineages:

- **Chocolate Thai** -- A classic landrace sativa from Thailand, originally brought to the United States in the 1960s and 1970s. Chocolate Thai is legendary for its unique chocolate and coffee aroma, energetic effects, and its role as one of the foundational genetics of the modern cannabis industry
- **Cannalope Haze** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid crossing melon-flavored genetics with classic Haze, known for its sweet, fruity character and elevated sativa energy

**Parent lineage:** Chocolate Thai x Cannalope Haze

The cross was designed to combine the genuinely unique chocolate aroma of the Chocolate Thai landrace with the elevated sativa energy and yield of Cannalope Haze. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the unmistakable chocolate character of its Thai parent while gaining the structural vigor and energetic effects from the Cannalope Haze genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Chocolope is widely reported to produce:

- **Sharp focus and concentration** -- A noticeable increase in mental clarity and task-oriented attention
- **Sustained energy** -- A long-lasting, functional energy suitable for all-day use
- **Motivation** -- A drive to engage with tasks and projects with renewed enthusiasm
- **Mood elevation** -- A subtle, functional uplift that promotes a positive mindset without sedation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear and functional, making Chocolope one of the more productive sativa-dominant strains available.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Rich chocolate and coffee with earthy undertones and a sweet finish. The caryophyllene-myrcene combination gives it a warm, roasted quality that genuinely resembles dark chocolate or espresso, while the terpinolene adds a subtle floral layer that keeps the profile from being one-dimensional.
- **Taste:** Chocolate and earth on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and distinctive, with the chocolate character carrying through from aroma to taste.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the Haze influence adds complexity, but the genetics are generally resilient |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- typical for a sativa-dominant strain with Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor -- above average, thanks to Cannalope Haze contribution |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch from both parent lineages; training and height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. The chocolate terpene profile is most pronounced in warm, dry conditions with consistent light. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The sativa-dominant structure provides good airflow through the canopy. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

Chocolope is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require height management during the flowering stretch, and the Haze influence extends the flowering period. The strain is valued for its above-average yields, which are higher than many comparable sativa-dominant cultivars. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The unique chocolate aroma during growing is distinctive and may require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
