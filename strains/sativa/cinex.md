# Cinex

![Cinex](/images/strains/cinex.jpg)

## Overview

Cinex is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by the legendary breeder TGA Subcool as a cross between Cinderella 99 (C99) and Vortex. The strain was named as a portmanteau of its parent genetics -- "Cin" from Cinderella 99 and "ex" from Vortex. Cinex quickly gained a reputation as one of the most energizing and focus-promoting strains in the TGA lineup, delivering THC levels in the 15-22% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, limonene, and pinene. The result is a bright, citrus-forward strain with orange and pine notes that produces energetic, focused effects ideal for daytime use. Cinex is one of TGA Subcool's most celebrated creations and remains a benchmark for functional sativa genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Cinex was created by TGA Subcool from two of his own genetically rich lines:

- **Cinderella 99 (C99)** -- A legendary fast-flowering sativa created by Brothers Grimm from Shiva Skunk x Jack Herer genetics, refined through tissue culture to produce an extremely fast, potent sativa
- **Vortex** -- A TGA Subcool creation crossing Apollo 13 (itself a Jack Herer phenotype) with Jack the Ripper, producing a resinous, terpene-rich sativa

**Parent lineage:** Cinderella 99 x Vortex

Both parent strains carry Jack Herer genetics, meaning Cinex is deeply rooted in the Haze-influenced sativa tradition. TGA Subcool designed Cinex to combine the rapid flowering and clean cerebral effects of C99 with the complex terpene profile and resin production of Vortex.

## Effects & Experience

Cinex is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A strong, invigorating effect that promotes daytime activity and wakefulness
- **Mental focus** -- Enhanced concentration and mental clarity, making it popular among professionals and students
- **Uplifting mood lift** -- A bright, cheerful elevation that promotes positivity
- **Creative flow** -- Enhanced creative thinking without mental scattering

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and functional, making Cinex one of the best daytime sativas available.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Bright citrus and orange with pine undertones. The limonene-terpinolene combination gives it a fresh, sunny quality that is immediately recognizable and uplifting.
- **Taste:** Citrus and orange on the inhale, with pine and slightly floral notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and refreshing, appealing to users who appreciate bright, complex citrus profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the C99 heritage makes it more manageable than many sativas |
| **Flowering Time** | 50-60 days (indoor) -- remarkably fast for a sativa-dominant strain, inherited from Cinderella 99 |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the C99 heritage keeps height more manageable than pure sativas |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. The C99 heritage contributes resilience and reliability. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Cinex is moderately easy to grow for a sativa-dominant strain. The fast flowering time (50-60 days) inherited from Cinderella 99 is a significant advantage, making it one of the faster sativa-dominant strains available. The plant structure is manageable, and the Vortex heritage contributes heavy resin production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September. TGA Subcool's breeding work ensured that Cinex is genetically stable and reliable.
