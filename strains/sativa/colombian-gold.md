# Colombian Gold

![Colombian Gold](/images/strains/colombian-gold.png)

## Overview

Colombian Gold is a pure sativa landrace cultivar originating from the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta mountain region of northern Colombia. It is one of the most legendary and historically significant strains in cannabis culture, achieving iconic status during the 1960s and 1970s when it was among the most sought-after cannabis imports into the United States. Colombian Gold is celebrated for its distinctive golden-green bud coloration, sweet earthy aroma, and uplifting cerebral effects that define classic sativa character. The strain was a staple of the era's cannabis market, often sold in distinctive "bricks" that were compressed for transport. Its name references both its Colombian origin and the golden hue of its cured flowers. As a landrace, Colombian Gold represents cannabis genetics shaped by the unique microclimate of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta -- an isolated mountain range near the Caribbean coast.

## Genetics & Lineage

Colombian Gold is a **landrace strain** -- a naturally occurring cannabis population that adapted to the warm, humid tropical climate of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta in northern Colombia.

**Parent lineage:** Pure landrace -- no intentional crossbreeding

The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta is an isolated coastal mountain range that provides a unique microclimate for cannabis cultivation -- warm temperatures, high humidity, and significant elevation variation. Colombian Gold was cultivated in this region for generations before being exported in significant quantities during the mid-20th century. The strain was a cornerstone of the Colombian cannabis trade during the 1960s and 1970s and was among the first recognizable named strains many American consumers encountered. Its genetics have been incorporated into numerous modern hybrids, though pure Colombian Gold lines are increasingly difficult to source.

## Effects & Experience

Colombian Gold is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting cerebral effects** -- A bright, head-focused elevation that is characteristic of quality sativa landraces
- **Energy and alertness** -- A clean, stimulating effect suitable for daytime use
- **Euphoria** -- A cheerful, expansive mood lift
- **Creativity and sociability** -- Enhanced creative thinking and social engagement

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, well-suited to users seeking daytime stimulation without overwhelming intensity.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, earthy, and golden with subtle herbal and citrus undertones. The aroma is warm and inviting, with a characteristic earthiness that distinguishes it from fruit-forward modern hybrids.
- **Taste:** Sweet earthy flavor on the inhale with a smooth, golden quality, followed by mild herbal and citrus notes on the exhale. The taste is classic and refined, representing the traditional sativa flavor profile.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Difficult -- requires experience with sativa landrace characteristics and long flowering times |
| **Flowering Time** | 75-90+ days (indoor) -- very long, typical of equatorial sativa landraces |
| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall to very tall -- significant sativa stretch; substantial height management required indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Outdoor in warm, sunny climates strongly preferred; indoor possible with significant vertical space |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The long flowering time and height are the primary challenges. Responds to training but requires patience and expertise. |

Colombian Gold is a difficult strain to grow due to its pure sativa landrace characteristics. The very long flowering time (75-90+ days) demands significant patience and consistent environmental management over an extended period. The plant stretches dramatically during flowering, requiring substantial vertical space and training expertise. It performs best in warm, sunny outdoor conditions that approximate its native Colombian mountain habitat. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late October to November, and in some climates may be challenged by early frost. The plant can grow very large outdoors in appropriate climates.

## Awards & Recognition

- Colombian Gold achieved legendary status during the 1960s and 1970s as one of the most recognizable and prized cannabis imports from Colombia
- The strain is frequently referenced in cannabis history literature and accounts from that era
- Colombian cannabis bricks containing Gold phenotypes were considered premium product and helped establish the reputation of Colombian cannabis
- While it has not won modern Cannabis Cup awards, its historical and cultural significance is widely acknowledged
- Pure Colombian Gold genetics are increasingly rare and valued by landrace preservationists
