# Critical Plus

![Critical Plus](/images/strains/critical-plus.png)

## Overview

Critical Plus is a sativa-dominant hybrid developed by the renowned Spanish seed company Dinafem, crossing Skunk #1 with Afghani genetics to produce a strain that combines the reliable, fast-flowering framework of classic cultivars with an uplifting, energetic sativa-leaning effect. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and terpinolene, producing an earthy, sweet, and skunky aroma that connects directly to its foundational parentage. Critical Plus is valued for its accessible effects, fast flowering time, and the backing of one of the cannabis industry's most established breeders.

## Genetics & Lineage

Critical Plus unites two foundational cannabis genetics through Dinafem's breeding program:

- **Skunk #1** -- A legendary hybrid developed in the 1970s by crossing Afghan, Mexican, and Colombian landraces. Skunk #1 is widely considered one of the most influential genetics in modern cannabis history, responsible for introducing the term "skunk" as a cannabis descriptor and establishing the framework for virtually all hybrid breeding that followed
- **Afghani** -- A pure indica landrace from Afghanistan, known for its dense resin production, short flowering time, and heavy body effects. Afghani genetics are the backbone of the indica category and a key component in the development of modern hybrid cannabis

**Parent lineage:** Skunk #1 x Afghani

The cross was developed by Dinafem to produce a fast-flowering, high-yielding cultivar with accessible effects and the reliable genetics of two of the most foundational strains in the industry. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the recognizable skunky aroma from its Skunk #1 parent while gaining the rapid flowering and structural density from the Afghani genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Critical Plus is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting mood elevation** -- A cheerful, positive lift that promotes optimism and engagement
- **Energy and wakefulness** -- A functional, accessible energy suitable for daytime use
- **Social sociability** -- Increased conversational flow and social comfort
- **Light euphoria** -- A pleasant, warm euphoria that is present without being overwhelming

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are accessible and functional, making Critical Plus suitable for a wide range of users including those with lower tolerance.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy and sweet with a pronounced skunky backbone. The myrcene gives it a deep, herbal quality, while the caryophyllene adds a spicy edge that grounds the profile. The terpinolene contributes a subtle floral sweetness that keeps it from being one-dimensionally pungent.
- **Taste:** Earthy and skunky on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is classic and familiar, appealing to users who appreciate traditional cannabis profiles with recognizable character.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to Moderate -- the Afghani and Skunk #1 heritage make this one of the more forgiving sativa-dominant strains |
| **Flowering Time** | 45-55 days (indoor) -- exceptionally fast, among the quickest sativa-dominant strains available |
| **Yield** | 450-550 g/m² indoor; 550+ g/plant outdoor -- above average, a key selling point |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- the Afghani heritage contributes structural density and limits excessive stretch |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; adaptable to a range of conditions. The fast flowering time makes it suitable for shorter outdoor seasons. Prefers warm, dry conditions. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests, thanks to the resilient Afghani and Skunk #1 heritage. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping techniques. |

Critical Plus is one of the easier sativa-dominant strains to grow. Its standout feature is the exceptionally fast flowering time of 45-55 days, which is significantly shorter than most sativa-dominant cultivars and makes it suitable for growers with shorter growing seasons. The Afghani heritage contributes structural density and resilience. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The skunky aroma during growing is pronounced and may require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
