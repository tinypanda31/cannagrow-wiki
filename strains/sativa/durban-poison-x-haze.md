# Durban Poison x Haze

![Durban Poison x Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/durban-poison-x-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Durban Poison x Haze is a sativa cultivar created by crossing two of the most legendary and influential genetics in cannabis history -- Durban Poison and Haze. This cross represents a deliberate combination of pure South African sativa landrace character with the cerebral intensity of classic Haze breeding, producing a strain that amplifies the best qualities of both parents. The result is an extremely energetic and creative sativa with a sweet-anise-citrus aroma that combines Durban Poison's distinctive licorice sweetness with Haze's complex herbal-citrus depth. Durban Poison x Haze is best suited to experienced sativa users seeking maximum cerebral stimulation and creative energy.

## Genetics & Lineage

Durban Poison x Haze combines two foundational sativa genetic sources:

- **Durban Poison** -- The pure South African sativa landrace from Durban, contributing its distinctive terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, sweet anise aroma, and clean energizing effects
- **Haze** -- The classic sativa-dominant strain (believed to include Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian genetics), contributing cerebral intensity, creative effects, and complex terpene depth

**Parent lineage:** Durban Poison x Haze

The combination produces a strain that captures the sweet anise character of Durban Poison amplified by the cerebral complexity of Haze. Both parents are foundational genetics in cannabis breeding -- Durban Poison has contributed to Girl Scout Cookies, Cherry Pie, and numerous other hybrids, while Haze genetics underpin an enormous number of modern sativa-dominant cultivars. Crossing these two legends produces a strain that is both historically significant and practically valuable for its extremely energetic and creative effects.

## Effects & Experience

Durban Poison x Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Extremely energetic effects** -- A powerful, invigorating cerebral stimulation that is among the most energizing of any sativa strain
- **Creativity and mental clarity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and intellectual engagement, making it popular among artists, writers, and professionals
- **Euphoria** -- An expansive, cheerful mood lift that is bright and uplifting
- **Focus and sociability** -- Improved concentration and social engagement

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and functional, though the energetic quality can be pronounced for users sensitive to sativa stimulation.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet anise and licorice with citrus and herbal undertones. The Durban Poison genetics contribute the distinctive sweet-anise character, while the Haze adds herbal complexity and citrus brightness.
- **Taste:** Sweet anise and licorice on the inhale, with citrus and herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and layered, combining the classic Durban sweetness with the more complex Haze character.

| Attribute | Details |

| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |

| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Both parents contribute resilient genetics. Responds to topping, LST, and SCROG but requires significant vertical space. |

Durban Poison x Haze is a moderately to difficult strain to grow due to its pure sativa characteristics from both parents. The plant stretches significantly during flowering, requiring substantial vertical space and training expertise. The extended flowering time (65-80 days) demands patience and consistent environmental management. Both Durban Poison and Haze are known for their vigor and health, which this cross inherits. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The plant can grow large outdoors in warm climates.
