# Durban Venom

![Durban Venom photo](/uploads/images/strains/durban-venom.jpg)

## Overview

Durban Venom is a sativa-dominant hybrid cultivar that crosses two of the most influential strains in cannabis history: Durban Poison, the pure South African sativa landrace, and White Widow, the legendary Brazilian x South Indian hybrid that defined the Dutch coffee shop era of the 1990s. This combination produces a cultivar with a THC range of 17-22%, a terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, and a sweet anise-and-earthy aroma that blends the distinctive licorice character of Durban Poison with the earthy, woody notes of White Widow. Durban Venom is valued by consumers seeking clean, energizing sativa effects with the added resin production and growing resilience inherited from White Widow genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Durban Venom is a hybrid combining a pure sativa landrace with a legendary hybrid.

**Parent lineage:** Durban Poison x White Widow

Durban Poison is a pure sativa landrace from the port city of Durban in South Africa's KwaZulu-Natal province. It is characterized by a distinctive terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, sweet anise-and-licorice aroma, and clean, energizing effects. White Widow is a balanced hybrid created by Green House Seeds in the Netherlands, crossing a Brazilian sativa landrace with a South Indian indica. White Widow is known for its exceptional resin production (the "white" in its name refers to the thick white trichome coating), earthy and woody aroma, and balanced effects that combine cerebral energy with body relaxation. Crossing these two produces a sativa-dominant hybrid estimated at approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica that carries the pure sativa energy of Durban Poison with the resinous vigor and growing resilience of White Widow.

## Effects & Experience

Durban Venom is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic focus** -- A clean, alert mental state that supports sustained concentration and productivity
- **Mental clarity** -- A clear-headed quality that distinguishes it from more scattered sativa experiences
- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A bright, cheerful mood lift that accompanies the energizing effects
- **Functional body calm** -- A subtle physical relaxation that prevents the energy from becoming anxious or jittery

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are functional and clear-headed, making Durban Venom suitable for daytime use even by users with moderate tolerance.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Despite the "Venom" name, this strain is not inherently more potent or dangerous than similarly classified sativa-dominant hybrids.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet anise and licorice with earthy undertones. The terpinolene-dominant profile from Durban Poison creates the signature sweet, herbal aroma while White Widow adds earthy depth and a faint woody quality.
- **Taste:** Sweet anise and herbal notes on the inhale with earthy, slightly woody flavors on the exhale. The flavor carries the clean, smooth character of Durban Poison with deeper earthy notes from White Widow adding complexity.

- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or jitteriness at higher doses, particularly in users sensitive to energizing sativa effects
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia possible at high doses
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day
- Headaches reported by a small percentage of users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to Moderate -- White Widow genetics contribute resilience; Durban Poison adds straightforward growing characteristics |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- notably efficient for a Durban Poison cross |
| **Yield** | 400-550 g/m² indoor; 500-700 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- moderate sativa stretch; more manageable than pure Durban Poison |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; adaptable to various conditions. Prefers warm, sunny environments. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. White Widow genetics contribute strong disease resistance and overall plant vigor. The plant is vigorous and healthy with dense, resinous buds. |

Durban Venom benefits from the growing resilience of both parent strains. White Widow contributes hardiness and disease resistance, while Durban Poison is a vigorous landrace that adapts well to different environments. The plant exhibits a moderate sativa stretch more manageable than pure Durban Poison. Training techniques such as topping and LST are recommended to improve light penetration and yield. The dense, resinous buds develop a heavy trichome coating resembling the White Widow parent. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
