# Ghost Train Haze

![Ghost Train Haze](/images/strains/ghost-train-haze.png)

## Overview

Ghost Train Haze is a sativa-dominant cultivar created by crossing Ghost OG with Neville's Wreck. It is widely regarded as one of the most potent sativa strains available, with THC levels that frequently test in the 25-28% range -- among the highest recorded for any sativa-dominant cultivar. The strain was developed by Rare Dankness seeds and quickly gained recognition for its extremely intense cerebral effects, citrus-sour-pine aroma, and formidable growing characteristics. The name references both the ghostly, almost surreal potency of the experience and the Haze lineage that drives its cerebral character. Ghost Train Haze is best suited to experienced users who can handle its intense effects and to experienced growers who can manage its challenging cultivation requirements.

## Genetics & Lineage

Ghost Train Haze combines two potent genetic lines:

- **Ghost OG** -- A phenotype of the OG Kush family, contributing resin production, potency, and the "Ghost" naming heritage
- **Neville's Wreck** -- A Haze-dominant strain created by legendary breeder Neville Schoenmakers (of Sensi Seeds), combining Haze genetics with wrecking-ball potency

**Parent lineage:** Ghost OG x Neville's Wreck

The combination of OG Kush family genetics with Haze-dominant Neville's Wreck produces a strain that captures extreme THC levels while maintaining a distinctly cerebral, sativa-forward effect profile. The Haze influence from Neville's Wreck is dominant in the effects, while the OG heritage contributes resin density and structure.

## Effects & Experience

Ghost Train Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Extremely intense cerebral effects** -- An overwhelming, head-focused experience that is among the most potent of any sativa strain
- **Energy and stimulation** -- A powerful, invigorating effect that can be almost too intense for some users
- **Euphoria** -- An intense, expansive mood elevation
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking at moderate doses, though high doses may be too overwhelming for focused activity

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Very strong. Ghost Train Haze is among the most potent sativa strains available. Users should approach with caution and start with low doses, especially if inexperienced with high-THC sativa effects.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. This strain is extremely potent -- not suitable for inexperienced users. Start with a low dose. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus, sour, and pine with earthy undertones. The aroma is bright and sharp, with a sour-citrus edge that is immediately distinctive. The Haze influence adds herbal complexity.
- **Taste:** Citrus and sour flavors on the inhale, with pine and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is sharp and stimulating, matching the intense cerebral effect.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety and panic at moderate to high doses -- very common with this strain due to extreme potency
- Paranoia and racing thoughts
- Significantly increased heart rate
- Dizziness and disorientation
- Difficulty sleeping, potentially for an extended period after use
- Overwhelming cerebral effects that may be distressing to inexperienced users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Difficult -- requires experienced grower comfortable with tall, sativa-dominant plants |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-80 days (indoor) -- extended, typical of Haze-dominant genetics |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch; substantial height management required indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with excellent air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The tall stature and extended flowering time require experienced management. Responds to topping, LST, and SCROG but requires significant space. |

Ghost Train Haze is a difficult strain to grow due to its sativa-dominant characteristics. The plant stretches significantly during early flowering, requiring substantial vertical space and training expertise. The extended flowering time (65-80 days) demands patience and consistent environmental management. The plant performs best in warm, sunny conditions and can grow very large outdoors. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The extreme potency of the flowers requires careful handling during trimming and processing due to heavy resin production.
