# Green Crack

![Green Crack](/images/strains/green-crack.png)

## Overview

Green Crack is a sativa-dominant cultivar whose name was coined by Snoop Dogg after experiencing the strain's intense cerebral energy. The strain is a cross of Skunk #1 and an unknown indica variety. Despite its provocative name, Green Crack is widely regarded as one of the most effective daytime sativa strains for promoting energy, mental focus, and productivity. Its mango-and-citrus tropical flavor profile adds to its broad appeal. Some dispensaries and retailers market the strain under the alternative name "Green Cush" or "Mango Crack" to avoid the potentially off-putting original name, though "Green Crack" remains the most widely recognized designation.

## Genetics & Lineage

Green Crack's genetic origins are not fully documented:

- **Skunk #1** -- The classic hybrid (Afghani x Acapulco Gold x Colombian Gold) contributing reliable genetics and moderate flowering time
- **Unknown indica** -- The second parent has not been definitively identified, though it is widely understood to be an indica variety

**Parent lineage:** Skunk #1 x Unknown Indica

The strain was popularized in the 1990s and 2000s, gaining widespread recognition after Snoop Dogg's endorsement of the name. The exact breeder and origin location remain unclear, though the strain is associated with the California cannabis scene.

## Effects & Experience

Green Crack is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A strong, invigorating mental effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Focus and mental clarity** -- Improved concentration and task-oriented thinking, making it popular for work and creative projects
- **Euphoria** -- An uplifting, cheerful mood lift
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Moderate increase in hunger, though less pronounced than with indica strains

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The energizing effects are pronounced but functional, allowing users to remain productive and engaged.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Mango, citrus, and tropical fruit sweetness with earthy undertones. The aroma is bright and fruity without being overly candy-like.
- **Taste:** Mango and citrus flavors on the inhale, with earthy and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The tropical fruit flavor is distinct and enjoyable, making it a favorite among flavor-conscious users.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or jitteriness at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia possible at high doses, particularly in users sensitive to sativa effects
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience with sativa growing |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 550+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- sativa stretch is moderate; training recommended indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Vigorous grower. Responds well to topping and SCROG methods. Adequate vertical space is needed. |

Green Crack is a moderately challenging strain to grow. Its sativa characteristics mean it stretches during early flowering, requiring some height management. The Skunk #1 heritage contributes reliable genetics and a reasonable flowering time. The strain is a vigorous grower that responds well to training. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
