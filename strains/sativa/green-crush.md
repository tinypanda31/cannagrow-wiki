# Green Crush

![Green Crush photo](/uploads/images/strains/green-crush.jpg)

## Overview

Green Crush is a sativa-dominant hybrid cultivar with genetic ties to the Green Crack family, one of the most popular energizing sativa strains in modern cannabis culture. With a THC range of 16-21%, a terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, and a citrus-sweet-earthy aroma, Green Crush delivers clean, focused energy and mental clarity that makes it a staple daytime cultivar. The strain is valued by consumers seeking the characteristic Green Crack-style energizing effects with a potentially distinct phenotype expression that may offer subtle differences in flavor, aroma, and growing characteristics. The terpinolene-forward profile drives the creative, alert effects that define this cultivar's experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

Green Crush is a sativa-dominant hybrid with genetics connected to the Green Crack family.

**Parent lineage:** Green Crack x unknown sativa

Green Crack (also known as Green Cush or simply ISO) is a sativa-dominant hybrid with disputed genetics, most commonly described as a cross between Skunk #1 and an unknown Afghani indica. Despite the controversial name (which was reportedly coined by Snoop Dogg), the strain is valued for its intensely energizing effects and citrus-forward flavor profile. Green Crush represents a phenotype selection or cross within this genetic sphere, potentially incorporating additional unknown sativa genetics that shift the effect profile or growing characteristics. The exact breeding history is not well-documented, but the strain consistently expresses sativa-dominant effects estimated at approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica.

## Effects & Experience

Green Crush is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic focus** -- A clean, sustained mental energy that supports productivity and concentration throughout the day
- **Mental clarity** -- A clear-headed quality that distinguishes it from more scattered or anxious sativa experiences
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and ideation, making it popular among creative professionals
- **Mood elevation** -- A bright, cheerful effect that is frequently cited for improving low mood and motivation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are functional and sustaining rather than overwhelming, making Green Crush suitable for all-day daytime use.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Despite the name similarity to Green Crack, Green Crush may express subtle differences in effect profile depending on the specific phenotype.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus-forward with sweet and earthy undertones. The terpinolene content creates a bright, herbal-citrus aroma. The earthy undertones from myrcene and caryophyllene add depth and prevent the profile from being one-dimensionally fruity.
- **Taste:** Sweet citrus on the inhale with earthy, slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and smooth, with citrus most prominent on the palate and an earthy-spicy finish providing contrast.

- Dry eyes
