# Harlequin

![Harlequin](/images/strains/harlequin.jpg)

## Overview

Harlequin is a sativa-dominant high-CBD cultivar that has become one of the most important and widely recommended strains for medical cannabis patients seeking therapeutic relief without the intoxicating effects of high-THC cannabis. With a characteristic CBD:THC ratio of approximately 5:2, Harlequin provides clear-headed, functional relief that allows patients to remain active and productive throughout the day. The strain was created through a complex cross of Colombian Gold, Thai, a Swiss landrace, and a Nepali indica, combining diverse genetics to produce a unique cannabinoid profile. Harlequin's name references its multifaceted genetic heritage -- like the Harlequin character, it draws from multiple sources to create something distinctive.

## Genetics & Lineage

Harlequin's genetic composition draws from four distinct landrace and hybrid sources:

- **Colombian Gold** -- The legendary Colombian sativa landrace, contributing sativa structure and CBD genetics
- **Thai** -- Southeast Asian sativa landrace, adding cerebral sativa characteristics
- **Swiss landrace** -- European landrace genetics, contributing additional CBD expression
- **Nepali indica** -- Himalayan indica genetics, providing structure and balancing the sativa dominance

**Parent lineage:** Colombian Gold x Thai x Swiss Landrace x Nepali Indica

The combination of these diverse genetics was specifically selected to produce a high-CBD phenotype. The CBD-rich expression likely originates from the Swiss landrace and Colombian Gold components, while the sativa-dominant structure ensures the clear-headed, functional effect that defines Harlequin's medical utility.

## Cannabinoid Profile

| Cannabinoid | Typical Range |
|-------------|--------------|
| THC (Total) | 4-10% |
| CBD | 8-16% |
| CBD:THC Ratio | Approximately 5:2 (varies by phenotype) |
| CBG | 0.3-1.0% |
| CBN | <0.5% (increases with age) |

Harlequin's cannabinoid profile is its defining characteristic. The 5:2 CBD:THC ratio provides therapeutic benefits from both cannabinoids while minimizing THC's intoxicating effects. The CBD content moderates THC's psychoactive impact, producing a clear-headed, functional experience that is well-suited to daytime medical use. Phenotype selection is important -- some Harlequin pheno-hunts express higher CBD ratios than others.

## Effects & Experience

Harlequin is widely reported to produce:

- **Clear-headed relief** -- Noticeable therapeutic effects without significant intoxication, allowing users to remain functional and productive
- **Relaxation without sedation** -- A calming physical effect that does not impair mental clarity
- **Mild mood lift** -- A gentle, uplifting effect from the sativa genetics and modest THC content
- **Pain and inflammation reduction** -- The high CBD content combined with caryophyllene is frequently reported for physical relief

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The effects are subtle and functional rather than overwhelming, making Harlequin suitable for users who need to remain clear-headed during the day.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. CBD-rich strains like Harlequin produce significantly less intoxication than high-THC strains.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy and sweet with subtle mango and herbal undertones. The aroma is warm and inviting without being overpowering, reflecting the balanced cannabinoid profile.
- **Taste:** Earthy and mango-like on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and approachable, appealing to users who may be sensitive to strong cannabis taste.

## Medical Usesdanger
**Not Medical Advice** -- The following lists commonly reported therapeutic applications. This does not constitute medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before using cannabis therapeutically.

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Chronic pain** -- The high CBD content combined with caryophyllene is widely cited for significant pain relief without sedation
- **Inflammation** -- CBD and caryophyllene are both reported for anti-inflammatory effects
- **Anxiety** -- The high CBD:THC ratio is frequently recommended for anxiety relief, as CBD moderates THC's anxiety-producing effects
- **Seizures and epilepsy** -- CBD-rich strains are commonly used as part of seizure management protocols, though patients should consult their physician
- **Migraines** -- Reported to provide relief while maintaining daytime functionality
- **Arthritis** -- The anti-inflammatory quality is cited for joint pain and stiffness relief

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Mild dizziness (less common than with high-THC strains)
- Headache at higher doses
- The mild THC content means adverse effects are generally less severe than with high-THC strains
- Some phenotype selections may express higher THC levels, producing more intoxication than expected

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience with sativa-dominant growing but generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; more manageable than pure sativas |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The diverse genetic heritage contributes resilience. Responds well to topping and SCROG. |

Harlequin is a moderately challenging strain to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the Nepali indica heritage keeps the plant more compact than pure sativa varieties. The reasonable flowering time (55-65 days) is an advantage. For medical growers, phenotype selection is critical -- growers should test multiple seeds to identify plants expressing the desired high-CBD ratio. Phenotypes can vary significantly in their CBD:THC expression. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
