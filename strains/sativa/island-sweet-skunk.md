# Island Sweet Skunk

![Island Sweet Skunk](/images/strains/island-sweet-skunk.png)

## Overview

Island Sweet Skunk is a sativa-dominant hybrid cultivar that combines the foundational genetics of Skunk #1 with an unidentified sativa landrace to produce a sweet, tropical-leaning cultivar with uplifting and creative effects. With a THC range of 15-20%, a myrcene-dominant terpene profile, and a sweet-skunky-tropical aroma, Island Sweet Skunk appeals to consumers who appreciate the classic Skunk character enhanced with bright, tropical fruit notes. The strain's name reflects both its genetic heritage and its sensory profile, suggesting a cultivar developed in or adapted to island or tropical growing conditions that emphasizes the sweeter, fruitier expression of Skunk genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Island Sweet Skunk is a hybrid combining a foundational breeding strain with a sativa landrace.

**Parent lineage:** Skunk #1 x sativa landrace

Skunk #1 is one of the most influential hybrid cultivars in cannabis history, created by crossing Afghan indica, Mexican sativa, and Colombian sativa landraces into a single, stabilized hybrid. Developed by breeders including those at Sensi Seeds, Skunk #1 revolutionized cannabis breeding by producing a reliable, consistent, and pungent cultivar that became the genetic foundation for countless modern hybrids. Crossing Skunk #1 with an unidentified sativa landrace (potentially a Hawaiian, Thai, or other tropical sativa population) produces a sativa-dominant hybrid estimated at approximately 60% sativa / 40% indica that emphasizes the sativa characteristics of the Skunk #1 parent while adding the tropical fruit notes and pure sativa energy of the landrace parent.

## Effects & Experience

Island Sweet Skunk is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A bright, cheerful mood lift that arrives quickly and sets a positive tone
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and ideation, making it popular among artists, musicians, and writers
- **Gentle energy** -- A sustainable mental energy that is noticeable without being overwhelming or anxious
- **Light body relaxation** -- The myrcene content provides a subtle physical calmness that prevents overstimulation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-6 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 15-20% THC range and balanced terpene profile produce effects that are noticeable and pleasant without being overwhelming.

Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. Island Sweet Skunk is a moderate-potency sativa-dominant hybrid suitable for consumers with varying tolerance levels.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and skunky with tropical fruit undertones. The classic Skunk #1 pungency is present but softened by the sativa landrace parent, producing an aroma that is more tropical and fruity than purely skunky. The myrcene content creates a sweet, almost mango-like fruit character.
- **Taste:** Sweet tropical fruit on the inhale with skunky, earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor is more fruit-forward than the aroma, with genuine tropical fruit sweetness complemented by the classic earthy Skunk character.

Island Sweet Skunk is one of the most forgiving sativa-dominant hybrids, primarily due to its Skunk #1 heritage. The plant is vigorous, healthy, and resistant to most common growing issues. It adapts well to indoor, outdoor, and greenhouse environments. The moderate flowering time of 55-65 days is efficient for a sativa-dominant hybrid. The buds develop a moderate trichome coating and display the classic Skunk dense flower structure with tropical fruit aromas becoming more pronounced during curing. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The strain's resilience makes it an excellent choice for first-time growers.
