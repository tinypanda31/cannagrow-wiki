# Jack the Ripper (JTR)

![Jack The Ripper](/images/strains/jack-the-ripper.png)

## Overview

Jack the Ripper, also known as JTR, is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by the legendary breeder TGA Subcool. The strain is a cross of Jack's Cleaner (a Jack Herer phenotype) with Vortex (itself an Apollo 13 x Jack the Ripper backcross, meaning JTR carries deep Jack Herer genetics). Jack the Ripper delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, pinene, and caryophyllene, producing a bright citrus-pine aroma with peppery complexity. The strain is renowned for its fast-acting cerebral effects that are intense and immediate, making it one of the most potent and quick-onset sativas in the TGA lineup. Jack the Ripper is one of TGA Subcool's most celebrated creations and remains a benchmark for fast-acting sativa genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Jack the Ripper was created by TGA Subcool from his own genetically rich lines:

- **Jack's Cleaner** -- A Jack Herer phenotype selected for exceptional resin production and clean cerebral effects, carrying the Haze x Northern Lights #5 x Shiva Skunk heritage
- **Vortex** -- A TGA Subcool creation crossing Apollo 13 (itself a Jack Herer phenotype) with Jack the Ripper, producing a terpene-rich, resinous sativa

**Parent lineage:** Jack's Cleaner x Vortex

Both parent strains carry deep Jack Herer genetics, meaning Jack the Ripper is firmly rooted in the Haze-influenced sativa tradition. The repeated presence of Jack Herer genetics across both parent lines concentrates the cerebral, creative effects that define the strain. TGA Subcool designed JTR to be one of the most fast-acting and potent sativas in his catalog.

## Effects & Experience

Jack the Ripper is widely reported to produce:

- **Fast-acting cerebral effects** -- A rapid-onset head high that is notably quick even among sativa strains
- **Energy and intensity** -- A strong, invigorating effect that promotes immediate daytime activity
- **Euphoria and mood lift** -- An intense, cheerful mood elevation
- **Creative stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; notably fast-acting even among sativa strains. Oral onset is 30-90 minutes.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Jack the Ripper delivers potent, fast-acting effects that are best suited to experienced sativa users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus, pine, and peppery with floral undertones. The terpinolene-pinene combination gives it a fresh, bright quality that is immediately recognizable, with the caryophyllene adding a spicy edge.
- **Taste:** Citrus and pine on the inhale, with peppery and slightly floral notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and complex, appealing to users who appreciate bright, layered sativa profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the TGA genetics are generally stable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; training recommended for indoor spaces |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. The TGA breeding work contributed genetic stability and resilience. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Jack the Ripper is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the reasonable flowering time (55-65 days) is an advantage. The TGA Subcool breeding program ensured genetic stability, making JTR a reliable cultivar. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The heavy resin production inherited from the Jack's Cleaner parent makes this strain particularly suitable for extraction.
