# LA Confidential Haze

![LA Confidential Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/la-confidential-haze.jpg)

## Overview

LA Confidential Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the well-known LA Confidential genetics with classic Haze lineage, producing a strain that combines the smooth, refined character of its LA Confidential parent with the powerful, focused cerebral energy of Haze. The strain delivers THC levels in the 17-22% range with a terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, terpinolene, and myrcene, producing a pine, earthy, and spicy aroma that bridges the two parent lineages. LA Confidential Haze is valued for its ability to deliver the clean, focused sativa energy of Haze while maintaining the accessible, polished character of its LA Confidential parent.

## Genetics & Lineage

LA Confidential Haze unites a modern hybrid classic with a foundational sativa:

- **LA Confidential** -- A well-known indica-dominant hybrid developed by DNA Genetics, crossing OG LA Affie with Afghani genetics. LA Confidential is recognized for its smooth, refined effects and has been widely used in breeding programs to introduce accessible potency and structural quality into hybrid crosses
- **Haze** -- A foundational sativa landrace hybrid originating in California during the 1960s, crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian genetics. Haze is the genetic backbone of the modern sativa category and provides the cerebral energy that defines the sativa experience

**Parent lineage:** LA Confidential x Haze

The cross was designed to introduce the powerful, focused cerebral energy of Haze into the smooth, refined framework of LA Confidential. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the accessible, polished character of its LA Confidential parent while gaining the focused, energetic effects from the Haze genetics.

## Effects & Experience

LA Confidential Haze is widely reported to produce:

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Pine-forward with earthy undertones and a distinct spicy edge. The caryophyllene contributes a peppery warmth, while the terpinolene adds a subtle floral layer that keeps the profile from being one-dimensional. The myrcene provides an herbal backbone connecting to the LA Confidential heritage.
- **Taste:** Pine and earth on the inhale, with spicy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional, earth-forward cannabis profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the Haze influence adds complexity, but the LA Confidential heritage contributes manageable structure |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- typical for a sativa-dominant strain with Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- noticeable sativa stretch from Haze genetics; height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. The pine and earthy terpene profile is most pronounced in warm, dry conditions with consistent light. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The sativa-dominant structure provides good airflow through the canopy. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

LA Confidential Haze is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require height management during the flowering stretch, and the Haze influence extends the flowering period to 60-70 days. The LA Confidential heritage contributes some structural density that helps manage the sativa stretch. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The pine and earthy aromas during growing are moderate and may require standard carbon filtration in indoor setups.
