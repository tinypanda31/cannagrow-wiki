# Lamb's Bread

![Lamb's Bread photo](/uploads/images/strains/lambs-bread.jpg)

## Overview

Lamb's Bread (also spelled Lamb's Breath) is a legendary sativa strain originating from Jamaican landrace genetics. The strain is most famously associated with Bob Marley, who was reported to be a frequent consumer of Lamb's Bread. The name references the traditional Jamaican practice of preparing cannabis in bread-like formations for consumption. Lamb's Bread delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, caryophyllene, and myrcene, producing a sweet, earthy, and herbal aroma that reflects its Caribbean landrace origins. The strain is prized for its uplifting and spiritual effects, making it one of the most culturally significant sativa strains in cannabis history.

## Genetics & Lineage

Lamb's Bread's genetic composition traces to Jamaican landrace genetics:

- **Jamaican landrace** -- Indigenous sativa genetics that developed naturally in Jamaica's tropical climate over generations, contributing the strain's characteristic uplifting and spiritual effects
- The exact genetic composition of modern Lamb's Bread varies, as the original Jamaican landrace has been adapted and stabilized by various breeders outside of Jamaica

**Parent lineage:** Jamaican Landrace genetics (specific modern breeding varies)

The original Lamb's Bread was grown in Jamaica's warm, humid tropical climate, and the landrace genetics reflect centuries of natural selection in that environment. Modern versions of Lamb's Bread available outside Jamaica are typically stabilized selections from the original landrace, adapted for cultivation in different climates while attempting to preserve the original effect profile and aroma.

## Effects & Experience

Lamb's Bread is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting euphoria** -- A bright, cheerful mood lift that is immediately noticeable and promotes positivity
- **Spiritual and meditative qualities** -- The landrace sativa genetics are frequently reported to produce a reflective, spiritually-oriented head space
- **Energy and sociability** -- Increased energy and social engagement, making it suitable for communal settings
- **Creative stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and spiritual, making Lamb's Bread suitable for users seeking a meaningful, reflective sativa experience.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to Challenging -- landrace genetics require attention to environmental conditions |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-75 days (indoor) -- longer flowering typical of landrace sativas |
| **Yield** | 300-400 g/m² indoor; 400+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch; landrace sativas tend to grow large and require significant height management indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, humid conditions mimicking the Jamaican tropical climate. Outdoor growing in warm climates produces the best results. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Landrace genetics are naturally resilient but adapted to tropical conditions. Responds well to topping and SCROG. |

Lamb's Bread is moderately challenging to grow. The landrace sativa genetics require significant height management, and the longer flowering time (65-75 days) demands patience. The strain performs best in warm, humid conditions that approximate its Jamaican origins. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October, and the strain excels in warm climates. Indoor growers should plan for significant vertical space or employ aggressive training techniques.
