# Lemon Iced Tea

![Lemon Iced Tea photo](/uploads/images/strains/lemon-iced-tea.jpg)

## Overview

Lemon Iced Tea is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the popular Lemon Skunk with an Iced Tea variety (itself an unknown sativa), producing a strain that delivers a genuinely refreshing lemon and citrus aroma alongside the bright, cheerful sativa effects that make it an ideal daytime cultivar. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by limonene, terpinolene, and caryophyllene, producing a lemon, citrus, and sweet aroma that lives up to its beverage-inspired name. Lemon Iced Tea is valued for its cheerful, happy effects and a sensory profile that genuinely evokes a cold, refreshing citrus drink on a warm day.

## Genetics & Lineage

Lemon Iced Tea combines citrus-forward genetics with sativa energy:

- **Lemon Skunk** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid developed by the Greenhouse Seed Co., selecting Lemon Skunk phenotype crosses to produce an intensely citrus-forward cultivar. Lemon Skunk is recognized for its powerful lemon aroma and cheerful, energetic sativa effects, making it a popular parent in citrus-cross breeding programs
- **Iced Tea** -- An unknown sativa variety that contributes the sativa-dominant structure and cerebral energy. The Iced Tea parent is not widely documented, but its name suggests a smooth, refreshing quality that complements the citrus character of the Lemon Skunk parent

**Parent lineage:** Lemon Skunk x Iced Tea (Unknown Sativa)

The cross was designed to intensify the citrus, lemon-forward character of Lemon Skunk while introducing the smooth, refreshing energy associated with the Iced Tea parent. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the powerful lemon aroma of its Lemon Skunk parent while gaining the cheerful, happy effects from the Iced Tea sativa genetics.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Powerful lemon and citrus with a sweet, refreshing finish. The limonene delivers an intense lemon character that genuinely evokes the aroma of a cold lemon drink, while the terpinolene adds a subtle floral brightness. The caryophyllene provides a light spicy edge that keeps the profile from being one-dimensionally citrus.
- **Taste:** Lemon and citrus on the inhale, with sweet and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is genuinely refreshing and smooth, with the lemon character carrying through from aroma to taste.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the sativa-dominant characteristics require standard management |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Lemon Skunk heritage |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- the Lemon Skunk heritage contributes manageable sativa stretch |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. The lemon terpene profile is most pronounced in warm, dry conditions with consistent light exposure. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The sativa-dominant structure provides good airflow through the canopy. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

Lemon Iced Tea is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require standard height management during the flowering stretch, and the Lemon Skunk heritage contributes a manageable flowering period of 55-65 days. The strain benefits from warm, sunny conditions that intensify the lemon terpene production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The powerful lemon aroma during growing is pronounced and requires effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
