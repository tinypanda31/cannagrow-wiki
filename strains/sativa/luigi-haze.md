# Luigi Haze

![Luigi Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/luigi-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Luigi Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses classic Haze genetics with an unknown sativa variety, producing a strain that delivers the powerful, imaginative cerebral energy of Haze alongside a sweet, earthy, and spicy aroma profile. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, caryophyllene, and pinene, producing a sweet, earthy, and spicy aroma that connects to traditional Haze breeding while maintaining its own distinctive identity. Luigi Haze is valued for its creative, energetic effects and its embodiment of the pure Haze sativa experience.

## Genetics & Lineage

Luigi Haze combines foundational sativa genetics with an unknown partner:

- **Haze** -- A foundational sativa landrace hybrid originating in California during the 1960s, crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian genetics. Haze is the genetic backbone of the modern sativa category, responsible for the cerebral, energetic effects that define the sativa experience and the parent of countless award-winning cultivars
- **Unknown sativa** -- The second parent is an unspecified sativa variety, contributing additional sativa characteristics. The exact identity varies between breeders, meaning Luigi Haze genetics can differ between sources

**Parent lineage:** Haze x Unknown Sativa

The cross was designed to intensify the cerebral, creative energy of Haze by crossing it with an additional sativa variety. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the powerful, imaginative effects of its Haze parent while gaining additional sativa energy and structural characteristics from the unknown partner. The combination produces a cultivar that represents the pure Haze sativa experience.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with a distinct spicy edge. The terpinolene contributes a floral, slightly fruity sweetness, while the caryophyllene adds a peppery warmth that grounds the profile. The pinene delivers a clean pine freshness that keeps the aroma bright and inviting.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with spicy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional Haze profiles with a sweet character.

Luigi Haze is moderately challenging to grow. Its Haze genetics result in significant height and an extended flowering period of 65-80 days, which requires patience and experience. The sativa-dominant characteristics demand proactive height management through training and canopy control. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late October to November. The sweet and earthy aromas during growing are moderate and may require standard carbon filtration in indoor setups.
