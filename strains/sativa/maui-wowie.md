# Maui Wowie

![Maui Wowie](/images/strains/maui-wowie.png)

## Overview

Maui Wowie is a pure sativa landrace cultivar originating from the Hawaiian Islands, specifically the island of Maui where it was grown in the volcanic soil and tropical climate of the region's valleys and hillsides. As a landrace strain, Maui Wowie represents cannabis genetics that adapted naturally to the unique Hawaiian environment over many generations, developing a distinctive pinene-dominant terpene profile, tropical-pine-sweet aroma, and THC range of 13-19%. The strain holds legendary cultural significance in Hawaiian cannabis history and is one of the most famous tropical sativa landraces in the world. Its effects are characteristically energetic, creative, and uplifting, reflecting the pure sativa genetics shaped by decades of Hawaiian growing conditions.

## Genetics & Lineage

Maui Wowie is a **landrace strain** -- a naturally occurring cannabis population that adapted to the tropical Hawaiian environment over many generations.

**Parent lineage:** Pure landrace -- no intentional crossbreeding

Maui Wowie's origins trace back to cannabis plants brought to the Hawaiian Islands that naturalized in the volcanic soil, warm temperatures, and high humidity of Maui's tropical valleys. Over many generations, these plants adapted to the specific Hawaiian conditions, developing a pinene-dominant terpene profile, longer flowering time typical of equatorial sativas, and the ability to thrive in tropical environments. The strain was brought to the mainland United States during the 1970s and 1980s, where it gained fame for its tropical flavor and clean sativa effects. Maui Wowie has contributed genetics to numerous tropical and Hawaiian-themed hybrids.

## Effects & Experience

Maui Wowie is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic uplift** -- A clean, tropical sativa energy that is bright and motivating
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and artistic engagement, popular among musicians and visual artists
- **Mood elevation** -- A cheerful, sunny effect that is frequently compared to the feeling of a Hawaiian vacation
- **Mental clarity** -- Clear-headed focus that supports productivity without the scatteriness some sativas produce

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The 13-19% THC range produces effects that are noticeable and pleasant without being overwhelming, making Maui Wowie accessible to consumers with varying tolerance levels.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. As a landrace strain, Maui Wowie's moderate THC range reflects natural growing conditions rather than modern selective breeding for maximum potency.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Tropical and piney with sweet undertones. The pinene dominance creates a fresh, forest-like aroma layered with sweet tropical fruit notes. The overall scent is reminiscent of a Hawaiian rainforest -- fresh pine, sweet flowers, and warm tropical air.
- **Taste:** Sweet tropical fruit on the inhale with pine and earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor carries tropical sweetness with the fresh pine character of pinene providing a clean, cooling finish. The taste is smooth and approachable with a faint citrus hint.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience with pure sativa characteristics and longer flowering times |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-80 days (indoor) -- longer flowering time typical of equatorial sativa landraces |
| **Yield** | 300-450 g/m² indoor; 500-800+ g/plant outdoor in tropical climates |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch; height management is essential indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Outdoor in tropical or warm climates is ideal. Indoor growing requires significant vertical space. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Adapted to high-humidity tropical environments, which confers some mold resistance, but indoor growers in humid climates should maintain good airflow. |

Maui Wowie presents moderate growing challenges due to its pure sativa characteristics. The plant exhibits a significant sativa stretch during early flowering, reaching considerable heights that require management indoors through topping, LST, or SCROG techniques. The extended flowering time of 65-80 days requires patience but is typical of equatorial sativa landraces. The plant thrives in warm, sunny environments with moderate to high humidity. Outdoor growing in tropical or subtropical climates produces the best results, with plants reaching large sizes and substantial yields. Outdoor harvest in tropical climates can occur year-round; in the Northern Hemisphere temperate zones, harvest typically falls in late October to November. The buds develop a moderate trichome coating and display the elongated, airy flower structure characteristic of sativa landraces.
