# Moby Dick

![Moby Dick](/images/strains/moby-dick.png)

## Overview

Moby Dick is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by the Spanish seed bank Dinafem Seeds as a cross of White Widow with a Northern Lights x Haze hybrid. Named for its massive size and potency, Moby Dick delivers THC levels in the 18-24% range with a terpene profile dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and terpinolene, producing an earthy, citrus, and spicy aroma. The strain is renowned for its potent cerebral effects that are both intense and long-lasting, making it one of the most powerful sativa-dominant strains in the Dinafem catalog. Moby Dick combines the heavy resin production of White Widow with the cerebral Haze genetics to create a strain that is as productive for hash and extract production as it is for recreational and medical use.

## Genetics & Lineage

Moby Dick was created by Dinafem Seeds from three iconic genetics:

- **White Widow** -- The legendary Dutch hybrid created by Green House Seeds in the 1990s (Brazilian Sativa x South Indian Indica), known for extreme resin production and balanced effects
- **Northern Lights x Haze** -- A classic sativa-dominant cross combining the Afghani indica Northern Lights with the original Haze sativa genetics

**Parent lineage:** White Widow x (Northern Lights x Haze)

The cross was designed by Dinafem to combine the legendary resin production of White Widow with the potent cerebral effects of the Northern Lights x Haze combination. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid is named for its massive potential -- both in terms of physical size and potency. The Northern Lights heritage contributes structural density, the Haze genetics provide cerebral intensity, and the White Widow genetics ensure heavy resin coverage.

## Effects & Experience

Moby Dick is widely reported to produce:

- **Potent cerebral effects** -- A strong, pronounced head high that is intense and long-lasting
- **Energy and motivation** -- Increased drive and mental stimulation suitable for daytime activity
- **Euphoria and mood lift** -- A cheerful elevation that promotes positivity
- **Creative stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration inherited from the Haze genetics

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Moby Dick delivers potent, long-lasting effects that are best suited to experienced sativa users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Earthy, citrus, and spicy with herbal undertones. The myrcene-caryophyllene combination gives it a warm, organic quality with a bright citrus edge from the terpinolene and limonene.
- **Taste:** Earthy and citrus on the inhale, with spicy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is rich and layered, appealing to users who appreciate complex, multi-dimensional sativa profiles.

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or racing thoughts at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia, particularly in users sensitive to potent sativa effects
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day
- The potency means effects can be overwhelming for low-tolerance users

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the Dinafem genetics are generally stable and reliable |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 500-600 g/m² indoor; 600+ g/plant outdoor -- among the higher-yielding sativa-dominant strains |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- significant sativa stretch; training recommended for indoor spaces |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. Performs well in both environments. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. The Northern Lights heritage contributes resilience. The White Widow heritage contributes heavy resin production, making it excellent for extraction. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Moby Dick is moderately challenging to grow but is considered one of the more productive sativa-dominant strains. The high yield (500-600 g/m² indoor) is a significant advantage, and the reasonable flowering time (60-70 days) is manageable. The heavy resin production inherited from White Widow makes this strain particularly suitable for hash and extract production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The plant can grow large outdoors in warm climates, and Dinafem's breeding work ensured genetic stability.
