# Northern Lights #5 x Haze

![Northern Lights #5 x Haze photo](/uploads/images/strains/northern-lights-5-x-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Northern Lights #5 x Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by Sensi Seeds, one of the most respected seed companies in the cannabis industry, crossing the legendary Northern Lights #5 phenotype with classic Haze genetics. This strain represents one of the most historically significant sativa-dominant crosses in the industry, uniting two of the most influential genetics in cannabis history. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, caryophyllene, and pinene, producing a sweet, earthy, and pine aroma that bridges the sweet Northern Lights character with the sharp Haze profile. Northern Lights #5 x Haze is valued for its historically significant genetics, its balanced sativa-dominant effects, and the backing of Sensi Seeds' decades of breeding expertise.

## Genetics & Lineage

Northern Lights #5 x Haze unites two foundational cannabis genetics through Sensi Seeds' breeding program:

- **Northern Lights #5** -- A specific phenotype of the Northern Lights strain, a pure indica landrace originally from Afghanistan and later refined in the Pacific Northwest of the United States. Northern Lights #5 was selected for its superior resin production, structural quality, and sweet, earthy aroma. Northern Lights is one of the most awarded strains in cannabis history and a foundational indica genetics
- **Haze** -- A foundational sativa landrace hybrid originating in California during the 1960s, crossing Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian genetics. Haze is the genetic backbone of the modern sativa category

**Parent lineage:** Northern Lights #5 x Haze

The cross was created by Sensi Seeds to produce a cultivar that combines the resinous, sweet character of Northern Lights #5 with the cerebral, creative energy of Haze. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the sweet, earthy aroma of its Northern Lights parent while gaining the energetic, imaginative effects from the Haze genetics. This cross is one of the most historically significant sativa-dominant cultivars in the industry.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet and earthy with a distinct pine backbone. The terpinolene contributes a subtle floral sweetness, while the pinene delivers a clean, fresh pine aroma that connects to the Northern Lights heritage. The caryophyllene adds a peppery edge that provides depth and prevents the profile from being one-dimensionally sweet.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with pine and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional cannabis profiles with both sweet and sharp characteristics.
