# Pineapple Haze

![Pineapple Haze](/images/strains/pineapple-haze.png)

## Overview

Pineapple Haze is a sativa-dominant hybrid cultivar that combines tropical Pineapple genetics with the legendary Haze sativa family to produce a fruit-forward, energizing cultivar with a THC range of 16-21%, a terpinolene-dominant terpene profile, and a tropical-pine-sweet aroma. The strain bridges the sweet, tropical fruit character of its Pineapple parent with the intensely cerebral, creative energy of Haze genetics, creating a cultivar that appeals to consumers seeking both a distinctive sensory experience and functional sativa effects. The extended flowering time of 65-80 days reflects the Haze genetics and is a trade-off for the complex terpene profile and energizing effects that define this cultivar.

## Genetics & Lineage

Pineapple Haze is a hybrid combining tropical fruit genetics with the Haze sativa family.

**Parent lineage:** Pineapple x Haze

The Pineapple parent (sometimes referred to as Pineapple Kush or Pineapple phenotype) is a tropical fruit-forward cultivar with genetics that may involve OG family lines and tropical sativa varieties. It is characterized by a distinctive sweet, tropical fruit aroma and a myrcene-influenced terpene profile that contributes to a relaxing body effect alongside its sativa characteristics. Haze refers to the family of sativa cultivars developed in California during the 1960s and 1970s, originally combining South American (Colombian, Mexican) and Southeast Asian (Thai) landraces. Haze genetics are characterized by long flowering times, tall plant structure, and intensely cerebral, energizing effects. Crossing these two produces a sativa-dominant hybrid estimated at approximately 65% sativa / 35% indica that carries the tropical fruit sweetness of the Pineapple parent with the clean, creative energy of Haze genetics.

## Terpene Profile

| Terpene | Approximate Share | Notes |
|---------|------------------|-------|
| Terpinolene | 25-40% | Dominant terpene; floral, herbal, citrus notes; primary driver of energetic, creative sativa effects |
| Myrcene | 10-20% | Earthy, musky, tropical fruit notes from the Pineapple parent; contributes subtle body relaxation |
| Caryophyllene | 5-10% | Spicy, peppery depth that adds complexity to the tropical fruit profile |
| Limonene | 3-8% | Citrus brightness that enhances the tropical fruit character |

The terpinolene-dominant profile drives the characteristic Haze-family sativa energy, while the myrcene content from the Pineapple parent adds a subtle body relaxation that prevents the experience from becoming overly stimulating. This balance is what makes Pineapple Haze a functional daytime cultivar rather than an intensely cerebral one.

## Effects & Experience

Pineapple Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Energetic uplift** -- A bright, motivating mental energy that supports daytime activity and engagement
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and ideation, popular among artists, musicians, and writers
- **Mood elevation** -- A cheerful, sunny effect that is frequently cited for improving low mood
- **Gentle body relaxation** -- The myrcene content provides a subtle physical calmness that rounds out the sativa energy

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are functional and bright, making Pineapple Haze suitable for daytime use across a range of tolerance levels.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience. The extended flowering time of this strain reflects its Haze genetics, which also contribute to the intensity and duration of effects.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Tropical pineapple sweetness with pine and herbal undertones. The terpinolene content creates a bright, floral-citrus aroma layered with sweet tropical fruit character. The overall scent is sweet, fruity, and immediately recognizable as a tropical sativa.
- **Taste:** Sweet pineapple and tropical fruit on the inhale with pine and slightly earthy notes on the exhale. The flavor carries genuine tropical fruit sweetness complemented by the herbal-pine character of Haze genetics. The finish is smooth with a faint spicy quality and lingering tropical fruit aftertaste.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience with Haze genetics and longer flowering management |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-80 days (indoor) -- extended due to Haze lineage |
| **Yield** | 300-450 g/m² indoor; 400-600 g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch from Haze genetics; height management essential indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with stable environment |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Haze genetics can make the plant more susceptible to humidity-related issues during flowering. Good air circulation and humidity control are essential. |

Pineapple Haze requires moderate growing experience, primarily due to the extended flowering time and significant sativa stretch inherited from its Haze genetics. The plant benefits greatly from topping, LST, and SCROG to manage height and improve light penetration. The flowering time of 65-80 days requires patience but rewards with dense, highly aromatic buds displaying the tropical-pine character of both parents. The buds develop a moderate-to-heavy trichome coating with the tropical fruit aroma becoming increasingly pronounced during late flowering and curing. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October.
