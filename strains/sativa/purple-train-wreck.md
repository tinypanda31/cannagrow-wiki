# Purple Train Wreck

![Purple Train Wreck photo](/uploads/images/strains/purple-train-wreck.jpg)

## Overview

Purple Train Wreck is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the legendary Trainwreck with Mendocino Purps, producing a strain that combines the intense, energetic sativa power of Trainwreck with the purple coloration and grape character of Mendocino Purps. The strain delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, caryophyllene, and myrcene, producing a spicy, grape, and lemon aroma that is genuinely distinctive. Purple Train Wreck is valued for its creative, energetic effects, its visually appealing purple coloration, and a sensory profile that bridges the spicy intensity of Trainwreck with the fruity sweetness of Mendocino Purps.

## Genetics & Lineage

Purple Train Wreck unites two respected California cultivars:

- **Trainwreck** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid originating in Northern California's Emerald Triangle, with genetics believed to include Mexican, Thai, and Afghani landraces. Trainwreck is recognized for its intense, fast-acting sativa effects and its spicy, pine, and lemon aroma. It is one of the most influential genetics in Northern California cannabis breeding and a parent of numerous popular cultivars
- **Mendocino Purps** -- A well-known indica-dominant hybrid from Mendocino County, California, known for its deep purple coloration, grape and berry aroma, and relaxed body effects. Mendocino Purps carries the purple gene and is valued for its visual appeal and sweet, fruity profile

**Parent lineage:** Trainwreck x Mendocino Purps

The cross was designed to combine the intense, energetic sativa power of Trainwreck with the purple coloration and grape sweetness of Mendocino Purps. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the spicy, energetic character of its Trainwreck parent while gaining the purple coloration and grape-lemon sweetness from the Mendocino Purps genetics.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Spicy and grape with a lemon edge. The terpinolene contributes a floral, slightly fruity sweetness, while the caryophyllene delivers a peppery warmth connecting to the Trainwreck heritage. The grape character from Mendocino Purps is present, and the limonene adds a bright lemon edge that keeps the profile complex and engaging.
- **Taste:** Spicy and grape on the inhale, with lemon and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and genuinely distinctive, with both the Trainwreck spice and the Mendocino Purps grape sweetness carrying through.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the Trainwreck heritage contributes resilience, while the Mendocino Purps adds visual appeal |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Trainwreck heritage |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- the Trainwreck heritage contributes noticeable sativa stretch; height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm conditions with good light exposure. Cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering enhance the purple coloration inherited from Mendocino Purps. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The sativa-dominant structure provides good airflow through the canopy. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

Purple Train Wreck is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require height management during the flowering stretch, and the Trainwreck heritage contributes a reasonable flowering period of 55-65 days. The Mendocino Purps contribution adds purple coloration that is enhanced by cooler nighttime temperatures during flowering. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The spicy, grape, and lemon aromas during growing are pronounced and may require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
