# Scooby Snacks

![Scooby Snacks photo](/uploads/images/strains/scooby-snacks.jpg)

## Overview

Scooby Snacks is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses the popular Animal Cookies with Cinex, producing a strain that combines the sweet, dessert-like profile of its Cookies lineage with the bright, citrus-forward energy of Cinex. The strain delivers THC levels in the 17-22% range with a terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, terpinolene, and limonene, producing a sweet, earthy, and vanilla aroma that is reminiscent of its namesake treat. Scooby Snacks is prized for its ability to deliver the physical comfort and sweetness of Cookies genetics while maintaining the cerebral energy and functionality of a sativa-dominant cultivar, making it a popular choice for users who want the best of both worlds.

## Genetics & Lineage

Scooby Snacks combines Cookies genetics with Cinex energy:

- **Animal Cookies** -- A popular hybrid created by Jigga Farms crossing Girl Scout Cookies (GSC) with Fire OG, known for its sweet, doughy aroma and potent effects. Animal Cookies has become one of the most influential Cookies-family genetics in modern cannabis
- **Cinex** -- A sativa-dominant hybrid crossing Cinderella 99 with Voodoo, known for its bright, citrus-forward profile and energetic, uplifting sativa effects. Cinex is valued for its clean, functional energy and sweet, fruity character

**Parent lineage:** Animal Cookies x Cinex

The cross was designed to introduce the bright, citrus-forward energy of Cinex into the sweet, dessert-like framework of Animal Cookies. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid retains the dessert-like aroma and flavor profile of its Cookies parent while gaining the uplifting, energetic effects from the Cinex genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Scooby Snacks is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A noticeable increase in energy and wakefulness suitable for daytime use
- **Uplifting mood lift** -- A bright, cheerful elevation that promotes positivity
- **Euphoria** -- A pleasant, warm euphoria inherited from the Cookies genetics
- **Sociability** -- Increased social engagement and conversational energy

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are cheerful and accessible, making Scooby Snacks suitable for a wide range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, earthy, and vanilla with dessert-like undertones. The caryophyllene-myrcene combination gives it a warm, baked-good quality that is immediately recognizable and appealing, with the limonene from Cinex adding a bright, cheerful edge that distinguishes it from heavier Cookies crosses.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with vanilla and slightly citrus notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and dessert-like, appealing to users who appreciate sweet, complex cannabis profiles.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the Cinex heritage makes it generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the Cookies heritage contributes some structural density |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with good air circulation. The sweet terpene profile is most pronounced in warm, dry conditions. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Cinex heritage contributes some resilience. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Scooby Snacks is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the reasonable flowering time (55-65 days) is an advantage. The Cookies heritage contributes structural density and heavy resin production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The sweet aroma during growing may require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
