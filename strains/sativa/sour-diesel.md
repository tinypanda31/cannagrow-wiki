# Sour Diesel (East Coast Sour Diesel)

![Sour Diesel](/images/strains/sour-diesel.jpg)

## Overview

Sour Diesel, also known as East Coast Sour Diesel (ECSD), is a sativa-dominant cultivar that originated in New York City in the early 1990s. It is widely understood to be a cross of Chemdawg 91 and Super Skunk, though some accounts of its origins vary. Sour Diesel quickly became one of the most iconic and recognizable strains on the East Coast and eventually worldwide, celebrated for its unmistakable diesel-and-citrus aroma, fast-acting cerebral effects, and energizing, functional high. The strain has become a cultural touchstone, referenced extensively in hip-hop and popular media, and remains one of the most requested sativa strains in dispensaries across the United States.

## Genetics & Lineage

The origins of Sour Diesel are intertwined with the broader Chemdawg family:

- **Chemdawg 91** -- A specific Chemdawg phenotype from the legendary Chemdawg lineage (origins traced to a bag of cannabis purchased at a Grateful Dead concert in 1991)
- **Super Skunk** -- A Skunk #1 selection, contributing reliable genetics and moderate flowering

**Parent lineage:** Chemdawg 91 x Super Skunk

Sour Diesel was developed in New York City in the early 1990s, and its rise is closely tied to the East Coast cannabis scene. The Chemdawg connection also links Sour Diesel to a broader family that includes OG Kush and countless other influential strains. The exact breeding history has some uncertainty, as is common with strains from this era, but the Chemdawg 91 x Super Skunk account is the most widely accepted.

## Effects & Experience

Sour Diesel is widely reported to produce:

- **Fast-acting cerebral effects** -- A rapid-onset head high that is intense and immediate
- **Energy and alertness** -- A strong, invigorating effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Euphoria and mood lift** -- An intense, cheerful mood elevation
- **Creativity and sociability** -- Enhanced creative thinking and social engagement

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; notably fast-acting even among sativa strains. Oral onset is 30-90 minutes.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. Sour Diesel delivers potent, fast-acting effects that are best suited to experienced users.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Diesel fuel, citrus, and pungent skunk with earthy undertones. The diesel aroma is immediate and unmistakable -- it is the defining characteristic of Sour Diesel and one of the most recognizable smells in cannabis.
- **Taste:** Diesel and citrus on the inhale, with a pungent, slightly skunky exhale. The flavor is bold and assertive, appealing to users who appreciate strong, distinctive cannabis taste.

Commonly reported uses include:

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Anxiety or racing thoughts at higher doses
- Increased heart rate
- Paranoia, particularly in users sensitive to potent sativa effects
- Difficulty sleeping if used late in the day
- The pungent aroma can be difficult to mask during growing

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience with sativa cultivation |
| **Flowering Time** | 65-75 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- sativa stretch is significant; training recommended indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with good air circulation. The strong aroma requires effective carbon filtration indoors. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The long flowering period requires consistent environmental management. Responds well to topping and SCROG. Odor control is a significant consideration. |

Sour Diesel is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa characteristics require height management, and the long flowering time (65-75 days) demands patience. The most notable growing consideration is odor control -- the diesel-pungent aroma is extremely strong and requires effective carbon filtration and sealed growing spaces. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October. The plant can grow large outdoors in warm climates.
