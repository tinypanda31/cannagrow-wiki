# Sour Tsunami

![Sour Tsunami](/images/strains/sour-tsunami.jpg)

## Overview

Sour Tsunami is a sativa-dominant high-CBD cultivar created by the late Lawrence Ringo (also known as Lawrence Tsunami), one of the most important figures in the high-CBD cannabis breeding movement. The strain is a cross of Sour Diesel with NYC Diesel CBD, combining the iconic diesel-citrus profile of the Diesel family with significant CBD expression. Sour Tsunami delivers CBD levels in the 10-15% range with THC in the 5-10% range, producing a balanced CBD:THC ratio that provides clear-headed therapeutic relief with minimal intoxication. The strain features a terpene profile dominated by caryophyllene, humulene, and limonene, producing a diesel, citrus, and earthy aroma. Sour Tsunami is one of the pioneering strains in the medical CBD movement and represents Lawrence Ringo's legacy of developing cannabis cultivars specifically for therapeutic purposes.

## Genetics & Lineage

Sour Tsunami combines Diesel genetics with CBD expression:

- **Sour Diesel** -- The iconic sativa-dominant strain (Chemdawg 91 x Super Skunk) known for its diesel-citrus aroma and fast-acting cerebral effects
- **NYC Diesel CBD** -- A high-CBD phenotype of NYC Diesel (itself a grapefruit x Mexican Sativa cross), selected for elevated CBD expression

**Parent lineage:** Sour Diesel x NYC Diesel CBD

Lawrence Ringo created Sour Tsunami as part of his broader mission to develop high-CBD cultivars that could provide therapeutic relief without the intoxicating effects of high-THC cannabis. The Diesel family genetics were chosen for their reliable structure and distinctive terpene profile, while the CBD expression was introduced through phenotype selection of NYC Diesel. The result is a sativa-dominant strain that carries the recognizable diesel-citrus character while delivering significant CBD content.

Sour Tsunami's cannabinoid profile provides therapeutic levels of both CBD and THC while keeping THC's intoxicating effects moderated by CBD. The CBD:THC ratio varies by phenotype, and growers are encouraged to test multiple plants to identify those expressing the desired ratio. The modest THC content means Sour Tsunami produces mild intoxication that is significantly tempered by the CBD content.

## Effects & Experience

Sour Tsunami is widely reported to produce:

- **Clear-headed relief** -- Noticeable therapeutic effects with minimal intoxication, allowing users to remain largely functional and productive
- **Physical relaxation** -- A calming physical effect that reduces tension without significant sedation
- **Mild mood lift** -- A gentle, uplifting effect from the sativa genetics and modest THC content
- **Sustained functionality** -- The effects support daytime use with only mild cognitive impact

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Mild to moderate. The effects are functional and therapeutic, with the CBD content significantly moderating THC's intoxicating impact.

:::warning
Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. CBD-rich strains like Sour Tsunami produce significantly less intoxication than high-THC strains. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.
:::

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Diesel, citrus, and earthy with herbal undertones. The caryophyllene-limonene combination gives it a pungent, diesel-forward quality with a bright citrus edge inherited from the Diesel lineage, while the humulene adds earthy depth.
- **Taste:** Diesel and citrus on the inhale, with earthy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is bold and recognizable, appealing to users who appreciate the classic Diesel taste profile.

## Medical Uses

:::danger
**Not Medical Advice** -- The following lists commonly reported therapeutic applications. This does not constitute medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional before using cannabis therapeutically.
:::

Commonly reported uses include:

- **Chronic pain** -- The high CBD content combined with caryophyllene and humulene is widely cited for significant pain relief with minimal intoxication
- **Inflammation** -- CBD, caryophyllene, and humulene are all reported for anti-inflammatory effects
- **Anxiety** -- The CBD:THC ratio is frequently recommended for anxiety relief, as CBD moderates THC's anxiety-producing effects
- **Seizures and epilepsy** -- CBD-rich strains are commonly used as part of seizure management protocols, though patients should consult their physician
- **Migraines** -- Reported to provide relief while maintaining reasonable daytime functionality
- **Arthritis** -- The anti-inflammatory quality is cited for joint pain and stiffness relief
- **Nausea** -- The modest THC content can address nausea while the CBD keeps users functional

## Adverse Effects

Commonly reported adverse effects:

- Dry mouth
- Dry eyes
- Mild dizziness (less common than with high-THC strains)
- Headache at higher doses
- The modest THC content means adverse effects are generally less severe than with high-THC strains
- Some phenotype selections may express higher THC levels, producing more intoxication than expected -- testing is essential

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires experience with sativa-dominant growing and phenotype selection |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Diesel genetics |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 450+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the Diesel heritage contributes manageable structure |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, dry conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The Diesel heritage contributes some resilience. Responds well to topping and SCROG. Odor control is a consideration due to the diesel aroma. |

Sour Tsunami is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the reasonable flowering time (60-70 days) is manageable. For medical growers, phenotype selection is critical -- growers should test multiple seeds to identify plants expressing the desired high-CBD, moderate-THC ratio. Phenotypes can vary significantly in their CBD:THC expression. The diesel aroma during growing is strong and may require effective carbon filtration. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October.
