# Strawberry Fields

![Strawberry Fields](/images/strains/strawberry-fields.png)

## Overview

Strawberry Fields is a sativa-dominant cultivar that emerged from the Pacific Northwest cannabis scene with unknown genetic origins. The strain is named for its distinctive strawberry-sweet aroma and creative, uplifting effects that evoke the strain's namesake Beatles song. Unlike many modern cultivars with well-documented breeding histories, Strawberry Fields appeared as a distinct phenotype in the Pacific Northwest and gained popularity through the regional cannabis community for its unique flavor profile and reliable sativa effects. Its unknown genetics add an element of mystery to a strain that has otherwise earned recognition for its approachable strawberry flavor and functional creative energy.

## Genetics & Lineage

Strawberry Fields has **unknown genetic origins** -- the strain emerged as a distinct phenotype in the Pacific Northwest cannabis community without documented breeding records.

**Parent lineage:** Unknown -- emerged as a distinct phenotype in the Pacific Northwest

The Pacific Northwest (particularly the states of Washington and Oregon) has a long history of cannabis cultivation dating back decades before legalization. Strawberry Fields likely emerged from the region's rich breeding community, where growers have long exchanged and refined genetics. The strawberry flavor profile suggests possible genetic connection to strawberry-flavored varieties such as Strawberry Cough or other berry-forward cultivars, though this has not been confirmed. The sativa-dominant effects and moderate flowering time suggest breeding that included both sativa landrace and hybrid genetics.

## Effects & Experience

Strawberry Fields is widely reported to produce:

- **Uplifting effects** -- A bright, cheerful mood lift that promotes daytime positivity
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and artistic engagement, making it popular among creative professionals
- **Energy and alertness** -- A moderate stimulating effect suitable for daytime activity
- **Sociability** -- Increased social engagement and conversation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, well-suited to users seeking daytime creative stimulation without overwhelming intensity.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet strawberry with earthy undertones. The strawberry aroma is genuine and recognizable, making this one of the more convincing berry-flavored strains. The earthy base prevents the sweetness from being cloying.
- **Taste:** Strawberry and sweet flavors on the inhale, with earthy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The berry flavor is distinct and pleasant, appealing to users who enjoy fruity strain profiles.

Strawberry Fields is a moderately challenging strain to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the reasonable flowering time (60-70 days) makes it more manageable than pure sativa landraces. The plant stretches during early flowering, requiring training techniques. The strain performs well in both indoor and outdoor environments, and its Pacific Northwest origins suggest good adaptation to the region's growing conditions. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October.
