# Super Lemon Haze

![Super Lemon Haze](/images/strains/super-lemon-haze.jpg)

## Overview

Super Lemon Haze is a sativa-dominant cultivar created by crossing Lemon Skunk with Super Silver Haze. Developed by the Dutch breeders at Green House Seeds, Super Lemon Haze is one of the most awarded and decorated sativa-dominant strains in modern cannabis history, with multiple High Times Cannabis Cup wins to its name. The strain is celebrated for its intense lemon-citrus aroma, energetic and creative effects, and reliable growing characteristics. Super Lemon Haze represents the pinnacle of Dutch Haze breeding -- combining the classic cerebral intensity of Haze genetics with the approachable citrus sweetness that gives the strain its name and broad consumer appeal.

## Genetics & Lineage

Super Lemon Haze combines two influential genetic lines:

- **Lemon Skunk** -- A Skunk #1 selection emphasizing lemon-citrus terpene expression, contributing the intense citrus flavor and reliable growing characteristics
- **Super Silver Haze** -- The multi-Cannabis Cup-winning Haze hybrid (Skunk x Northern Lights x Haze), contributing cerebral intensity, creative effects, and proven genetics

**Parent lineage:** Lemon Skunk x Super Silver Haze

The combination produces a strain that captures the best of both parents -- the approachable lemon-citrus flavor of Lemon Skunk amplified by the legendary cerebral character of Super Silver Haze. The result is a sativa-dominant hybrid (approximately 70% sativa / 30% indica) that delivers both intense effects and broad consumer appeal.

## Effects & Experience

Super Lemon Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A strong, invigorating cerebral effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and artistic engagement, making it popular among creative professionals
- **Euphoria** -- An uplifting, cheerful mood lift characteristic of quality Haze genetics
- **Sociability** -- Increased social engagement and conversation

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and functional, suitable for a wide range of users seeking daytime creative stimulation.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Intense lemon and citrus with sweet undertones. The limonene expression is among the most pronounced of any strain, producing a genuine, immediate lemon-candy aroma that is immediately recognizable.
- **Taste:** Lemon and citrus flavors on the inhale, with sweet and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is bright and refreshing, with a lemon-candy quality that is distinctive and widely appealing.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience with sativa-dominant cultivation but generally manageable |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a Haze-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 400-550 g/m² indoor; 600+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- sativa stretch is moderate; training recommended indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate to good resistance to mold and pests. The Green House Seeds breeding emphasizes reliable genetics. Responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG. The strong citrus aroma requires effective carbon filtration indoors. |

Super Lemon Haze is a moderately challenging strain to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the Super Silver Haze genetics contribute reliable growing characteristics. The reasonable flowering time (60-70 days) for a Haze-dominant strain is an advantage. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The plant can grow large outdoors in warm climates. The intense citrus aroma requires effective odor control during cultivation.
