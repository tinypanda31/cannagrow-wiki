# Super Silver Haze

![Super Silver Haze](/images/strains/super-silver-haze.png)

## Overview

Super Silver Haze (SSH) is a sativa-dominant cultivar created by crossing Skunk, Northern Lights, and Haze genetics. Developed by Green House Seeds in the Netherlands, SSH quickly distinguished itself by winning the High Times Cannabis Cup three consecutive years (1997, 1998, and 1999) -- a feat matched by very few strains in history. It captures the cerebral, energizing character of Haze while the Skunk and Northern Lights parentage contribute structure, resin production, and a more manageable flowering time. SSH remains a benchmark for quality sativa effects and a favorite among users seeking energetic, euphoric daytime cannabis.

## Genetics & Lineage

Super Silver Haze is a three-way cross:

- **Skunk** -- The classic hybrid contributing reliable genetics and moderate flowering time
- **Northern Lights** -- The legendary indica contributing structure, resilience, and faster flowering
- **Haze** -- The original sativa powerhouse (Colombian x Mexican x Thai x South Indian) contributing cerebral, creative effects

**Parent lineage:** Skunk x Northern Lights x Haze

Green House Seeds developed SSH in the 1990s with the goal of creating a Haze-dominant strain that retained the signature cerebral Haze effects while being somewhat more practical to cultivate. The Northern Lights and Skunk contributions shortened the flowering time compared to pure Haze and added yield and resin production. SSH has been widely distributed and cloned, and multiple phenotype selections exist across different growers.

## Effects & Experience

Super Silver Haze is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A strong, invigorating cerebral effect that promotes daytime activity and engagement
- **Euphoria** -- A bright, intense mood lift that is cheerful and uplifting
- **Creativity and sociability** -- Enhanced creative thinking and social engagement, making it popular for social settings and creative work
- **Appetite stimulation** -- Moderate increase in hunger

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Strong. The effects are pronounced and long-lasting, making SSH best suited to experienced sativa users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Citrus, earthy, and spicy with herbal undertones. The terpinolene-caryophyllene combination gives it a bright, sharp aroma with a spicy depth that is distinctive of quality Haze genetics.
- **Taste:** Citrus and earthy on the inhale, with spicy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and classic, with a pleasant citrus brightness.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Difficult -- requires experience with sativa cultivation and patience with longer flowering |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) |
| **Yield** | 450-550 g/m² indoor; 600+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Tall -- significant sativa stretch; height management is essential indoors |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions. Needs vertical space. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. Vigorous grower but requires experience. Responds well to SCROG and extensive training. Nutrient sensitivity requires attention. |

Super Silver Haze is a challenging strain to grow, primarily due to its significant sativa height characteristics and longer flowering time. The plant stretches considerably during early flowering, requiring aggressive training (SCROG, topping, supercropping) to manage height indoors. The Haze genetics bring some nutrient sensitivity and environmental demands. However, the Northern Lights and Skunk heritage provides more resilience than pure Haze varieties. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in mid to late October, and the plant can grow very large in outdoor settings.
