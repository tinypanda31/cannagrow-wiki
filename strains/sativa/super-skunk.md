# Super Skunk

![Super Skunk](/images/strains/super-skunk.jpg)

## Overview

Super Skunk is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by Sensi Seeds, crossing the legendary Skunk #1 with Afghani genetics to produce a strain that amplifies the already powerful skunky character of its Skunk #1 parent while adding the resinous, structural quality of Afghani. The strain delivers THC levels in the 14-19% range with a terpene profile dominated by myrcene, caryophyllene, and terpinolene, producing a skunky, earthy, and sweet aroma that is bold, pungent, and instantly recognizable. Super Skunk is valued for its uplifting, energetic effects, its historically significant genetics, and the backing of Sensi Seeds' decades of breeding expertise.

## Genetics & Lineage

Super Skunk unites two foundational cannabis genetics through Sensi Seeds' breeding program:

- **Skunk #1** -- A legendary hybrid developed in the 1970s by crossing Afghan, Mexican, and Colombian landraces. Skunk #1 is widely considered one of the most influential genetics in modern cannabis history, responsible for introducing the term "skunk" as a cannabis descriptor and establishing the framework for virtually all hybrid breeding that followed
- **Afghani** -- A pure indica landrace from Afghanistan, known for its dense resin production, short flowering time, and heavy body effects. Afghani genetics are the backbone of the indica category and a key component in the development of modern hybrid cannabis

**Parent lineage:** Skunk #1 x Afghani

The cross was created by Sensi Seeds to intensify the already powerful qualities of Skunk #1 by backcrossing it with Afghani genetics. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the bold, skunky aroma of its Skunk #1 parent while gaining the rapid flowering, structural density, and resin production from the Afghani genetics. Super Skunk represents an amplification of the original Skunk #1 formula.

## Effects & Experience

Super Skunk is widely reported to produce:

- **Social sociability** -- Increased conversational flow and social comfort

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Bold skunky and earthy with a sweet finish. The myrcene gives it a deep, herbal quality, while the caryophyllene adds a spicy edge that grounds the profile. The terpinolene contributes a subtle floral sweetness that keeps it from being one-dimensionally pungent. The skunky character is pronounced and unmistakable.
- **Taste:** Skunky and earthy on the inhale, with sweet and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is classic and familiar, appealing to users who appreciate traditional cannabis profiles with recognizable character.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Easy to Moderate -- the Afghani and Skunk #1 heritage make this one of the most forgiving sativa-dominant strains available |
| **Flowering Time** | 45-55 days (indoor) -- exceptionally fast, among the quickest sativa-dominant strains available |
| **Yield** | 450-550 g/m² indoor; 550+ g/plant outdoor -- above average, a key selling point |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- the Afghani heritage contributes structural density and limits excessive stretch |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; highly adaptable to a range of conditions. The fast flowering time makes it suitable for shorter outdoor seasons. Prefers warm, dry conditions. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests, thanks to the resilient Afghani and Skunk #1 heritage. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping techniques. |

Super Skunk is one of the easiest sativa-dominant strains to grow. Its standout features are the exceptionally fast flowering time of 45-55 days and the high yields, both of which make it suitable for growers of all experience levels. The Afghani heritage contributes structural density and resilience. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September to early October. The bold skunky aroma during growing is pronounced and requires effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
