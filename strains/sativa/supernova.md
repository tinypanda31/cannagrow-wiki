# Supernova

![Supernova](/images/strains/supernova.png)

## Overview

Supernova is a sativa-dominant hybrid that crosses Northern Lights #5 with classic Haze genetics, producing a strain that combines the reliable, resinous structure of Northern Lights with the intensely cerebral effects of original Haze. The name Supernova references the explosive, expansive quality of the strain's cerebral effects. Supernova delivers THC levels in the 16-21% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, caryophyllene, and pinene, producing a sweet, earthy, and pine aroma that bridges the classic indica and sativa aromatic profiles. The strain is prized for its energetic and creative effects, making it a popular choice for users seeking a functional sativa with the growing reliability of Northern Lights genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Supernova combines two foundational genetics:

- **Northern Lights #5** -- A specific Northern Lights phenotype selected for its Afghani indica structure, reliable genetics, and heavy resin production. Northern Lights #5 is one of the most influential genetics in modern cannabis breeding.
- **Haze** -- The classic sativa genetics originating from the 1960s California scene, believed to include Colombian, Mexican, Thai, and South Indian landrace genetics

**Parent lineage:** Northern Lights #5 x Haze

The cross follows the classic formula that has produced many of the most successful sativa-dominant hybrids in cannabis history: combining Haze's cerebral intensity with Northern Lights' structural reliability and resin production. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid captures the creative, energetic head space of Haze while gaining the manageable growing characteristics and resin coverage of Northern Lights #5. This genetic combination is the same foundational approach used in several landmark sativa-dominant strains, including Jack Herer.

## Effects & Experience

Supernova is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A noticeable increase in energy and wakefulness suitable for daytime use
- **Creative stimulation** -- Enhanced creative thinking and mental exploration, inherited from the Haze genetics
- **Uplifting mood lift** -- A bright, cheerful elevation that promotes positivity
- **Functional cerebral high** -- The effects are pronounced but allow users to remain productive and engaged in tasks

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate to strong. The effects are clear-headed and functional, making Supernova suitable for a wide range of users.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Sweet, earthy, and pine with herbal undertones. The terpinolene-pinene combination gives it a fresh, forest-like quality that is bright and inviting, while the caryophyllene from the Northern Lights heritage adds an earthy, spicy foundation.
- **Taste:** Sweet and earthy on the inhale, with pine and slightly spicy notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional sativa taste with added depth from the Northern Lights genetics.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the Northern Lights heritage makes it more manageable than pure Haze varieties |
| **Flowering Time** | 60-70 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the Northern Lights #5 heritage keeps height more manageable than pure sativas |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. Performs well in both environments. |
| **Resistance** | Good resistance to mold and pests. The Northern Lights #5 heritage contributes significant resilience and reliability. Responds well to SOG, SCROG, and topping. |

Supernova is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the Northern Lights #5 heritage keeps the plant more compact and manageable than pure Haze varieties. The reasonable flowering time (60-70 days) is an advantage inherited from the Northern Lights genetics. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The heavy resin production from the Northern Lights parent makes this strain suitable for extraction.
