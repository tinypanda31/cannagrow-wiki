# Tangerine Dream

![Tangerine Dream](/images/strains/tangerine-dream.png)

## Overview

Tangerine Dream is a sativa-dominant hybrid created by Barney's Farm, the renowned Amsterdam seed company, crossing G13, Afghan Neville's A5 Haze, and Tangerine genetics to produce a strain that delivers a genuinely powerful tangerine and citrus aroma alongside the creative, energetic sativa effects. The strain delivers THC levels in the 15-20% range with a terpene profile dominated by terpinolene, limonene, and caryophyllene, producing a tangerine, citrus, and earthy aroma that is bold, fruity, and instantly recognizable. Tangerine Dream is valued for its creative, energetic effects, its distinctive citrus sensory profile, and the backing of Barney's Farm's established breeding expertise.

## Genetics & Lineage

Tangerine Dream unites three distinguished genetic lines through Barney's Farm breeding program:

- **G13** -- A legendary, mysterious genetics often described as a government-bred super strain. While the exact origins are debated, G13 is known for its potent effects, heavy resin production, and its role as a foundational genetics in numerous breeding programs. G13 has been used as a parent in many well-known cultivars
- **Afghan Neville's A5 Haze** -- A Haze-dominant hybrid developed by Neville Schoenmakers, the "Father of Cannabis Breeding," crossing Afghan landrace with classic Haze genetics. Neville's A5 Haze is one of the most historically significant sativa-dominant cultivars and a cornerstone of the Amsterdam seed bank tradition
- **Tangerine** -- A citrus-forward variety selected for its powerful tangerine and orange aroma, contributing the defining sensory character of Tangerine Dream

**Parent lineage:** G13 x Afghan Neville's A5 Haze x Tangerine

The cross was created by Barney's Farm to combine the potent, resinous qualities of G13 with the cerebral, creative energy of Neville's A5 Haze and the powerful tangerine aroma of the Tangerine genetics. The resulting sativa-dominant hybrid inherits the creative, energetic effects of its Haze parent while gaining the distinctive tangerine citrus profile that defines the strain.

## Effects & Experience

Tangerine Dream is widely reported to produce:

- **Creative stimulation** -- A noticeable increase in imaginative thinking and artistic energy, inherited from the Haze lineage
- **Energy and alertness** -- A sustained, functional uplift in wakefulness suitable for daytime use
- **Elevated mood** -- A cheerful, positive elevation that promotes optimism and creative engagement
- **Mental clarity** -- A clear-headed effect that allows for productive engagement with creative and analytical tasks

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear and creative, making Tangerine Dream suitable for extended creative sessions.

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Powerful tangerine and citrus with an earthy backbone. The limonene delivers an intense tangerine character that genuinely evokes the aroma of fresh-peeled citrus fruit, while the terpinolene adds a subtle floral brightness. The caryophyllene provides a light spicy edge that grounds the profile and prevents it from being one-dimensionally fruity.
- **Taste:** Tangerine and citrus on the inhale, with earthy and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is smooth and genuinely citrus-forward, with the tangerine character carrying through from aroma to taste.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- the Haze influence adds complexity, but the G13 heritage contributes resilience |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- reasonable for a sativa-dominant strain with Haze genetics |
| **Yield** | 400-500 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor -- above average, benefiting from the G13 and Haze contributions |
| **Plant Height** | Medium to tall -- noticeable sativa stretch from Haze genetics; height management recommended |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation. The tangerine terpene profile is most pronounced in warm, dry conditions with consistent light exposure. |
| **Resistance** | Moderate resistance to mold and pests. The sativa-dominant structure provides good airflow through the canopy. Responds well to SCROG, topping, and LST techniques. |

Tangerine Dream is moderately challenging to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require height management during the flowering stretch, and the Haze influence extends the flowering period to 55-65 days. The G13 heritage contributes structural density and heavy resin production. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in early to mid-October. The powerful tangerine and citrus aromas during growing are pronounced and require effective carbon filtration in indoor setups.
