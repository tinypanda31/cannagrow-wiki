# Williams Wonder

![Williams Wonder photo](/uploads/images/strains/williams-wonder.jpg)

## Overview

Williams Wonder is a sativa-dominant cultivar created by crossing Northern Lights #2 with an unknown sativa variety. The strain is notable for its relatively fast flowering time of 55-65 days, which is unusually short for a sativa-dominant strain. Williams Wonder was developed as part of efforts to combine the cerebral, creative effects of sativa genetics with the faster flowering and manageable structure of indica breeding. The result is a strain that delivers energizing, creative sativa effects while remaining practical to cultivate -- a combination that has made it a reliable choice for growers seeking sativa characteristics without the extended flowering times typically associated with sativa-dominant genetics.

## Genetics & Lineage

Williams Wonder combines a well-known indica selection with sativa genetics:

- **Northern Lights #2** -- A specific Northern Lights phenotype from the Pacific Northwest, contributing faster flowering, manageable structure, and resin production
- **Unknown sativa** -- The sativa parent has not been definitively identified, though it contributes the energizing, creative sativa effects and sativa structure

**Parent lineage:** Northern Lights #2 x Unknown Sativa

The strain was developed to capture sativa cerebral effects while maintaining the faster flowering time and manageable growing characteristics inherited from Northern Lights #2. The Northern Lights genetics are well-documented as originating from the Pacific Northwest cannabis scene before being brought to the Netherlands, where they were refined by breeders including Sensi Seeds.

## Effects & Experience

Williams Wonder is widely reported to produce:

- **Energy and alertness** -- A clean, stimulating effect that promotes daytime activity
- **Creativity** -- Enhanced creative thinking and artistic engagement, making it popular among creative professionals
- **Euphoria** -- An uplifting, cheerful mood lift
- **Functional high** -- The effects are pronounced but allow users to remain productive and engaged in tasks

**Onset:** Effects begin within minutes when smoked or vaporized; 30-90 minutes when consumed orally.

**Duration:** 2-4 hours for inhaled consumption; 4-8 hours for oral consumption.

**Intensity:** Moderate. The effects are clear-headed and functional, well-suited to users seeking daytime creative stimulation without overwhelming intensity.

**⚠️ Effects are self-reported and vary by individual, dose, tolerance, and consumption method. Not a prediction of any individual's experience.**

## Aroma & Flavor

- **Smell:** Pine, earthy, and sweet with subtle herbal undertones. The pinene expression gives it a fresh, forest-like quality, while the myrcene adds a smooth sweetness that softens the sharper pine notes.
- **Taste:** Pine and earthy flavors on the inhale, with sweet and slightly herbal notes on the exhale. The flavor is clean and classic, appealing to users who appreciate traditional cannabis taste with a sweet edge.

## Growing Information

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Difficulty** | Moderate -- requires some experience but the fast flowering time is a significant advantage |
| **Flowering Time** | 55-65 days (indoor) -- relatively fast for a sativa-dominant strain |
| **Yield** | 350-450 g/m² indoor; 500+ g/plant outdoor |
| **Plant Height** | Medium -- sativa stretch is moderate; the Northern Lights genetics help control height |
| **Preferred Environment** | Indoor or outdoor; prefers warm, sunny conditions with good air circulation |
| **Resistance** | Moderate to good resistance to mold and pests. The Northern Lights genetics contribute resilience and reliability. Responds well to topping, LST, and SCROG. |

Williams Wonder is a moderately manageable strain to grow. Its sativa-dominant characteristics require some height management, but the Northern Lights #2 genetics keep the plant more compact and faster-flowering than most sativa-dominant strains. The 55-65 day flowering time is one of the strain's key advantages. Outdoor harvest in the Northern Hemisphere typically falls in late September. The plant performs well in both indoor and outdoor environments, and the Northern Lights heritage contributes reliable growing characteristics.
